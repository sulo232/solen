# PROCESS.md , how design work is scoped, briefed and graded

Solen `AGENTS.md` owns design responsibility, production versus exploration scope, mockup approval, preview delivery and measured acceptance. Global instructions own delegation decisions and review depth; `fable-execution` owns consequential contract mechanics. Use those owners rather than copying their full rules into each brief.

## The brief, in full

Use the existing task brief; ordinary implementation needs no additional document. A brief carries current source pointers instead of copied literals or stale environment details:

```
ROLE: [main author | approved-design implementer | warranted verifier]
ROUTE: [actual route]
SCOPE: [owned sections/files and approved change]
PROJECT ROOT: [verify current checkout]
DEV SERVER: [verify actual server; never assume a port]
THE JOB: [one sentence]
DONE MEANS: [checkable outcome]
APPROVED ARTIFACT: [when applicable, including widths/states/motion]
EVIDENCE: [current reference, source and rendered checks]
```

Identify the relevant source owner before judgment or edits:

- `COMPONENT_REGISTRY.md`: existing components and their contracts.
- `LOCKFILE.md`: frozen production values.
- `TASTE_LOG.md`: dated decisions on this surface.
- `TASTE_AUTHORITY.md` and `QUESTIONS.md`: available discretion and unresolved choices.

Read the applicable sections with enough surrounding contract to interpret them. Reuse accepted evidence while file identity and conditions remain current; re-read missing or changed evidence, or a premise challenged by a failure. Follow the additional first-read and reference triggers in project `AGENTS.md`.

## The three steps

**1. Ground.** Run the project exists check before creating. Reuse the existing surface unless the owner commissions a separate exploration. Capture the exact named reference; project sources determine its authority. A settled decision is not reopened without the owner's instruction or directly relevant new evidence.

**2. Build.** Main owns the creative decisions and final fidelity. A justified implementer follows the actual approved design, returning missing choices to main. Preserve the project's simulated-payment milestone unless deeper integration is commissioned. A new shared component needs its Layer 1/2/3 contract and registry entry in the same authorized change; do not introduce category-specific branches.

Use locked production values. For an unlocked choice, apply taste authority and existing authorization; ask only for a material remaining decision and continue independent work. An explicit multi-direction or net-new commission may vary anatomy, layout, type, shape and responsive motion within that commission. The project owns the complete exception and hard requirements; exploration does not authorize production adoption.

**3. Check and grade.** Use the actual approved viewport, route and states; the standard customer reference is 390x844 unless the owner specifies another viewport. Inspect screenshots, measured DOM, interaction, accessibility and real data against project floors and reference evidence. Global consequence-based review applies; when required, one read-only `design-verifier` may cover all applicable criteria. It reports a verdict or an evidence-backed punch list without editing. Production approval and taste remain the owner's decisions.

## What a verifier is told to ignore

Do not re-flag deliberate reference departures authorized by current Solen decisions. Primary CTAs, semantic colors, sparse accent and focus treatment come from the project and `LOCKFILE.md`, not the reference brand. In particular, pointer click/tap causes no visible focus change, while keyboard navigation retains a visible ink indicator.

For commissioned exploration, judge each direction against its commission and captured evidence; do not force one shared layout or treat production aesthetic floors as automatic rejection of authorized variation. Accessibility, data truth, security, money/terms and the production-approval boundary remain mandatory.
