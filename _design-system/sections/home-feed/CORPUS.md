# Home / discovery feed: cross-app corpus

**Archetype:** marketplace home feed (search entry, category shortcuts, titled rows of provider cards)
**Instrument:** Mobbin MCP, 16 searches (15 screen searches: 11 iOS, 4 web; plus 1 website-section search)
**Date of sweep:** 2026-07-29
**Solen surface this governs:** `app/[locale]/page.tsx` + `app/[locale]/_components/homepage/*`

---

## 0. Sample size, stated plainly

- **140 screen results plus 10 website-section results** were returned and examined as images. After removing 3 exact repeats across searches, that is **147 distinct captures**.
- **84 of those are home or discovery-feed screens** in this archetype, drawn from **44 distinct apps and websites**.
- The remaining ~63 are search results, map views, detail pages, category grids and appointment lists that the queries pulled in alongside. I read those too and they inform the "what happens after the tap" notes, but **they are excluded from every frequency below**.
- **Every frequency in this document is counted over one named denominator**: a tally set of **34 home/discovery screens (23 iOS, 11 web) from 28 distinct brands**, chosen because I could read their first viewport top to bottom. Where a number uses a different denominator, that denominator is named inline.

**Tally set, iOS (23):** Airbnb, Fresha, Uber, Uber Eats, Zocdoc, Angi, TheFork, Resy, ClassPass, Square Go, Skip, Rappi, Grab, Swiggy, Blinkit, Instacart, Mercari, Whatnot, Faire Wholesale, Airtasker, Trip.com, Shop, Nike.
**Tally set, web (11):** Airbnb, Fresha, DoorDash, Uber Eats, Etsy, Faire, Instacart, Airtasker, Eventbrite, Klook, Givingli.

**Three honesty notes up front.**

1. **Booksy is not in Mobbin's corpus.** I ran three separate searches naming it and it never returned one screen (the closest query [returned Fresha instead](https://mobbin.com/screens/781cf27c-6d77-465b-8302-f106505dc691)). Treatwell, StyleSeat, Vagaro, Squire and Mindbody also returned nothing. **The only pure beauty-booking apps I could actually observe are Fresha and [Square Go](https://mobbin.com/screens/4f9be7f8-db2b-42f4-afdd-900d15e25c8c).** Where this document says "appointment-booking apps" the members are Fresha, Square Go, Zocdoc, ClassPass and Care.com. That is a sample of five, not a category.
2. **Mobbin returns still images.** I cannot observe timing, easing, gesture or transition from a screenshot. Section 5 states exactly what the static corpus does and does not license, and refuses the rest.
3. **Approximations are labelled.** Cards-per-viewport in section 3 are read off screenshot proportions, not measured in pixels. I say so there rather than dress them up as measurements.

## 3. Grid

**Mobile.** Every horizontal carousel in the corpus shows **roughly 1.7 to 2.1 cards per viewport with the next one visibly cropped**. Read off screenshot proportions, so these are approximations and not pixel measurements: [Airbnb](https://mobbin.com/screens/41e035de-eb59-433c-a13a-b2367812536f) ~1.9, [Fresha](https://mobbin.com/screens/33f7d0bd-2edc-4768-a4a4-7de2b9b90489) ~1.8, [TheFork](https://mobbin.com/screens/f889959f-35a3-4d6c-a718-bbe83176374a) ~1.8, [Uber Eats](https://mobbin.com/screens/9ab7c936-80cd-4e55-b063-0ada4f26e868) ~1.7, [ClassPass](https://mobbin.com/screens/5c60648c-ebfc-46f1-96e1-0765f8719f67) ~2.1. The crop is universal: **I found no mobile carousel that ended flush with the viewport edge.** That is the scroll promise, and it corroborates Solen's density floor rather than challenging it.

**Desktop, at the widths Mobbin rendered.** Two settings only:
- **4 cards per row**, arrows for the rest: [DoorDash](https://mobbin.com/screens/e0ddd0d5-254b-499d-a15e-649bb1d52bde), [Uber Eats web](https://mobbin.com/screens/8d7b3c5e-0630-406f-9f61-27a9ac5de7a5), [Fresha web](https://mobbin.com/screens/962315e5-a36e-4a70-ba2d-f2e1d7871be2).
- **7 cards per row plus a cropped 8th**: [Airbnb web](https://mobbin.com/screens/a92f14ea-1a94-4a63-99f3-8b5fbd42d106), the outlier, and a deliberate one. Airbnb's card body is two short lines, so 7 fit without the row reading as a wall.

Nothing rendered 5 or 6. The choice tracks card-body height, not taste. Solen's card carries 3 body rows, which puts it in the DoorDash / Fresha band, so **4 across on desktop is the grounded number, not 7.**

**Carousel navigation on web.** 9 of 9 web feeds with a horizontal row provide arrow buttons, in two placements: on the header line, right-aligned ([Airbnb web](https://mobbin.com/screens/a92f14ea-1a94-4a63-99f3-8b5fbd42d106), [DoorDash](https://mobbin.com/screens/e0ddd0d5-254b-499d-a15e-649bb1d52bde), [Uber Eats web](https://mobbin.com/screens/8d7b3c5e-0630-406f-9f61-27a9ac5de7a5), [Airtasker](https://mobbin.com/screens/756a4205-72b6-4e12-ac24-c7f31f921cfe), [Givingli](https://mobbin.com/screens/ef9cfa07-1939-4531-a5fc-d3881252d7b8)), or as a single circle overlaid at the row's right edge, vertically centred on the photo band ([Etsy](https://mobbin.com/screens/be1301b4-ad5c-4d6f-ad32-da0e91a9cd5a), [Klook](https://mobbin.com/screens/49ac4d43-90b7-4dc0-a9fc-5ff5bb6141bc), [Fresha web](https://mobbin.com/screens/962315e5-a36e-4a70-ba2d-f2e1d7871be2), [Faire web](https://mobbin.com/screens/c15617c3-a6be-4a79-8581-bd4e2f08604d)). **Arrows and the cropped edge coexist**; the arrow is not a substitute for the scroll promise.

**Vertical rhythm.** In every web capture the section-to-section gap is a clear step larger than the card-to-card gap, visibly at or above 2x in [DoorDash](https://mobbin.com/screens/3514a909-264b-44c5-b8b7-677a5a0423f1) and [Airbnb web](https://mobbin.com/screens/ef8fe96f-1fb9-4b17-97d3-625b20344e39). I did not measure these in pixels and am not claiming a ratio.

**Surface.** All 11 web feeds sit on **white or near-white**, and **not one alternates grey and white bands down the page**. Depth comes from the card, not the tray. [Fresha web](https://mobbin.com/screens/962315e5-a36e-4a70-ba2d-f2e1d7871be2) gives each card a hairline border with a rounded white body block; [Airbnb web](https://mobbin.com/screens/a92f14ea-1a94-4a63-99f3-8b5fbd42d106) gives it no border, no shadow and no body chrome at all, letting the flush photo edge be the only boundary. Both satisfy Solen's Edge-Visibility floor, by routes (b) and (a) respectively. Solen's `FeedZone` alternation is therefore a Solen-specific rhythm choice with no corpus support either way, not a corpus-mandated pattern.

## 4. Type

**Card text.** The corpus is aggressively compressed. [Airbnb web](https://mobbin.com/screens/a92f14ea-1a94-4a63-99f3-8b5fbd42d106) uses exactly **two** treatments per card: a dark title line and one grey line holding price and rating together. [Fresha web](https://mobbin.com/screens/962315e5-a36e-4a70-ba2d-f2e1d7871be2) uses three plus a category chip. [DoorDash](https://mobbin.com/screens/e0ddd0d5-254b-499d-a15e-649bb1d52bde) uses four: name, grey meta, grey fee, red promo. **No card in the corpus carried more than four text lines.** Solen's `SalonCard` renders name + rating, meta, address + price, which is three and inside the ceiling.

**Anchor.** Two camps, and both are legitimate:
- **Display anchor present, and large.** [Fresha web](https://mobbin.com/screens/7832695b-2761-4f79-aa14-5cefe5a5e7ff): a two-line serif headline, by far the largest element, roughly 3.5x the search-field text. Also [ClassPass "For You"](https://mobbin.com/screens/5c60648c-ebfc-46f1-96e1-0765f8719f67), [Resy "Eight Things to Do This Month"](https://mobbin.com/screens/21468b4a-b398-4a54-b8de-8cb287aab12e), [Airtasker "Book a Tasker directly"](https://mobbin.com/screens/756a4205-72b6-4e12-ac24-c7f31f921cfe). In every case everything else drops hard to body; there is no middle band.
- **No display anchor at all.** [Airbnb iOS](https://mobbin.com/screens/41e035de-eb59-433c-a13a-b2367812536f), [Airbnb web](https://mobbin.com/screens/a92f14ea-1a94-4a63-99f3-8b5fbd42d106) and [Fresha iOS](https://mobbin.com/screens/33f7d0bd-2edc-4768-a4a4-7de2b9b90489): the largest text is the section title and **the photograph is the focal**. That is the escape clause in Solen's FLOORS LAW 6 being exercised by two of the three most relevant references in the whole corpus.

**Emphasis discipline.** On [Airbnb web](https://mobbin.com/screens/a92f14ea-1a94-4a63-99f3-8b5fbd42d106) and [Airbnb iOS](https://mobbin.com/screens/41e035de-eb59-433c-a13a-b2367812536f) the card title is **not bold**: it is a medium weight, and the line under it is lighter and grey. The only heavy weight on the screen is the section title. This is consistent with the 3.1% weight-600 figure already recorded in the EMPHASIS BUDGET block. Solen matches: `CardName` bakes `font-medium` and `CardMeta` bakes `font-normal`.

**Section titles.** Across the web captures the section title is a modest bold step above body, never a display step: [DoorDash](https://mobbin.com/screens/e0ddd0d5-254b-499d-a15e-649bb1d52bde), [Uber Eats web](https://mobbin.com/screens/8d7b3c5e-0630-406f-9f61-27a9ac5de7a5), [Etsy](https://mobbin.com/screens/be1301b4-ad5c-4d6f-ad32-da0e91a9cd5a), [Fresha web](https://mobbin.com/screens/962315e5-a36e-4a70-ba2d-f2e1d7871be2). Solen's `clamp(18px,2vw,20px) font-semibold` sits in the same band.

**Serif.** [Fresha](https://mobbin.com/screens/7832695b-2761-4f79-aa14-5cefe5a5e7ff) is the only brand in the corpus using a serif display face, on both its web headline and its iOS "Hey, John". Everyone else is grotesque throughout. Solen is on Inter Tight, so this is one axis where the structural source of truth does something Solen deliberately does not copy. Naming it so nobody "fixes" the sans later by citing Fresha.

## 5. Motion

**What I can and cannot claim.** Mobbin returns still images. **I observed zero transitions, durations, easing curves or gestures.** Everything below is either a static artefact that implies motion, or is explicitly marked unverified.

**Observed statically, therefore real:**
- **A skeleton that mirrors the final layout.** [Uber's loading state](https://mobbin.com/screens/6ab4af73-4d1f-4a2b-b616-9a0a9e4766e6) keeps the real "Where to?" pill fully rendered and replaces only the content below it with a label bar plus a large block, repeated three times at the exact positions the real sections occupy. The chrome does not flash. This corroborates Solen's locked `<Skeleton>` rule (shape matches the final layout, never a bare spinner).
- **In-card photo carousels exist.** [Airbnb results cards](https://mobbin.com/screens/abf8b4d4-23c7-433e-8e0a-723005ac2abb) and [Resy](https://mobbin.com/screens/ad5c9623-c245-4593-acf8-b959d98c9c59) render dot pagination inside the card photo. The dots are visible in the capture; the swipe is not.
- **Disabled-aware carousel arrows.** [Fresha web](https://mobbin.com/screens/962315e5-a36e-4a70-ba2d-f2e1d7871be2) shows a right arrow and no left arrow at scroll position 0; [Airbnb web](https://mobbin.com/screens/a92f14ea-1a94-4a63-99f3-8b5fbd42d106) shows the left arrow greyed and the right one active. Solen's `SectionTitle` already tracks `canScrollLeft` / `canScrollRight` from a passive scroll listener and disables accordingly, which matches.
- **Transient in-feed notices.** Airbnb's "Prices include all fees" pill is present in [one capture](https://mobbin.com/screens/41e035de-eb59-433c-a13a-b2367812536f) of a screen, absent from [another capture of the same screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/179b0617-5e5e-479b-9cff-de092b6f804b), and appears elsewhere as [a full modal with a "Got it" button](https://mobbin.com/screens/b0dccec4-a867-4bab-b181-117f65b4acb6). That it comes and goes is observed. How it enters is not.

**Not verified, and I will not guess:** scroll snapping, card press feedback, hover elevation, row-enter cascade, heart-tap animation, category-tile press tier, and the stem-draw hover on Solen's own section chevron (which exists in `SectionHeader.tsx` but has no corpus counterpart because hover is unobservable in a screenshot). **If any of these are needed, they come from `_design-system/MOTION.md` and the Motion-22 vocabulary, or from a `reference-lock` capture with real video. Mobbin cannot answer them.**

## 7. The one collision, both sides stated

**Side A, Solen's own imagery floor.** FLOORS LAW 2 and the LOCKED design contract require every customer browse/discovery viewport at 390x844 to carry roughly >= 1/3 photographic area. Measured against the code: `Hero.tsx` contains no `Image` and no `src`, and it carries `md:min-h-[92dvh]`. On desktop, Solen's home therefore opens with **92% of the viewport holding zero photographic pixels**, and the first `SalonCard` photo sits below the fold.

**Side B, the structural source of truth does the same thing.** [Fresha web](https://mobbin.com/screens/0b68f8e9-3c79-4302-83c6-990eefc611d7) is a full-viewport gradient hero with a serif headline, a search bar, a counter and no photography at all. Fresha is Solen's declared STRUCTURE axis. But it is a genuine minority of **1 of the 11 web home feeds examined**: [Airbnb](https://mobbin.com/screens/a92f14ea-1a94-4a63-99f3-8b5fbd42d106), [DoorDash](https://mobbin.com/screens/e0ddd0d5-254b-499d-a15e-649bb1d52bde), [Uber Eats](https://mobbin.com/screens/8d7b3c5e-0630-406f-9f61-27a9ac5de7a5), [Etsy](https://mobbin.com/screens/be1301b4-ad5c-4d6f-ad32-da0e91a9cd5a), [Faire](https://mobbin.com/screens/c15617c3-a6be-4a79-8581-bd4e2f08604d), [Klook](https://mobbin.com/screens/49ac4d43-90b7-4dc0-a9fc-5ff5bb6141bc), [Eventbrite](https://mobbin.com/screens/048836a8-1d86-4419-bda6-a62c8c2797d0), [Airtasker](https://mobbin.com/screens/756a4205-72b6-4e12-ac24-c7f31f921cfe), [Givingli](https://mobbin.com/screens/ef9cfa07-1939-4531-a5fc-d3881252d7b8) and [Instacart](https://mobbin.com/screens/40137564-73c7-411f-a3b9-fdde2ce45cbe) all put real content in the first viewport.

**The resolution the corpus supports, and it is emphatically NOT "add a hero image."** FLOORS LAW 2 was clarified 2026-07-25 to say the imagery floor is satisfied by **content**, never by decoration. The corpus agrees without a single exception: **not one of the 34 screens meets its imagery need with a hero image.** They meet it by pulling the first row of real provider cards up into the first viewport. So the only move both sides license is to **shorten the hero until the first `SalonCard` row crops into view**. That is a live owner decision and a taste call on the exact height, so it needs a mockup, not a silent edit.

**One further honest note.** [Fresha's hero](https://mobbin.com/screens/7832695b-2761-4f79-aa14-5cefe5a5e7ff) does pass the "at least one tabular real number" floor, via the live counter. Solen's hero carries no equivalent. If the hero stays tall, that counter is the highest-value single addition to it, and it must be wired to real bookings or not shipped.
