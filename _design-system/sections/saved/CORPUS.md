# Saved / Favorites: screen-archetype corpus

**Screen:** the customer's saved / favourites surface (the list of salons they hearted), plus its empty state.
**Solen surfaces this maps to:** `app/[locale]/profile/favorites/page.tsx` (saved SALONS) and `app/[locale]/inspo/saved/page.tsx` (saved LOOKS, a different entity).
**Method:** Mobbin sweep, 14 searches, images examined directly. Every claim below is either something I SAW in a screenshot (marked plainly) or something I INFERRED (marked as inferred).
**Date:** 2026-07-29

---

## 0. Sample

**159 unique screens returned across 14 Mobbin searches (11 on iOS, 3 on web). Of those, 131 are an actual saved / favourites surface, spanning 80 apps.** The remaining 28 were adjacent (a PDP save confirmation, a discovery collection, an order-history screen) and are used below only as counter-evidence, never counted in the frequencies.

Search axes used, so the corpus is not one app repeated: generic saved-grid, Airbnb wishlists, beauty-booking favourites (Fresha / Booksy / Treatwell named), empty states, desktop wishlist pages, provider-marketplace saves (Vagaro / StyleSeat / Mindbody named), segmented saved tabs, wishlist detail with map, restaurant saves (OpenTable / Resy / Yelp named), Airbnb on web, salon booking on web, per-row rebook actions, and saved headers with counts and sort.

**What the corpus does NOT contain:** Booksy, Treatwell, Vagaro, StyleSeat, Mindbody, SimplyBook.me, Calendly, OpenTable, Uber, Revolut, Linear and Stripe returned zero saved screens across the searches that named them. Mobbin either does not index a saved screen for them or they do not have one. Fresha is the only beauty-booking app in the corpus, on both iOS and web. That is a real limitation: the beauty-vertical evidence rests on one app, and every "the vertical does X" claim below is therefore a claim about Fresha, not about the vertical.

Denominators used in this file, all named at the point of use:
- **80 apps** = every app with at least one saved surface in the corpus.
- **26 venue apps** = the apps whose saved item is a place, venue or provider (the salon-shaped subset). Fresha, Airbnb, Careem, Grab, Tabby, Too Good To Go, Uber Eats, Zocdoc, World of Hyatt, IHG, Marriott Bonvoy, Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com, Resy, Google Maps, Apple Maps, NAVER, Placify, AllTrails, komoot, Klook, Dribbble, Commons, Afterpay, WEAR.
- **14 empty states** = the full-screen zero states I could see end to end.
- **~53 populated iOS saved screens** = the iOS screens showing at least one saved item.

## 1. Dominant anatomy

Top to bottom, the shape that recurs. Frequencies are over the 26 venue apps unless stated.

```
[ back or nothing ]                       ← 26/26 have a way out; 13 use a plain back chevron
Title "Favourites" / "Saved" / "Wishlists"
[ optional count line: "4 saved" / "5 venues" ]   ← 23 of 80 apps print a count
[ optional sort / filter row ]                    ← 18 of 80 apps offer one
────────────────────────────────────────
ITEM
  photo                                   ← 23/26 venue apps show one
  name (the largest text in the item)
  rating ★ + (count)
  neighbourhood or address, grey
  [ optional category chip ]
  [ heart or X, top-right of the photo ]  ← see §3, this is NOT universal
ITEM
ITEM
```

The closest single screen to this skeleton, and the one Solen's structure axis is already locked to, is [Fresha, Favourites, iOS](https://mobbin.com/screens/9e1ed828-f7f4-4472-a1ec-cff44b33ce8c): large left-aligned "Favourites" in the content (not a nav bar), then one-column full-bleed photo cards, filled red heart in a white circular chip at the photo's top-right, then name at the largest weight, then `4.9 ★ (822)`, then `Mission Bay, San Francisco` in grey, then a neutral outline `Massage` chip. [Fresha on web](https://mobbin.com/screens/077ca5a1-d91a-4ed8-b96b-1925e8b3992f) renders the identical card anatomy in a multi-column grid at roughly 170px card width.

What I did NOT find: a saved screen that groups saves by anything (by city, by category, by date saved). Zero of the 131. Chronological, most-recent-first, is the only ordering I could observe, and it is almost never labelled as such.

## 7. The Solen gap

Checked against the live code on 2026-07-29, not from memory.

**What Solen already does that the corpus supports.**
1. One column on mobile, two on `sm:` (`FavoritesList.tsx:101`). Matches the dominant grid.
2. Photo-forward salon card, photo from the salon record, never baked in.
3. Grey count line under the title (`FavoritesList.tsx:98-100`), the exact corpus treatment.
4. Optimistic removal with a neutral Undo toast and position-preserving reinsert (`FavoritesList.tsx:31-78`). Better than anything I could observe.
5. A rich empty state with a REAL top-rated rail and a real cover photo (`favorites/page.tsx:66-96`). Only Tabby, 1 of 14, does this in the corpus.
6. No collections layer, no sort, no filter, no search bar, no per-card book button. The corpus supports every one of those omissions.

**Three real gaps, in severity order.**

**(a) Two `SalonCard` implementations exist on disk, and the saved screen uses the one the registry does not name.**
```
./components-legacy/SalonCard.tsx          ← imported by FavoritesList.tsx:12
./app/[locale]/_components/homepage/SalonCard.tsx   ← what COMPONENT_REGISTRY.md line 128 names
```
The registry entry says `homepage/SalonCard.tsx` and says it is "Used across homepage feeds, /favoriten". The favourites screen actually imports `@/components-legacy/SalonCard`, along with `brand/[slug]`, `behandlungen/[...slug]`, `TreatmentsClient` and `dashboard/settings`. This is a FLOORS LAW 8 case by its own definition: one entity, more than one implementation, and the two can drift apart with nothing catching it. It is not a saved-screen bug specifically, it is a system bug that the saved screen sits on top of. Naming it here, not fixing it here.

**(b) The in-session zero state is a status label used as an empty-state headline.**
`FavoritesList.tsx:82-94`: when the user removes the LAST favourite without reloading, the component renders `t("removedFromFavorites")` as the headline and a blue link labelled `t("view")` pointing at `/inspo`. Read from `messages/*.json`, that resolves to:

| locale | headline that renders | link label that renders |
|---|---|---|
| de | "Aus Favoriten entfernt." | "Ansehen" |
| en | "Removed from favorites." | "View" |
| fr | "Retiré des favoris." | "Voir" |
| it | "Rimosso dai preferiti." | "Vedi" |

Two problems, both citable. First, the design contract's `states` row says an empty state carries a "PROMISE headline (never a bare status label)"; "Aus Favoriten entfernt" is exactly a bare status label, and it is borrowed from the `toasts` namespace. Second, "Ansehen" as a CTA pointing at Inspo says nothing about where it goes. In the corpus, 14 of 14 empty states carry a purpose-written headline and 12 of 14 a purpose-written CTA label ("Explore items", "Browse Stores", "Start searching", "Explore trails"). This state is reachable by a normal user in a normal session, and it is materially worse than the server-rendered `EmptyStateDiscovery` sitting one file away. **Recommended fix: render `EmptyStateDiscovery` here too, or at minimum give this state its own headline and CTA keys. This needs the real top-rated salons passed down from the server component, which the client wrapper does not currently receive, so it is a small prop change, not a copy change.**

**(c) The page has no in-content display anchor, and this collides with a dated owner decision.**
`favorites/page.tsx:77` records: "Title lives in the global header beside the back tile (owner, 2026-06-11)." So the first thing in the content is the 13px grey count line. FLOORS LAW 6 wants one display anchor at 28px or more per customer screen "unless the photograph is the focal", and here the SalonCard photo arguably is the focal, so this may be legal. But the corpus is one-sided against it: 22 of 22 web saved pages and [Fresha itself, on both platforms](https://mobbin.com/screens/9e1ed828-f7f4-4472-a1ec-cff44b33ce8c), put a large left-aligned title in the content. Fresha is Solen's locked STRUCTURE source of truth, and it disagrees with the 2026-06-11 call on this specific screen.

Per the precedence chain, I am surfacing the collision rather than resolving it: **owner decision 2026-06-11 (title in the global header) versus the structure reference (title in the content, large, left-aligned), on a screen where the content currently opens on 13px grey text.** This is a taste call the owner owns. It is not a defect and I am not proposing to override it.

**One thing the corpus says Solen does differently and should KEEP doing differently:** the two saved surfaces (`/profile/favorites` for salons, `/inspo/saved` for looks) render as two different shapes, a 1-column card list and a flat image grid. Reading FLOORS LAW 8 loosely, that looks like an inconsistency. It is not. They hold different entities, and the corpus is unambiguous that column count follows text weight: image-only saves go 3-column flush (5 of 5 cases), text-bearing saves go 1-column (~33 of ~53). Two entities, two components, one rule. That is a deliberate choice, not drift.
