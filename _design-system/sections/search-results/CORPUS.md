# Search results + filters, cross-app corpus

**Screen archetype:** a search-results list of bookable providers, with a filter control and a sort control at the top, each result carrying a photo, name, rating and price.
**Method:** 14 Mobbin searches (deep mode, iOS and web), varied phrasing, apps named explicitly in 8 of them, `exclude_screen_ids` used on 4 to push past already-seen results. Every screenshot cited below was opened and read as an image; nothing here is described from brand memory or from result metadata. Obeys `_design-system/RESEARCH_METHOD.md` R3 (a named gap is a first-class result) and R5 (no number without a stated denominator).
**Solen components this governs:** `app/[locale]/_components/search/SearchTemplate.tsx` (2412 lines), `SalonResultCard.tsx` (702 lines, 5 variants), `FilterSheet.tsx` (666 lines), `SearchOverlay.tsx`, `MapSalonDetail.tsx`
**Layer:** 1 (chrome: chip row, count row, sort menu, map button) + Layer 2 (`TabPill`, `RatingStars`, `PriceFrom`, `CardName`, `CardMeta`, `HeartButton`)

---

## 1. Sample size, stated honestly

149 screen images were returned across the 14 searches. After removing repeats that surfaced in more than one search, roughly **137 distinct screens**. Not all of them are this archetype: the searches also pulled booking-flow date pickers, fitness content libraries and developer marketplaces, which I read but did not count.

Every frequency in this document is counted over one of three explicitly-defined sets:

| Set | What it is | Size |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Screens showing a list or grid of bookable providers, venues or listings **together with** at least one visible filter or sort control | **63 screens across 30 brands** (22 iOS apps + 12 web sites; Airbnb, Fresha, Peerspace and Preply appear on both platforms) |
| **B** | Filter panels and sort panels opened as sheets, modals or menus | **19 panels across 18 apps** |
| **C** | No-results / zero-state screens on a search surface | **10 screens across 10 apps** |

**Set A brands, iOS (22):** Airbnb, Airtasker, Bolt Food, CVS Health, DICE, DoorDash, Expedia, Fresha, Google Maps, Grab, IHG Hotels & Rewards, Navan, Peerspace, Preply, Resy, ShopBack, Snapchat, StubHub, TheFork, Uber Eats, Vrbo, Zocdoc.
**Set A brands, web (12):** Airbnb, Care.com, Etsy, Faire, Fiverr, Fresha, Kiwi.com, Peerspace, Preply, Tripadvisor, Turo, Zillow.

**Not found in the corpus, despite being searched for by name:** Booksy, Treatwell, Vagaro, StyleSeat, Squire, Mindbody, SimplyBook.me, OpenTable, Calendly. Mobbin returned adjacent apps instead (Fresha, Resy, TheFork, Zocdoc, CVS Health). This matches the same gap already recorded in `RESEARCH_METHOD.md` R3 from the 2026-07-28 session. I could not verify any claim about those nine, so this document makes none.

The single most useful screen in the whole corpus is [Fresha iOS, "21 venues nearby"](https://mobbin.com/screens/f397bc85-dda6-42d5-8704-f7383b037285). It is the only card in Set A that carries all five layers at once: photo with pagination dots, name, a 5-glyph star row plus the value plus `(751)`, address, then a hairline, then per-service rows (`Women haircut / 45 min - 1 hr` left, `from $100` right), then three tappable time chips and a `⋮` overflow. Its web twin, [Fresha web with the map split](https://mobbin.com/screens/17fb8942-1a0e-4f07-b540-fd910816244c), renders the identical card anatomy at desktop width, and [a second web card](https://mobbin.com/screens/612f2a56-4a8c-4d16-bc51-08cca7209937) stacks three service rows with slot chips under each plus a "See more" link.

**Observed variance in band 4.** Photo-left thumbnails (10 of 30 brands: [Vrbo](https://mobbin.com/screens/de51d4a0-cfdb-4729-a57d-57540d44bed5), [Expedia](https://mobbin.com/screens/9aa106ab-3e44-43d3-b88e-8ad195f39025), [Preply](https://mobbin.com/screens/33980e45-a5cb-4f98-b344-f42793c4b520), [Zocdoc](https://mobbin.com/screens/53728922-3e77-4e0e-aef2-3c267714d5ec), [Turo](https://mobbin.com/screens/7faa693b-25f5-40c2-ab00-8d2e94a53e7d), [Care.com](https://mobbin.com/screens/afcc8806-e6e4-4b52-9044-82cb224472c5), [Kiwi.com](https://mobbin.com/screens/861fe098-3ac6-468d-95e5-9565d273bc75), Navan, ShopBack, Snapchat) cluster almost perfectly in price-comparison and directory products. Photo-top clusters in identity-led marketplaces. One brand, [Airtasker](https://mobbin.com/screens/8326793c-8872-4e44-a2e3-8f26974f441f), puts a small thumbnail on the RIGHT. Two brands ([CVS Health](https://mobbin.com/screens/1a06a826-1478-4f84-a50b-4a0313977cca), [Google Maps](https://mobbin.com/screens/cdf52403-8878-40f2-b7cc-126b574c8379)) render no photo at all.

## 4. Grid

**Mobile (iOS, Set A).** One column, cards running gutter to gutter, separated by vertical gaps rather than dividers when the card is photo-led. Card photos in the photo-top group read as landscape, roughly between 3:2 and 5:4. The photo-left group uses a square or near-square thumbnail occupying somewhere between a quarter and a third of the row width. Solen's `feed` variant is `aspect-[5/4]` full width in a `gap-6` column, which sits inside the observed band.

**Desktop (web, Set A).** Two shapes, and the choice is decided by whether there is a map.

- **With a map (7 of 7 put the map right).** Fresha runs the narrowest list of the seven: a single fixed rail against a map filling everything else. [Turo](https://mobbin.com/screens/7faa693b-25f5-40c2-ab00-8d2e94a53e7d) and [Kiwi.com](https://mobbin.com/screens/861fe098-3ac6-468d-95e5-9565d273bc75) give the list more than half the width. [Zillow](https://mobbin.com/screens/d45ea413-9934-4270-b2ea-336e39abfa78) and [Tripadvisor](https://mobbin.com/screens/6e948f58-5edf-40ba-b841-78b8016544a8) keep a two-column card grid on the list side and shrink the map. [Care.com](https://mobbin.com/screens/afcc8806-e6e4-4b52-9044-82cb224472c5) is the only three-column layout in the corpus: filter rail, single-column list, map.
- **Without a map.** Either a left filter rail plus a 4-to-5-column card grid ([Etsy](https://mobbin.com/screens/060b84d1-a334-4e7e-94e6-85801de20219), [Faire](https://mobbin.com/screens/fd230435-b822-4dc0-938a-0c7103e6b119), [Unity](https://mobbin.com/screens/5b890789-7046-4cd6-8c88-7023df1cce94), [Skillshare](https://mobbin.com/screens/36f20c84-9c78-4e87-ab8c-2130ce425904)), or no rail at all with filters as a dropdown row over a 4-to-5-column grid ([Fiverr](https://mobbin.com/screens/6f522afc-6035-4884-a098-f34668429e18), [Airbnb web services](https://mobbin.com/screens/2258df34-4be9-42f5-a410-f2e426d66ee2)).

Solen's desktop grid (`SearchTemplate.tsx:1665`) is `grid-cols-1 / md:grid-cols-2 / lg:grid-cols-3`, dropping to `lg:grid-cols-2` when the map opens. Three columns with no rail is on the sparse side of the corpus (Fiverr and Etsy run four, Airbnb web runs five), but Solen's cards are taller because they carry more per-card information, so this is not a like-for-like comparison. Flagging, not prescribing.

**Not measured.** Gutters, card padding and photo aspect ratios above were read by eye from Mobbin screenshots. None are pixel-derived. Any spec that needs an exact number runs `pixel-spec-auto` against a captured reference first, per the reference-measure gate.

## 5. Type

**The card carries three or four steps, never more.** Across the photo-top group the stack is: name (largest, semibold or bold), a metadata line (smaller, grey, dot-separated), a rating cluster (small, often tabular), and a price (small but heavier than the meta). I did not measure sizes on rendered pages, so this reports the ORDER of the hierarchy, not pixel values.

**Which element is biggest splits cleanly, and the split is meaningful.**

- **Name largest** (identity-led products): [Airbnb](https://mobbin.com/screens/6e634221-3826-44fe-a334-fec1fcbeb2ae), [Fresha](https://mobbin.com/screens/f397bc85-dda6-42d5-8704-f7383b037285), [Uber Eats](https://mobbin.com/screens/19ea0ffb-b592-4d1b-ac41-320153090964), [Resy](https://mobbin.com/screens/e4f524c1-e8e3-4e25-bb4c-b736d43c28a1), [TheFork](https://mobbin.com/screens/b448f1f5-8a43-4b81-8d00-60a95cf8c0a9), [Peerspace](https://mobbin.com/screens/c326f129-d7be-4af5-a671-dea756e7a6fc), [Tripadvisor](https://mobbin.com/screens/6e948f58-5edf-40ba-b841-78b8016544a8), [Etsy](https://mobbin.com/screens/060b84d1-a334-4e7e-94e6-85801de20219).

## 6. Motion

**I cannot report motion from this corpus, and I will not guess it.** Mobbin returned static screenshots. Any claim here about easing, duration or gesture would be invention. What the screenshots DO prove is which transient states these products bothered to design, which is the useful half:

- **A skeleton matching the final layout, not a spinner.** [Peerspace shipped a loading state](https://mobbin.com/screens/ecdbc522-f28d-4c0f-8759-deb9615df82d) that is the exact card layout with grey photo blocks carrying a building glyph, and the heart, the carousel arrows and the black price pill all still in position. Solen's locked `<Skeleton>` rule (shape matches the final layout) is the same decision, independently arrived at.
- **Per-row inline loading during a slot fetch.** [Resy renders a spinner directly over the slot chips](https://mobbin.com/screens/89521ccc-8b01-49a4-a8e3-eaae5c8b97b4) while the rest of the card stays live. This is the state Solen will need the moment pattern 3 is implemented: slots are the slow field on the row, and blocking the whole card on them would be wrong.
- **An anchored menu over the results, not a full-screen takeover.** [Fiverr's open sort dropdown](https://mobbin.com/screens/6f522afc-6035-4884-a098-f34668429e18) and [Unity's open price filter](https://mobbin.com/screens/5b890789-7046-4cd6-8c88-7023df1cce94) both leave the grid visible behind the panel.
- **Re-query as an explicit user action on a moved map, never automatic.** Three apps ship a "Search this area" pill: [Expedia](https://mobbin.com/screens/92e8ebc3-6697-4da5-9e63-f8c3877f467f), [Resy](https://mobbin.com/screens/eab03be9-df2b-445f-9068-5df01f50d72b), [McDonald's](https://mobbin.com/screens/c6336d5f-cb51-4861-8e94-f4a863eb6fce). None of the three re-queries on pan alone.

To get real motion data for this screen, the next step is `Skill(reference-lock)` with `record-interaction.mjs` against live Fresha and live Airbnb, not another Mobbin sweep.

## 8. Where Solen actually diverges

Verified against the source in this worktree, not from memory.

1. **Slot data is fetched and thrown away.** `SearchTemplate.tsx:887` sets `with_slots=1`. `SalonResultCard.tsx:74` declares `slots?: string[] | null`. `SalonResultCard.tsx:183` defines `formatSlotTime(iso, locale)`. Grepping that file for `formatSlotTime` and `.slots` returns the two definitions and zero call sites. The searched-state feed variant renders service name plus price rows (`SalonResultCard.tsx:551-572`) with no slots under them. This is the one gap that changes what the screen is FOR: 4 of the 5 time-booking apps in Set A make the result row bookable, and Fresha does it on both platforms. Solen's row is currently a directory entry with a time written on it.

2. **No way to drop one filter.** `SearchTemplate.tsx:1350`: the leading circle is sliders when nothing is active and ✕ that pushes the bare pathname when anything is. Removing one of four filters costs a sheet round trip. The corpus fix that does not break FLOORS LAW 3 is an inline ✕ on the active chip, not a second wrapped row; see [Bolt Food](https://mobbin.com/screens/59e6ed6e-858d-4e3f-ad34-8063fe202325) for the failure mode.

3. **Recovery content is switched off in exactly the state that needs it.** `CategoryBrowseRails` is gated to `activeFilterCount === 0 && q.length === 0` (`SearchTemplate.tsx:1447`). [Thrive Market](https://mobbin.com/screens/3e1f6d8b-6214-4eaf-9610-74802b1d606c) and [Satispay](https://mobbin.com/screens/052a233c-8aa8-4385-bae5-b901b0394a9c) put recovery content under the ZERO-result state, which is the opposite gate.

4. **Count-row weight sits between the two conventions.** See pattern 2.

5. **Deliberate divergences, not defects.** Recorded here so a design-verifier does not re-flag them every round: the selected chip is grey rather than the corpus-majority dark fill (locked, and it shares its no-heavy-fill logic with Fresha); the commit CTA is ink rather than a brand colour (locked); the filter escape hatch is pinned LEFT of the chips (no corpus precedent either way); mobile sort is an anchored menu rather than a bottom sheet (matches the web convention, unique on mobile); and Solen carries a save heart where Fresha does not, which is the dual-axis rule resolving toward Airbnb on a behaviour question.
