# PDP / listing detail: competitive corpus

**Screen archetype:** provider / venue / listing detail page (Solen: `/{locale}/salon/{slug}`)
**Method:** 16 Mobbin `search_screens` sweeps (11 iOS, 5 web), varied phrasing, several naming specific apps, `exclude_screen_ids` used to push past repeats. Every screen cited below was looked at as an image. Nothing here is described from brand memory.
**Date:** 2026-07-29
**Relates to:** the 20 per-section specs in this folder (`README.md` is the index)

---

## Sample size

- **151 unique screens** returned across the 16 searches.
- **110 of those were on-archetype** (a provider/venue/listing detail page, or one of its sections, modals, sheets, or galleries), spanning **38 apps**: Fresha, Airbnb, Square Go, ClassPass, Resy, Uber Eats, Google Maps, Tripadvisor, DoorDash, Time2book, Zocdoc, Preply, Peerspace, Zomato, Swiggy, CRED, Corner, Open, Careem, Viator, TravelPerk, KAYAK, Square, Etsy, Zillow, Headspace, Behance, Udemy, Alan, Marriott Bonvoy, Shangri-La Circle, IHG, Vrbo, Swarm, Grab, Realtor.com, Whop, Nextdoor.
- **31 of the 110 showed the page's FIRST viewport.** That subset of 31 is the denominator for every anatomy count in the next section, and the denominator is named each time.
- The remaining 41 screens were off-archetype (search results, avatar pickers, checkout, order details). They are excluded from all counts.

**Not covered, stated plainly:** Booksy, Treatwell, Vagaro, StyleSeat, Squire, Mindbody, Calendly, OpenTable, Revolut, Linear and Stripe returned no on-archetype screens in these 16 sweeps. Named searches for Booksy, Vagaro, StyleSeat, Treatwell and OpenTable resolved to Square Go, Fresha, Resy and Google Maps instead. Any claim about how those specific products build this screen would be invention, so there is none here.

## Dominant anatomy (top to bottom, mobile)

Counted over the **31 first-viewport screens**.

```
1  photographic hero            23 / 31
2  entity name                  31 / 31   (always the first text after the hero)
3  rating + review count        19 / 31   (and 19 / 19 of those show the count, never a bare average)
4  place / status meta line     26 / 31   (address, neighbourhood, open-closed, distance, or a mix)
5  action or context row        14 / 31   (Book / Call / Directions pills, or a Delivery|Pickup segment)
6  in-page section tabs         10 apps   (of the 38; underline-marked in 9, pill-marked in 1)
7  the bookable list            the page's spine wherever the product is bookable
8  reviews block
9  about + map + hours
10 persistent bottom bar        15 of the 21 mobile apps whose bottom edge I could see
```

**3. Rating always ships with its count, 19 of 19.** Not one screen in the corpus rendered an average alone. Formats seen: `5.0 ★★★★★ (83)` Fresha, `4.96 ★★★★★ | 298 Reviews` Airbnb, `4.9 ★ (7500+) Excellent` ClassPass, `★ 4.8 (5559)` Resy, `4.6 ★ (40k+ ratings)` DoorDash, `71 Good (513)` TravelPerk. Two apps additionally colour a qualitative word next to the number: ClassPass "Excellent" in accent purple, TravelPerk "Good" in a green score chip.

**5. Action row.** Two shapes. A horizontal pill row of peer actions: [Google Maps](https://mobbin.com/screens/c8bd1b60-ffb5-4c71-b6f1-73a7b9255aa8) (Directions filled blue, then Book, Call), [Square Go](https://mobbin.com/screens/e1e68966-8785-4703-9d99-958da86de00f) (Book filled purple, then Directions / Instagram / Hours / Links as icon-over-label circles), [Corner](https://mobbin.com/screens/34312b81-6deb-4aad-802d-6906b9c3ef53). Or a mode segment: [Uber Eats](https://mobbin.com/screens/bdd63dac-ea02-4c89-a3c0-8b316a454245) Delivery | Pickup | Dine out, selected rendered as a **white** pill on a light gray track.

**7. The bookable list.** 8 distinct booking-service implementations examined ([Fresha iOS](https://mobbin.com/screens/f2b83609-779f-4fc9-a249-ba3feeaa66a9), [Fresha web](https://mobbin.com/screens/a4ed4d89-766a-4cd7-8e34-92f56ad08a61), [Fresha select-services sheet](https://mobbin.com/screens/6215e2b9-fff2-495d-a78d-247ff90d313d), [Square Go](https://mobbin.com/screens/b302dc27-ed66-4442-a59e-1e4dc73799b1), [Careem](https://mobbin.com/screens/18967ec3-fd3a-4605-9c56-d6081fba09c6), [Time2book](https://mobbin.com/screens/d0da45c2-f9d2-4085-8585-d9e538b9c520), [Square web](https://mobbin.com/screens/1926963a-1b7c-41ca-8ca9-67e497e093c5), [ClassPass schedule](https://mobbin.com/screens/222757f4-74d8-435b-a780-6149bb5fac62)). Field frequency across those 8: name 8/8, duration 8/8, price 7/8 (ClassPass substitutes credits), a per-row action control 7/8 (ClassPass taps the whole row), a description 4/8, a thumbnail image 2/8.

**10. Persistent bottom bar, 15 of 21 mobile apps.** Present: Fresha, Square Go, Airbnb, ClassPass, Resy, Peerspace, Zomato, Swiggy, CRED, Viator, Careem, Preply, Udemy, Alan, and Uber Eats (cart pill only, appears after an item is added, [evidence](https://mobbin.com/screens/6536f8b0-9c05-4b28-927d-75e110ba1b6c)). Absent: Google Maps, Zocdoc, Corner, Open, Behance, Realtor.com. Resy is the odd one: its persistent bar is a **context selector** (`2 · Today · All Day`) and the commit affordance is the inline time-slot chips, not a button.

## Grid

**Mobile, 21 apps.** One column throughout. Hero is full-bleed to the device edges in every photographic case; body content sits in a consistent side gutter. Two hero-to-body joins exist:
- **Flush**, the photo ends and the white body begins on the same line: Fresha ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/daa7dd6f-8d5c-4ebe-b82c-a8838d417d80)), ClassPass, Uber Eats, Viator, Open.
- **Overlapping sheet**, a white panel with rounded top corners rides up over the bottom of the photo: Airbnb ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/8a6b4476-52d7-4c85-965b-2a870b388eb3)), Square Go ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/b302dc27-ed66-4442-a59e-1e4dc73799b1)), Time2book. Square Go and Time2book additionally hang a circular logo over that join.

Solen uses the flush join. Both are legal; the sheet costs a clipped photo edge and buys a softer transition.

**Desktop, 3 layouts across 9 web apps.**
1. **Main + sticky right rail** (the booking layout): Fresha ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/a4ed4d89-766a-4cd7-8e34-92f56ad08a61)), Airbnb ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/0c43c484-091f-41b7-a41c-df660291490f)), Tripadvisor, TravelPerk. Content roughly two thirds, rail one third. This is Solen's layout.
2. **Left rail nav + main** (the menu layout): DoorDash ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/87812f0a-00cf-44a9-ac86-23df42fca05f)), Uber Eats ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/2b30ab06-de56-4532-a423-5fda84f8bb29)). The rail is a category jump list, not a booking card.
3. **Media + identity left, content right** (Airbnb's newer Experiences and Services pages): [Experiences](https://mobbin.com/screens/b2b30371-93db-4d33-a461-fbcbfb898f83), [Services](https://mobbin.com/screens/a3b51edb-e3b8-4e1d-bf88-ce2d79b28a64). Solen's desktop hero is the Fresha 3-tile pattern, the smaller of the two dominant options.

## Type

**Measurement honesty:** Mobbin returns scaled screenshots, so no pixel value in this file is measured from them. Everything below is a relative reading of what is visibly larger, heavier or greyer, plus the sizes recorded in Solen's own section specs. Any absolute px claim about a competitor would be invention.

Observed consistently across the 31 first-viewport screens:
- **The entity name is the largest text on the page.** True in 31 of 31, including on the pages with no photo. On the four screens where the name wraps to two lines (Fresha "Creation beauty & Nail lounge", Square Go "Monica Andrea at Salon Cozzolino", Airbnb "Private bedroom in Manhattan Upper East Side", Viator "The Big Apple Coaster at New York New York Hotel and Casino") the type does not shrink to fit; it wraps. Worth carrying into the worst-case-content pass.
- **The rating line sits at body size**, never scaled up, and gets its emphasis from the star's colour rather than from size. Fresha, Airbnb, ClassPass, Resy and Google Maps all do this.
- **Grey is the demotion device**, not smaller type. Address, hours, duration, neighbourhood and reviewer dates are all roughly body-size in a lighter ink. Fresha's `1 hr to 1 hr, 45 min` under a service name is the clearest case.
- **Section headings step down from the page title but stay clearly above body.** Fresha's `Services` on web ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/a4ed4d89-766a-4cd7-8e34-92f56ad08a61)) is visibly the second-largest text on the page, below the H1 and above everything else.
- **Price is bold but not large.** In every booking implementation the price is a weight step, not a size step, and stays smaller than the service name. This matches Solen's locked two-anchor card rule.
- **Only two apps in the corpus use an uppercase eyebrow near the title:** CRED (`GOA`) and Open (`EXPERIENCES`). Both are grey, neither is a brand colour. The three beauty-booking products (Fresha, Square Go, Time2book) use no eyebrow at all; Fresha renders category as a neutral pale chip on its nearby-venue cards ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/b5191eb1-9235-4bff-8117-26efee919cff)).

## Motion

**What I can and cannot claim.** Mobbin `search_screens` returns static images. I observed no animation. Everything below is inferred from *pairs of screenshots of the same screen in two states*, which evidences that a transition exists but says nothing about its duration or easing. Labelled as inference throughout.

**Inferred, high confidence (two states of the same screen, both seen):**
- **Header swap on scroll.** Fresha: overlay circular back / share / heart on the photo ([unscrolled](https://mobbin.com/screens/daa7dd6f-8d5c-4ebe-b82c-a8838d417d80)) become a solid white bar with a truncated venue name, a share glyph and a **filled red** heart, with the tab row appearing beneath ([scrolled](https://mobbin.com/screens/f2b83609-779f-4fc9-a249-ba3feeaa66a9)). The same swap is evidenced in Resy ([before](https://mobbin.com/screens/3befa160-2161-42ad-9410-aeeff7fc1be9) / [after](https://mobbin.com/screens/58af01fd-0bc1-4221-b4e3-9db5d699b621)) and Uber Eats ([before](https://mobbin.com/screens/bdd63dac-ea02-4c89-a3c0-8b316a454245) / [after](https://mobbin.com/screens/4ba4ca45-430a-4969-b007-69f3076d69c3)).
- **Save toggles fill, it does not just outline.** Fresha's heart goes from white outline on the photo to solid red in the collapsed header. Airbnb web goes from `♡ Save` to `♥ Saved` plus a toast card `Saved to Nusapenida 2025 · Change` ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/cd605803-b697-4c92-872c-c8dba1e5ca73)).
- **Selection changes the border, not the fill.** Fresha's select-services card goes from hairline + `+` to accent border + filled check ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/6215e2b9-fff2-495d-a78d-247ff90d313d)). Headspace tints the whole therapist card instead ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/6d26d0ea-2cf7-4bf0-b1cf-51e45442bd08)).
- **Sticky bar state change on commit.** ClassPass goes from `8 credits` + blue `Reserve` ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/6f1ab7f6-bd06-475b-9d5f-303d59095692)) to `8 credits Reserved` + gray `Cancel` ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/d7736357-44b8-44f0-bb00-ecfb58194500)). The bar is the same container, so this is a content swap in place, not a new bar.
- **Cart pill enters** on Uber Eats once an item is added, as a floating dark pill rather than a full-width bar ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/6536f8b0-9c05-4b28-927d-75e110ba1b6c)).
- **Photo carousels are swipe-paged**, evidenced by counter pills at different indices on the same venue: ClassPass `1/4` and `4/4`, Airbnb `2 / 27`, Grab `1/8`, Vrbo `5/7`, plus dot indicators on Uber Eats and CRED.

**Not evidenced, do not assume:** parallax on the hero, easing curves, durations, spring versus tween, staggered list entry. If Solen needs a real motion reference for this screen, it needs a recorded capture (`record-interaction.mjs` via the reference-lock skill), not Mobbin.

**One Solen-specific note.** `18-sidebar.md` animates the desktop rail open and closed on scroll via `max-height + opacity`, 300ms. I found **zero** desktop rails in the corpus that collapse. Fresha's and Airbnb's rails are static and always expanded. That does not make Solen's wrong, but it means the behaviour has no external precedent to lean on, and it puts a moving element in the reader's peripheral vision while they are reading the service list.

## Solen gap

Six findings. Four are gaps against the corpus, two are contradictions between Solen documents that the corpus happens to expose. The contradictions are named rather than smoothed over.

**1. `04-services.md` ships a black-fill active chip, which the design contract forbids and a gate blocks.**
The spec's Tokens block reads `Chip active: border-s-ink bg-s-ink text-white (TabPill outline-active)`. The locked selected/active row is `bg-s-bg-sunken` (#F4F4F5) + `text-s-ink` + semibold, "NEVER black/ink fill on a selected state (gate `no-black-selected`)", owner-dated 2026-06-29, superseding the earlier ink-fill lock the spec was written against. The spec is stale, not the lock. The corpus is genuinely on the spec's side (6 of 7 chip implementations use a high-contrast fill, Fresha's is literally a black pill), which is exactly why this needs fixing as a *document* correction rather than reopening as a *design* question. I did not check whether the rendered component still emits the black fill or whether only the doc is stale, and that check is the first thing to do.

**2. `03-header.md` puts the category eyebrow in blue uppercase, which three separate locks forbid.**
Spec: `11px font-bold uppercase tracking-[0.06em] text-s-accent`. Against it: taste rule 3 lists eyebrows explicitly in the not-blue set; the category-tag row locks category to `bg-s-bg-sunken` + `text-s-ink-2` with no per-category colour; and copy rule 5 bans tracked-uppercase eyebrows outright. The corpus does not rescue it either: only 2 of 31 first-viewport screens carry an uppercase eyebrow (CRED, Open), both grey, and no beauty-booking product in the corpus uses one at all. Same treatment as gap 1: verify the render, then fix the doc or the component.

**3. Both empty states are a bare italic grey sentence, which the locked `states` row forbids by name.**
`04-services.md`: "Dieser Salon hat noch keine Services hinterlegt." `06-reviews.md`: "Noch keine Bewertungen." The lock requires `<EmptyState>` with a promise headline at 18/600, a gesture subline, a filled ink CTA to the filling action, and a 3D category icon, and it states that a bare status label is not acceptable. Eight apps in the corpus render a structured thin state, and Fresha does two things Solen does not: it *disables the Book button* when a venue has no bookable inventory ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/b239767c-f3eb-4602-852d-03715690388c)), and it gives a fully offline venue an iconed block with a real explanation ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/8c8faa36-57f6-4860-bf1a-58df3d7eb05f)). For a pre-launch catalogue where thin salons are the norm rather than the exception, this is the highest-value gap on the list: it is the state most seed salons will actually render.

**4. Not a gap, recorded so it does not get "fixed" later.** Solen shows no rating-distribution histogram and no per-dimension sub-ratings. That is correct. All 7 apps in the corpus with a histogram are commerce or content products (Etsy, Faire, UNIQLO, App Store, foodpanda, Tabby, Binance). Not one of the five booking products (Fresha, Airbnb, ClassPass, Square Go, Time2book) shows one. A salon with 4 reviews under a 5-bar histogram would look like a wireframe of a bigger product. Zocdoc's per-review sub-ratings (`Overall / Bedside manner / Wait time`, [shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/a219ce14-7dc6-457c-80b2-1c905fec7cb3)) are the one variant worth a second look, and only once review volume justifies it.

**5. The mobile book bar carries a button and nothing else, making it the emptiest in a 15-app field.** See P4. The fix is one live count, not a price. `17-mobile-book-bar.md` also records a deviation ("Fresha uses smaller centered pill, Solen mobile target benefits from larger tap target") that the corpus supports: Square Go, Preply, Alan, ClassPass and Airbnb all use a full-width or near-full-width button. That deviation is fine. The empty left slot is the issue.

**6. The desktop sidebar collapses to a naked CTA on scroll, with zero precedent in the corpus.** `18-sidebar.md` calls it "Solen-specific UX" and reasons that "collapsed shows ONLY the CTA, minimal noise when title block has the same info inches above". The premise is checkable and it is only half true: at `scrollY < 200` the title block is indeed inches above, but by the time the user is deep in the service list the title block is long gone and the rail is the only place the salon name, rating and hours could still live, which is exactly the state where Fresha and Airbnb keep theirs. Both hold their rail card static and always expanded ([Fresha](https://mobbin.com/screens/a4ed4d89-766a-4cd7-8e34-92f56ad08a61), [Airbnb](https://mobbin.com/screens/0c43c484-091f-41b7-a41c-df660291490f)). Tripadvisor goes further and pins the reserve button into the collapsed top nav ([shot](https://mobbin.com/screens/5df39934-c915-43d7-97f3-24b5118768af)). Recommendation is to drop the collapse and hold the rail expanded, but this is a taste call the owner owns, so it is raised rather than assumed.

**Unmeasured, flagged for a render pass.** Reading the section specs, the top three sections alone enumerate seven distinct font sizes (28 H1, 18 H2, 15 service name, 14 price and review comment, 13 description and count and chip, 12 duration, 11 eyebrow). The ceiling is four per screen. I did **not** render the page and measure, so this is a claim about the specs, not about the screen. It needs `getBoundingClientRect` on the live PDP first viewport before anyone acts on it. Flagging rather than asserting.
