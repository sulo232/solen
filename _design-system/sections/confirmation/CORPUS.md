# Booking confirmation, reference corpus

**Method:** Mobbin sweep, 8 screen searches + 1 flow search, ios and web queried separately, every returned image opened and read. Nothing here is described from brand memory; where I could not see a thing, this file says so.
**Solen surface:** `app/[locale]/confirmation/page.tsx` → `components-legacy/booking/BookingConfirmation.tsx` (656 lines, read for this doc)
**Layer:** research input. Not a component spec. Governs no literal; LOCKFILE still wins.

## Sample

**80 screens across 49 distinct apps** form the core corpus (a post-commit booking / appointment / reservation confirmation, or the appointment record the confirmation hands off to).

- ios: 59 screens / 32 apps
- web: 21 screens / 19 apps
- Walmart and Peerspace appear on both platforms. Mobbin lists "Square Go" and "Square", and "Uber Eats" and "Uber", as separate apps; this file counts them separately.

Examined but counted **outside** the core corpus, because they are a different job: 13 payment-success screens (ANZ Plus, Jobber, Tabby, Apple Wallet, WeChat, Vivid, Keeta, Lyft, Turo, State Farm, Binance, Depop, Lloyds), 9 pre-commit checkout screens (Fresha x4, Airbnb x5), 3 cancel-flow screens (Grab, Tock, Kakao T). **107 screens opened in total.**

Every count below names what it was counted over. Percentages are not used; the raw fraction is.

### The structural fork: dead-end vs live record

Roughly half the corpus (~24 of 49) builds a **dead-end**: a terminal screen you acknowledge and dismiss, never to return ([Lugg](https://mobbin.com/screens/d2465ca8-a428-4152-bfa5-fe037efab0b6), [Warby Parker](https://mobbin.com/screens/49e69531-025b-4dee-b457-397896728321), [Calendly](https://mobbin.com/screens/a4372e19-a051-4e2a-a7f6-84dbd09f96b8), [Maze](https://mobbin.com/screens/dd5c804f-eb14-4836-b6ad-df23308425c6), [Selfridges](https://mobbin.com/screens/6f289ec1-3c6e-453d-9316-0bad17f59988)).

The other half (~25 of 49) builds a **live record**: the same screen persists as the appointment, and its status token mutates ([Fresha](https://mobbin.com/screens/2a9d16da-ae0a-47c0-befe-38f4786731cc), [Tock](https://mobbin.com/screens/30629f52-becc-4ead-9170-4d0fe7867819), [Resy](https://mobbin.com/screens/dfb9fa4b-9d6c-49c1-8181-3b7a0ec8cfed), [Airbnb](https://mobbin.com/screens/bb3a9535-0ef0-4dc6-b2de-bbf5113ebc15), [Grab](https://mobbin.com/screens/6184757f-a516-462d-bf13-7a43770d25a8)).

The split is not arbitrary. **Utilities and one-off services build a dead-end; marketplaces where the booking has a life after the click build a record.** Solen is the second kind, and `BookingConfirmation.tsx` already behaves that way (it takes a `status` prop and branches on `cancelled`).

**Fresha does both, and this is the single most transferable structure in the corpus.** The [Fresha "Making an appointment" flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/98f220ae-7b2b-40d3-b298-88c73f8d32a6) is 18 screens; position 18 is a **full-bleed gradient interstitial** carrying nothing but a white check and "Appointment confirmed" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/c3f8e603-b767-4a69-9045-f72a434a7929)), and the record ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/2a9d16da-ae0a-47c0-befe-38f4786731cc)) is a separate screen with a photo hero, a status pill and the full detail stack. Fresha ships the same interstitial for a reschedule ([Reschedule confirmed](https://mobbin.com/screens/6adda4de-df96-456e-a827-35387d328a1a)). The celebration and the record are **two screens, not one compromise screen.**

## Grid

**ios.** 16px side gutters, near-universal. Two containers only: a full-bleed photo hero that ignores the gutter ([Fresha](https://mobbin.com/screens/2a9d16da-ae0a-47c0-befe-38f4786731cc), [Resy](https://mobbin.com/screens/dfb9fa4b-9d6c-49c1-8181-3b7a0ec8cfed), [TheFork](https://mobbin.com/screens/d250fdc0-78dd-4602-a179-6fbb9e080af7), [GetYourGuide](https://mobbin.com/screens/185cc90b-294d-4559-bccc-69d0f57a36d1)), and everything else inset to it.

Sheet vs full screen splits the corpus. Bottom sheet or modal: [Square Go](https://mobbin.com/screens/0c8bc4e6-cf74-45a7-997d-329392adbcdc), [IKEA](https://mobbin.com/screens/30632d40-03ca-4fda-a6bf-687b68a5f7c7), [TheFork](https://mobbin.com/screens/093934b6-b9f4-4297-9722-304a5deb1bd9), [Mailchimp](https://mobbin.com/screens/3fd5de80-0538-4a72-84f7-27771fa84c01). Full screen: Fresha, Airbnb, Resy, Grab, Tock, Zomato. **The dead-end pattern skews to sheets; the record pattern skews to full screens.** Solen renders full screen (`min-h-[100dvh]`), consistent with being a record.

Section separation on ios is a **grey gap band**, not a hairline, in the two most polished cases: [Airbnb](https://mobbin.com/screens/9dd2352e-f54f-4090-badf-40592c9ebe4e) and [Peerspace](https://mobbin.com/screens/f2991d25-baf3-4c7c-9960-1f0d69c34a5c) both drop an ~8px grey band between "About your host" and "Payment info". That is FLOORS LAW 4's sunken-tray rhythm, arrived at independently.

**web.** **15 of 19 web apps use one narrow centred column**, not the site's normal wide grid. Content widths I could read off the frames: [Calendly](https://mobbin.com/screens/a4372e19-a051-4e2a-a7f6-84dbd09f96b8) a ~230px inner card inside a ~540px white panel, [Time2book](https://mobbin.com/screens/9db418a8-b527-4c2f-9e24-57e482844c58) ~235px, [Cal.com](https://mobbin.com/screens/9d826d23-ed7e-4ec6-9f7f-c2b23f7d8f2a) ~260px, [Apollo](https://mobbin.com/screens/31e9f0b8-1037-4bcc-a143-dcc579dff44d) ~280px, [Square](https://mobbin.com/screens/726db486-bedd-4731-9603-dfd12e911ab4) ~300px. These are pixel readings off Mobbin's downscaled frames, not measured DOM, so treat them as a ratio (the column is roughly a third to a half of the frame) rather than a spec.

Only 4 of 19 use a two-column split: [Selfridges](https://mobbin.com/screens/6f289ec1-3c6e-453d-9316-0bad17f59988) (details + a store-services rail), [Headspace](https://mobbin.com/screens/a3e730a6-ff92-412b-be2a-40ffb10721b4) (headline left, card right), [Lyssna](https://mobbin.com/screens/bd1d0c30-def1-4a4f-bcca-4dee7660e27d), [Peerspace](https://mobbin.com/screens/fa39be75-16f5-4492-9cf2-854f6fe97904) (the only one where the rail earns it: a live price breakdown of $25.00 + $5.00 processing = $30.00).

## Type

1. **The affirmation headline sits at roughly 22 to 28px bold on ios.** I am reading proportion off downscaled frames, not measuring, so this is a range not a spec. Fresha's date anchor is the largest single text object I saw anywhere in the corpus ([here](https://mobbin.com/screens/2a9d16da-ae0a-47c0-befe-38f4786731cc)), running two lines at what looks like ~28 to 30px.

2. **13 of 49 have no display anchor.** Named above. They are all label/value tables. This is the corpus confirming FLOORS LAW 6 from the outside.

3. **Solen's headline is green when paid, and that is a contrast question, not a taste one.** `BookingConfirmation.tsx:436` applies `text-s-success` (`#16A34A`) to the h1 when `payment_status === 'paid'`. Per the CLAUDE.md contrast table, `#16A34A` is **3.30:1 on white**, below the 4.5:1 AA body floor. The h1 is declared `text-[24px] font-bold`, which clears WCAG's large-text threshold (>= 18.66px bold), so the applicable floor is 3:1 and **it passes, with 0.30 of headroom**. It is legal today and it is one size change away from not being. Pattern 1's status chip removes the dependency entirely: put the green on a chip with an icon, keep the headline ink. Only 3 apps in the corpus colour the headline itself ([User Interviews](https://mobbin.com/screens/088c9142-6025-444f-b4b0-4b13f8139879), [Lyssna](https://mobbin.com/screens/bd1d0c30-def1-4a4f-bcca-4dee7660e27d), [Trip.com](https://mobbin.com/screens/5def65c6-c26b-4a3d-adee-083b6d448839)); the other 46 keep it ink and put the colour on a glyph or a chip.

4. **Money is right-aligned and column-aligned in every screen that shows a breakdown**: [Peerspace web](https://mobbin.com/screens/fa39be75-16f5-4492-9cf2-854f6fe97904), [Selfridges](https://mobbin.com/screens/37c70da8-3cd4-4279-9beb-7de2696fba29), [Time2book](https://mobbin.com/screens/9db418a8-b527-4c2f-9e24-57e482844c58), [Fresha](https://mobbin.com/screens/48b3b2e7-2f86-4e75-b2bc-581efa025e1d). I can see the alignment; I cannot see whether the figures are tabular-lining from a screenshot, so I am not claiming that.

5. **Tracked uppercase: 7 of 49.** Banned for Solen anyway; logged so the ban is known to be cheap.

## Motion

**I cannot measure animation from static screenshots, and I did not.** Everything in this section is either a sequence I inferred from screen ORDER inside a Mobbin flow, or a static frame that depicts motion.

- **The commit button enters an in-place loading state before the screen changes.** In the [Fresha "Making an appointment" flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/98f220ae-7b2b-40d3-b298-88c73f8d32a6), position 17 is the review screen with the black Confirm button rendered as **three dots** ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/e3ac99d0-c9f5-4ea2-9149-14cafae40634)), and position 18 is the gradient celebration ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/c3f8e603-b767-4a69-9045-f72a434a7929)). So the sequence is: commit → button becomes a loader in place → full-bleed celebration → record. The button never leaves; the label is replaced. That is a sequence I read off the flow, not a transition I watched.
- **The [Square Go flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/7ab7c8e8-808e-475e-a543-1d2314dd47da) has no interstitial at all**: 11 screens, and position 11 is the "Thank you for booking with us" sheet. The sheet content simply swaps.
- **Confetti: 1 of 49.** [Apollo](https://mobbin.com/screens/31e9f0b8-1037-4bcc-a143-dcc579dff44d) draws static confetti into the card header. Nobody else in the corpus does. The success-moment vocabulary in this category is a check and a rise, not particles.
- **Toasts land on top of the record**, twice: [Jobber web](https://mobbin.com/screens/6147c920-8fab-4c4e-ab10-8d9dc9114fdc) ("Appointment confirmed ✓ ×", dark green, bottom centre) and [Peerspace](https://mobbin.com/screens/6df3bc20-42be-4261-820f-913be728c301) ("Review submitted successfully.", green, bottom). Solen already uses `toast.success` for the cancel and reschedule outcomes on this screen.

**Solen's existing motion, read from source (`app/globals.css:510-525`), for comparison:** `confirm-pop` 0.55s on a spring bezier `(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` for the disc, `confirm-ring` 0.75s ease-out for the pulse, `confirm-draw` 0.4s ease-out delayed 0.36s for the check stroke, `confirm-rise` 0.42s on `(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for the staggered content, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` block that lands on the final state. That is a more considered success vocabulary than anything I could observe in the corpus, because the corpus is static images. Only `celebrate-rise` is currently wired into the real screen.

## The Solen gap

Read from source, not rendered. I did not run the page for this doc, so the size claims below are what the source declares, not what the browser paints.

**1. No cancellation policy renders on the confirmation screen.** Grepping `BookingConfirmation.tsx` for cancellation returns only the cancel BUTTON, the cancel SHEET and the cancel API call. There is no policy text. **This is a defect, not a choice**, and it is the same class of defect that `app/[locale]/walk-in-pay/page.tsx` shipped and had fixed on 2026-07-27 (`cancelPolicy` defined in all four locale objects, zero JSX render sites). The corpus evidence is unambiguous on the direction: 15 of 49 state it, and every single app that takes real money with a real penalty is in those 15 ([Resy](https://mobbin.com/screens/51d3d6d3-64de-4cff-8915-10de47b2697f) names the franc figure, [Marriott](https://mobbin.com/screens/62586c5b-c349-4b2c-bae7-faf05490acef) names the fee and the deadline, [Airbnb](https://mobbin.com/screens/f4c764a3-7d5b-41ea-a8c7-342030d16e5f) names the date and the non-refundable consequence, [Fresha](https://mobbin.com/screens/48b3b2e7-2f86-4e75-b2bc-581efa025e1d) names the 72-hour window). Solen's own trust floor (hierarchy-density-05, LOCKFILE §17.6) binds the pay screen; nothing binds this one, which is exactly how the hole survived.

**2. The declared display anchor is 24px and the total is 29px.** Source: `text-[24px]` on the h1 (line 436), `text-[29px]` on the total (lines 528 and 548). FLOORS LAW 6 wants one anchor >= 28px. The total already clears it, so the screen is not anchor-less, but **the largest thing on a booking confirmation is the price**, and in the corpus a price is the anchor on exactly one app ([American Airlines](https://mobbin.com/screens/35024b36-d949-42db-9e8d-f676c695f65b)). The four apps that anchor on the date and time are the better model for a screen whose job is "remember when to show up". I did not render the page, so I have not confirmed these declared sizes paint as declared.

**3. Status is carried by headline colour.** Covered under type note 3. It is legal today at 24px bold and it is fragile.

**4. `SuccessMark` is built, registered, animated, and unused here.** Covered under components.

**What Solen already does that most of the corpus does not, and should not be "fixed" toward the majority:** the venue photograph (10 of 49), the ink primary CTA (4 of 49, and Airbnb is one of them at exactly this step), no cross-sell (Fresha, Airbnb, Tock and Resy agree), the buried reference code (Fresha and Airbnb agree), and a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback that no static corpus could have taught it.
