## Shape & geometry
1. Concentric corners , outer radius = inner radius + padding, never equal. LAW(GEOMETRY).
2. The 4pt grid , every gap and size is a multiple of 4. LAW(spacing).
3. Touch floor 44 , everything tappable is >=44px; pad invisible hit areas. LAW(contract).
4. Optical over mathematical , align to what the eye sees, not the bounding box. LAW(GEOMETRY).
5. One radius family per container type (card 16, input 12, sheet 28, pill round). LAW(contract).
6. Icons share one calibrated stroke table , never blind-scale. LAW(ig9).
7. Border OR shadow, never both on one box. LAW(FLOORS 17.2).
8. Every edge visible , a container must read against its actual background. LAW(FLOORS 17.2).
