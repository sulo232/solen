## The signal, compressed

Recurring themes across all 30 (agent-converged):
1. **Morph over cut.** Digit roll/blur (17, 25), icon shape-morph (16, 29), collapse-to-pill (18, 19), cascade population (17, 20, 22). The single clearest gap vs our stack: Motion-22 has press/collapse/burst but no state-transition morph vocabulary.
2. **Progress as discrete, countable marks.** Segmented tick-bars (26), numbered-step checklists (13, 26), traveling rail-dot (28), status-chip queues (06). Aligns WITH the house Swiss-precision restraint.
3. **Live-value feedback tied to the control.** Ring-around-the-control countdown (19), value-visualizing illustration (15), pill-marker riding a bar (03), CTA label carrying the exact amount (02, 15).
4. **Cool sunken neutral base everywhere.** #EFF3F4 (17), #F4F4F4 (22, exact match to our token), #F1F1F1 (20), #F5F5F7 (26). VALIDATES the 80/17 system; not a fork.
5. **The one real tension cluster:** colorful ambient backdrops / gradients (01, 03, 05, 07, 21, 27, 30), per-category color coding (04, 22, 23), non-ink primary CTAs (14 blue, 23 green), selected states that are NOT gray-fill (08 ring, 09 pill, 13 dark fill). Each conflicts with a dated lock. The fork of forks: is the color pull PORTFOLIO STAGING taste (how designers present) or PRODUCT taste (wanting more color on real screens)? Asked explicitly on the probe page.
6. **Mono/tabular numerals for all data** (26): argues for extending the Inter Tight tabular lock from codes to KPI numbers and timestamps.
7. **Real product nested in marketing tiles** (28, 30): explainer/landing tiles convince with miniature real UI, not icons or stock.
