# UNFINISHED-LOOK AUDIT , why law-compliant mockups read unfinished (2026-07-21)

Owner charge (verbatim): 'there is concrete flaw in taste file and design file cz what it presents always in mockup it always looks unfinished and ass.'

7 law files read end-to-end by independent auditors; opus judge synthesis. Status: findings REPORTED, owner decision pending , nothing applied to law files.

## Verdict

Yes, there is a concrete, locatable flaw, and it is not taste, it is structure. Across all six law files the enforceable content is one-directional: every mechanized gate and nearly every written rule is a CEILING or a BAN (max 4 type sizes, blue sparse, no dividers, no dots, no badges, no shadow on calm surfaces, gap-only separation, delete-the-word copy tests), and the only FLOORS in the entire system are accessibility floors (44px targets, contrast). The system's own theory (RESTRAINT_TEST.md, LOCKFILE:126, RATIONALE:329) is that photography (~50% of "life") and semantic color carry the warmth and richness while the chrome stays cool and quiet, but ONLY the chrome-restraint half is written as law and enforced, while the imagery/warmth/density half it depends on is either optional, editorial ("CONV, not a formula"), owner-declined, or queued-but-never-built. A builder optimizing for compliance therefore rolls downhill to the sparsest screen that passes every gate: removing an element trips zero gates, adding one can trip several. That screen is a wireframe by construction. RATIONALE.md already wrote the diagnosis verbatim ("the stack is analytic, not generative... its floors are prose, not computed") and then shipped no interim floor. The flaw is fixable with owner-approved values that already exist elsewhere in the system, not with new taste.

## The 5 root flaws

### 1. The law is enforcement-asymmetric: it has ceilings but no richness floors, so the compliance-optimal screen is the emptiest one that passes. Every mechanized gate polices EXCESS (drift hexes, >4 type sizes, banned glyphs); not one polices sparseness. This is the root mechanism under all the other findings, the gradient every mockup rolls down.

**Why it reads unfinished:** Under task focus the path of least resistance is always the sparser screen, because subtraction can never trip a gate and addition can. Repeated audits (solen-taste-diagnosis walks TASTE_HIERARCHY/TASTE_GROUPING, which are pure excess-detectors) can only ever say 'too much', never 'too little', so each pass ratchets the screen emptier. When the owner says 'unfinished' the instrument literally has no named violation for the complaint to land on, so the fix reverts to guessing. Emptiness and compliance are structurally correlated.

**Evidence:** RATIONALE.md:812 records its own judges: 'the stack is analytic, not generative... its floors are prose, not computed; only static-code drift is mechanized, every perceptual floor is eyeballed under task focus.' RATIONALE G1 ('rich not bland has zero mechanics') is ranked HIGHEST and was never built. TASTE_HIERARCHY.md: all numeric rules are caps (max 3 sizes, max 2 large elements), zero of its 8 symptoms fire on emptiness. TASTE_GROUPING.md findings 2/4/5/9 all name ADDED chrome as the violation; its 9-item checklist has no under-containment symptom. GEOMETRY doc section 5 admits its proposed gate 'passes an empty card... an accident of emptiness, not order.'

**Proposed law (owner to approve):** Pin into CLAUDE.md as a co-equal block with the 10 taste rules: 'FLOORS LAW (customer surfaces). Every ceiling in this system has a matching floor; restraint without a life source is a FAIL, not a style (LOCKFILE:126). A customer screen or mockup ships only when the RESTRAINT_TEST 5-question pass is all-Pass: (1) a photographic focal is present, (2) exactly one element is clearly the biggest, (3) at least one tabular/real number, (4) at least one semantic-color moment, (5) no dead-grey zone. Any rule that DELETES an element (copy economy, divider ladder, de-label, drop-eyebrow) must state what the screen KEEPS; a deletion is legal only if the surviving cue passes a measured floor (between-group gap >= 2x in-group gap, or a full weight/size/color step). Every NEW ceiling rule added to this system must name its floor or state that none exists. Build the pre-delivery self-walk (RATIONALE D4) as a Stop-gate that asserts these floors with numbers before any mockup reaches the owner.'

### 2. Imagery, the system's designated primary carrier of warmth and life, has no PRESENCE law anywhere: not how many photos, not what viewport share, not a designed no-photo fallback. Every rule strips color and decoration on the promise that photos supply the richness, then photos are optional.

**Why it reads unfinished:** This is the single biggest lever. The 80/17 cool-neutral model only works when the imagery layer it presupposes is actually on screen; Pinterest/Airbnb/Fresha read finished because 40-70% of every browse viewport is photography. A fully law-compliant Solen screen can render zero photos, and then the only visual material left is white, two greys, ink, and hairlines, which is the skeleton without the skin. A photo-first card with a bare grey placeholder box (SOURCE.md documents this as the sanctioned current state) is the literal wireframe tell.

**Evidence:** RATIONALE.md status line: 'imagery DECLINED' (owner 2026-07-15); G6 confirms it as a known unlanded gap. LOCKFILE §1.5.0 makes imagery conditional ('On any screen WITH a photo, the photo IS the colour'); §11 covers ~6 marketing surfaces and its sourcing policy is 'AI placeholders during sweep'. SOURCE.md §9 governs only photo TREATMENT (chrome B&W) and its BusinessTeaser row reads 'Currently a grey placeholder'. CLAUDE.md books photos inside the 17% ink budget, has no imagery contract row. RESTRAINT_TEST ranks photography ~50% of page 'life' and calls it a PUBLISHING GATE, yet nothing enforces presence.

**Proposed law (owner to approve):** Re-ask the owner for the imagery domain (G6 needs a fresh yes), then add LOCKFILE §11.6 + a CLAUDE.md contract row 'imagery': '(a) Every customer browse/discovery/PDP viewport at 375x812 carries >= ~1/3 photographic area; the photo is the largest element of every SalonCard. (b) A photo-first surface NEVER renders a bare grey box: a missing photo gets the spec'd literal fallback (bg-s-bg-sunken + centered 3D category icon + salon initial, exact size/tray recipe), never slot-omission. (c) Mockups pull real seeded photography; a zero-imagery customer mockup auto-fails review. (d) Screens with no legitimate photo slot (forms, checkout, legal, receipts) are exempt BY NAME so the floor stays checkable.' Promote the rich-not-bland memory into law at this tier.

### 3. There is no content-density or populated-state floor, and the production-correct omission doctrine (no fabricated data, dead-affordance = no chevron, omit-don't-invent) is applied unbounded to design mockups of a pre-launch product, so cards and screens get stripped to minimum viable content.

**Why it reads unfinished:** The one state finished products design and demo FIRST, the ideal populated state, is the one state this system leaves undefined; loading/empty/error are rigorously specced, the populated case is not. Under deletion pressure plus no-fabrication, agents strip cards to photo+name and screens to 2-3 items floating in white, and a correct-but-minimal surface is the definition of a wireframe. On a pre-launch product where much is unwired, the omission law is legally REQUIRED to make the mockup less complete than the product it depicts, so the owner judges a hollowed-out screen and reads the gap as bad taste rather than compliance.

**Evidence:** SOURCE.md §10 defines loading/empty/error/async but never populated content volume; §5 SalonCardProps are ALL optional (rating?, reviewCount?, photoUrl?). CLAUDE.md word 'density' appears nowhere in the taste rules or contract. RATIONALE G1/G9 ('Density defaults per CUSTOMER surface') are unbuilt; the only density floors live in TASTE_DASHBOARDS/TASTE_CHECKOUT. RATIONALE §17 dead-affordance law has no BOUNDARY line distinguishing production from a target-state mockup, though the file's own template demands one. LOCKFILE §14.1's pro-richness counterweight is one line against ~30 deletion rules.

**Proposed law (owner to approve):** Add SOURCE.md §10.0 'The populated state is the design target' + a CLAUDE.md contract row 'density floor': 'Every surface is specced and mocked at IDEAL density with named numbers sourced from the SEED library (seed data is real wired data, satisfying no-fabrication): PDP gallery >= 5 photos, reviews >= 3 visible, services >= 6 rows, home feed >= 4 sections; a populated list/grid first viewport shows >= 4 content units mobile / >= 6 desktop, plus a visibly cropped next item as the scroll-promise; a card renders its full info stack (photo, name, star+rating+count, category, city/distance, ab-CHF price) WHENEVER the data exists, omission legal only for null data, never for minimalism. Loading/empty/error DERIVE from this populated layout, not the reverse.' Add a BOUNDARY line to the dead-affordance and no-fabrication rules: they bind PRODUCTION and any current-state mockup; a target-state mockup renders full seeded content and carries a one-line footer naming what is not yet wired.

### 4. Warmth has no carrier and surfaces collapse to white-on-white with invisible container edges. Every warm neutral was retired and all tokens locked cool; card, page, and raised surface are the identical #FFFFFF; rest elevation is 3-4% alpha (near-invisible); the hairline is banned once a card is elevated; and the gray tray that would make a white card legible is placed by editorial judgment, not rule.

**Why it reads unfinished:** 'Premium-warm' is declared but unreachable by the tokens alone, so a photo-less compliant screen is white + cool grey + #6B6B6B: clinical/hospital, the cold half of the unfinished read (SENIOR_SCORECARD dim-3 'dead-grey' FAIL). And a white card + 4% shadow + banned hairline sitting on a white page has a boundary the eye cannot find, so content floats unanchored, the 'nothing is finished / everything floating' percept. Uber/Fresha get 'finished' partly from surface banding (white cards on a sunken substrate, alternating tinted sections); Solen's law makes that composition illegal-by-omission, so every compliant screen is a flat white sheet.

**Evidence:** LOCKFILE §1: sunken #F4F4F5 'COOL (reverses warm #F8F5F2)', border #E4E4E7 'COOL'; retired list kills s-cream/butter/sage/atm-*. SOURCE §2.1: base/surface/raised all #FFFFFF 'same hex'; elevation-1 = 0.04/0.03 alpha. RATIONALE §14 records the warm palette was locked and shipped TWICE (V3-D447, V3-D460) and reversed both times 'without a stated formal reason'; surface placement is 'CONV, an editorial choice, not a formula.' The depth law is THREE contradictory eras: LOCKFILE:488 '§3 shadow sparingly, many cards flat', :507 'quiet lift OR a hairline (both fine)', :521 'drop border on any elevated card (1px+flat = the dated tell)', while CLAUDE.md:55 still pins 'card shadow-elevation-2 rest'. The risk-minimizing compliant choice is hairline-only flat white, the wireframe.

**Proposed law (owner to approve):** Replace the depth trio with ONE literal surface->rest-treatment table (mark §3 'sparingly' and §3.5 'both fine' superseded), and rewrite CLAUDE.md's shadow + hairline rows to match: 'SalonCard = photo + shadow-whisper + NO border; grouped list card = whisper; PDP/booking sidebar card = hairline only; tile on a gray tray = white, no shadow; overlays/sheets/dropdowns = elevation-2/3; a card carrying elevation drops its border, never both.' Add an EDGE-VISIBILITY floor to the depth law: 'every elevated container must have a perceivable boundary against its ACTUAL background, achieved by (a) sitting on s-bg-sunken, (b) a flush photo edge, or (c) on white, keeping the hairline OR stepping to elevation-2; a white card with only a 4% shadow on white is invalid.' Promote the gray tray from editorial CONV to rule: 'grouped/list/panel content on white with no photo anchor REQUIRES the sunken tray; alternate gray and white down a page for rhythm.' Add a WARMTH-CARRIER law: 'the cool chrome is legal only when photography OR a semantic-color moment shares the viewport; a customer screen with zero warm/chromatic pixels outside pure chrome is the dead-grey FAIL, not restraint' (pair with the imagery floor as the guaranteed carrier).

### 5. The copyable law is stale and self-contradictory, and every unresolved contradiction resolves toward the thinner, flatter, lower-contrast value. Type weight/size, price weight, the text-grey ramp, and the emphasis ceiling all carry live conflicts, and the compliant reading is always the anemic one.

**Why it reads unfinished:** Agents copy the exact class strings, not the disclaimer above them, so a superseded-but-copyable table is a live drift generator. Text renders spindly and provisional (weight 300, grey, capped at 20px headings, default line-height); cards render as one dark word floating in uniform mid-grey (one-ink-anchor + titles-and-body-in-#6B6B6B); meta cannot recede from body because tertiary was collapsed onto secondary (both #6B6B6B). Low ink coverage and binary hierarchy read as 'nothing here is finished' even when layout is correct. The system's own supersession note diagnoses this ('Inter 400 reads solid where Hanken 400 read thin') and then leaves the thin values as the copyable law.

**Evidence:** SOURCE.md §3 note says values are SUPERSEDED (body Inter 400, Hero 700 never 800) but the role table below still specifies font-light 300 body, 800 heroes, Hanken/Bricolage; §21.5 still says 'Bricolage + Hanken'. CLAUDE.md's in-context ramp caps the largest legal size at clamp(...,20) with no leading/tracking/display role, and gives eyebrow THREE values (11px / '13px semibold' / 'drop entirely'). Price weight is simultaneously 400 (§2.5 A13 'never bold'), 600 (§2 scale), and 700 (§6 service row). §2.5 sets card titles (H3) AND body to #6B6B6B. LOCKFILE §1 marks s-ink-3 #6B6B6B 'collapsed onto ink-2', so the named 3-tier ramp renders as 2.

**Proposed law (owner to approve):** Rewrite the SOURCE.md §3 role table IN PLACE with the canonical literals (body Inter 400, secondary 400-500, headings 600, Hero 700 with leading/tracking), delete the Hanken/Bricolage rows and the 3.0x contrast-formula paragraph to an archive note, and fix §21.5. Replace CLAUDE.md's text-size row with the full role ramp INCLUDING display roles (each: size + weight + leading + tracking) plus the floor: 'every customer screen carries one display anchor >= 28px unless the photograph is the focal.' Adopt the V3-D442 two-anchor law into LOCKFILE as the single card-emphasis rule (deleting A13's 'never bold' and §6's 700): 'TWO ink anchors per card, name (larger, 600) + price (600, tabular); rating value ink-2 beside the yellow star; card titles/H3 are s-ink, not grey.' Reinstate a true tertiary grey (#9CA3AF, already in inventory as s-chart-2) for non-load-bearing text only (chevrons, placeholders, timestamps, hints), forbidden on load-bearing copy. Resolve the eyebrow triple-contradiction to one value in the same edit.

## What is NOT broken (do not touch)

Do not touch these, they are working and are not the cause of the unfinished look. (1) The restraint vocabulary itself is correct and well-sourced: the cool 80/17 palette, sparse-blue discipline, gray-not-blue selected states, semantic-color independence, no-decorative-dots, no-em-dash, 4-size type budget, and the LOCKED SalonCard/DateTimePicker/EmptyState primitives are all sound. The problem is the MISSING floor half, not the ceiling half; do not loosen the ceilings to compensate (that produces loud/busy, the opposite failure). (2) The excess-side research (TASTE_HIERARCHY findings 1-9, TASTE_GROUPING's divider/border discipline, GEOMETRY's optical-overshoot/keyline/concentric-radius/cap-height work) is genuinely rigorous and correctly sourced, invent no gaps there. (3) The state machine for loading/empty/error/async (SOURCE §10) and focus/disabled/touch-target are complete and locked, keep them; only the POPULATED state is undefined. (4) The mockup-first law, exists-check protocol, dual-axis (Fresha structure / Uber aesthetic), and the precedence chain are the right governance and should carry the new floor rules unchanged. (5) The no-fabricated-data rule is correct for production, do not weaken it, only add the target-state-mockup BOUNDARY so it stops hollowing out design artifacts. Crucially, the system already CONTAINS the missing floors as owner-approved values (RESTRAINT_TEST's 5-question pass, LOCKFILE §11 imagery patterns + §3.5 depth, RATIONALE's gap register G1/G6/G9, the 32/12/16 rhythm tiers, SENIOR_SCORECARD dim-3), they were researched and then left as prose or declined rather than pinned as enforceable in-context law, so nothing here needs inventing, only landing.
