## The asks, atomised

- [ ] 1. Re-analyse the taste / design rule files (the written law)
- [ ] 2. Re-analyse the harness that enforces them (which checks exist, which are wired, which are dead)
- [ ] 3. Take the PDP and the home page as the LOOK HE LIKES, and say what makes them that
- [ ] 4. Find the screens that use a different look, with numbers, not impressions
- [ ] 5. Make it more visually appealing: BIG changes, explicitly not small ones
- [ ] 6. Run it as a loop (build, grade, fix, repeat), not one pass
- [ ] 7. Deliver a FEW MOCKUPS he can look at, pictures rather than prose

## Standing constraints that do not move

- Mockup first. Nothing applied to a real screen until he picks.
- Pre-launch, so every row is seed data and nothing here is an outage.
- The look he likes is the BASELINE, so the PDP and home are the reference, not Airbnb, wherever the
  two disagree. Airbnb stays the tie-breaker only where our own two screens are silent.
