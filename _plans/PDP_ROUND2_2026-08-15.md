- [ ] PARKED 2026-08-15 · The "In der Nähe" rail on the salon page shows other salons in the same category. Replace it with (A) keep as is, (B) "Zuletzt angesehen" from the browsing history we already store, (C) a real "Ähnliche Stores" built on overlapping services and price band, or (D) B and C as two short rails? · from: his 2026-08-15 dictation, "instead of having, like, in [Ihrer Nähe], what about if we have, like, specific to their searches and something similar or something similar to the store instead"
  Detail behind that question, investigated first so it is concrete rather than open (this is context for the PARKED line above, not a second open ask):
      what renders today is `SalonVenuesNearby`, a carousel of OTHER SALONS IN THE SAME CATEGORY
      from `/api/salons/by-category?cat=X&limit=8`, built on the real `SalonCard`. So the rail is
      already "similar stores" in the crudest sense, same category, and that is probably why it
      reads generic to him.
      What already exists to replace it with, so nothing here is a new system:
        A. keep it as is.
        B. "Zuletzt angesehen", the salons this visitor actually opened. `useRecentlyViewed`
           (app/[locale]/_components/homepage/useRecentlyViewed.ts) plus a whole
           /[locale]/recently-viewed route already ship. This is the literal "specific to their
           searches" reading and it needs no new backend.
        C. "Ähnliche Stores", similar to THIS salon rather than merely same-category: overlapping
           services, comparable price band, same neighbourhood. This is the only option that
           needs a net-new query.
        D. B and C both, as two short rails.
      His words: "instead of having, like, in [Ihrer Nähe], what about if we have, like, specific
      to their searches and something similar or something similar to the store instead."

      THE COST OF HIS OWN PROPOSAL, named rather than agreed with (rule 3): option B is empty for a
      FIRST-TIME visitor, which pre-launch is nearly all of them. `useRecentlyViewed` reads
      localStorage, so someone arriving from search or a link has viewed exactly one salon, the one
      they are looking at. The 2026-07-06 taste decision says a data-driven surface with nothing to
      show HIDES rather than renders empty, so the rail would vanish on precisely the visit where
      the page has least to offer, and the current same-category rail at least always has rows. So
      B alone is a downgrade for new traffic; B plus C (option D) is the version that always has
      something, because C is computed from THIS salon and never depends on history.
      That is why my pick is D rather than B, and it is a real objection to the simplest reading of
      what he asked for, not a preference.
