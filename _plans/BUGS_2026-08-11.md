# Bug sweep, 2026-08-11 (owner: "bugs alot go find on yr own n tell me all")

He sent three phone screenshots of the search panel and asked for a full hunt. He chose: sweep the

## Confirmed by measurement, mine

### B1. With the keyboard up, the search field is gone , BLOCKS THE USER
Reproduce: open the panel, tap the field on a phone. Measured by rendering at 390x420, which is
roughly what an iPhone leaves once its keyboard is up:
  - the four category rows sit at y 563 to 835 on a 420px screen, so all four are off screen
  - the suggestion scroller computes to `clientHeight: 20` against `scrollHeight: 1611`, so there
    is a 20px window onto a 1611px list and nothing can be scrolled into view
  - the rendered screen shows the big "Wonach suchen Sie?" heading, Wo?, Wann? and a clipped
    Suchen button, and no search field at all
His third screenshot (IMG_7083) is this state on a real phone: the last thing visible is the word
"Kategorien", half behind Safari's own bar.

### B2. The Wo? step is more than half empty , LOOKS BROKEN
Measured off his own screenshot (IMG_7081, 1206x2622): one unbroken white run from y989 to y2375,
1386px, **53% of the whole phone**, below the last row. The house floor for trapped dead space is
30%.
Cause, read out of the code rather than guessed: the sheet splits its height between three step
slots and gives the OPEN step all the surplus (`surplus * weight`, `slotSizes` in
SearchOverlay.tsx:1050-1058). A step with two rows in it therefore gets a card the height of the
whole sheet. The same model is why the service step can look right (it has a long list) while Wo?
looks broken (it has two rows).

### B3. The month is cut off in the Wann? step , NEEDS ONE MORE CHECK
His second screenshot (IMG_7082) shows August ending at the 16th with no visible way to the rest.
Measured on the image: the white card ends at y1856. I have NOT yet reproduced whether the calendar
scrolls to the end of the month at his exact viewport height, so this stays unconfirmed rather than
being written up as fact.

      row on screen in all six, close X opacity 1. Commit c395c60b1.

      open-the-service-step path, which raises the keyboard. Measured: both rows jumped to y 844
      and 860 the instant Basel was tapped. They compose instead now. Commit c395c60b1.
- [x] **Today's date was blue in the calendar**, so it read as already chosen when nothing was.
