the gate-legality test written the same day (`~/.claude/commands/harden.md` section 0).

## What we lose, and it is not nothing

Six of the eighteen were the only thing guarding their failure. Named honestly rather than buried
quietly, because "bury them all" was answered before this was known:

1. **`chrome-consistency-gate.py`** , the ONLY check in the estate that compares two files against
   each other. Everything else reads one file in isolation. Its own docstring names the hole:
   *"consistency is a RELATIONSHIP, and nothing was checking relationships."* Comparing element
   order across sibling files is objective and cheap, so this is a legitimate re-entry candidate
   under the new rule.
2. **`overstep-gate.py`** , the largest single loss. It caught a layout change shipped under a
   content-only request: he asked to delete one invented line and also got the icons re-centred,
   padding moved 14 to 56, and the menu button pulled out of flow. Nothing left checks that the
   KIND of change matches the KIND of ask, outside files in the approved-mockups ledger.
3. **`locked-value-gate.py`** , nothing else reads a LOCKED marker inside an ordinary component or
   mockup file. The design-law corpus is guarded; individual files are not.
4. **`fan-out-not-one-agent-gate.py`** , points the same way as his decision 14 the same day
   ("fan out MANY design subagents, never one at a time"), so burying it leaves that brand-new
   decision with no enforcement. Judged dead as a GATE, not as a rule: "does this prompt describe
   big work" is read off prose, which fails the legality test. It belongs in the orchestration
   brief, which is where it now lives.
5. **`measure-dont-ask-gate.py`** , the residual it covered: a plain factual question about the
   rendered page that is neither a visual choice nor an offer to fix is now unguarded.
6. **`plain-english-gate.py`** , he asked for this one by name on 2026-07-31 and it never enforced
   anything. Superseded as an approach by his decision Q9 the same day: the fix is a reply-shape
   law, not another vocabulary blocklist. `~/.claude/REPLY_LAW.md` now owns it.
