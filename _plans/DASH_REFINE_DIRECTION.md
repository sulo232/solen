# DASH_REFINE_DIRECTION , dashboard simplification directions (subagent council, 2026-07-04)

> Owner ask: "think HOW to refine the dashboard so the store (salon owner) INSTANTLY gets it. Easy as fuck. NOT complicated." This is the DIRECTION-SETTING output (subagent council). Pixel MOCKUPS (3+ visual variations, side-by-side) + the external LLM council are DEFERRED to post-D1 (they must be built on the real dashboard skin, which the Aurora D1 merge would change). Grounded in the verified P1 findings: 45 pages, only 14 in nav, 9 built pages unreachable, 4 nav definitions (3 dead), 13-tab Settings, 20 admin pages sharing /dashboard.

## The Six
**Cut the rail from 14 owner items (plus 15 admin items bleeding into the same space) down to 6 top-level destinations; everything else nests one level deep under one of the six or moves off /dashboard entirely.**

- **Principle:** A salon owner should be able to name every item in the rail from memory after one look. Six is the number a human scans without reading labels, this is why Fresha, Square, and every serious POS rail tops out around 5-7. Today's rail asks the owner to parse 14 icon-only items (calendar vs. sales vs. reports vs. refunds vs. upcharge, four of which are just different slices of "money") plus silently shows them zero of the 9 built-but-unreachable pages including their own payout history. Ruthless cut means: if it is not something the owner opens most days, it is not a top-level rail icon.

- **Nav shape:** Rail collapses from 14 to 6 top-level icons: (1) Übersicht -> /dashboard (unchanged), (2) Kalender -> /dashboard/calendar, absorbs Warteschlange as an in-page tab for barbershop salons instead of a separate rail icon, (3) Verkäufe -> merges today's separate "sales" (bookings) + "refunds" + "upcharge" into ONE money surface at /dashboard/bookings with Rückerstattungen and Mehrbelastung as filter chips or a details-drawer action on a booking row, not their own destinations, plus a new "Auszahlungen" tab pulling in the orphaned /dashboard/earnings, (4) Katalog -> /dashboard/services, absorbs Pakete (/dashboard/bundles) as a tab inside it since both are "what I sell", (5) Team & Kund:innen -> merges /dashboard/staff and /dashboard/clients into one people surface with a segmented toggle (today they are two separate rail icons for what is conceptually one audience: the people around the business), (6) Mehr -> a single "more" icon that opens a sheet listing everything else: Berichte (analytics), Marketing, Bewertungen (reviews, currently orphaned), Galerie (gallery, orphaned), Treueprogramm (loyalty, orphaned), Einstellungen (settings, 13 tabs unchanged), plus the category-specific hub for that salon's type (nail-clients OR barber-clients+barber-ops OR coiffeur-crm OR spa-admin, only one shows, matching the salon's actual category instead of all four leaking into one owner's nav). Cases (Fälle) drops out of the owner rail entirely, it is adminOnly today and has no business being in an owner's mental model at all. ALL of ADMIN_NAV (approvals, all-salons, all-users, revenue, commission-admin, platform-analytics, badge-manager, content-editor, review-moderation, segments, editor, discovery-admin, homepage-admin, cities-admin, admin-sandbox) moves off the /dashboard/* URL space onto a separate /admin/* route tree with its own layout, so an owner logging in never sees an admin icon appear at all, today the rail literally renders ADMIN_NAV icons inline below a divider for any admin-role user on the SAME rail an owner uses.

- **Concrete moves:**
  - Merge /dashboard/refunds + /dashboard/upcharge into /dashboard/bookings as row-level actions or filter chips, delete their rail icons
  - Merge /dashboard/bundles into /dashboard/services as a tab (Leistungen | Pakete), delete the bundles rail icon
  - Merge /dashboard/staff + /dashboard/clients into one Team & Kund:innen destination with a segmented toggle, delete one of the two rail icons
  - Fold Warteschlange (barber-ops) into /dashboard/calendar as a tab for barbershop-category salons only, remove it as a standalone rail icon
  - Surface the orphaned /dashboard/earnings by adding an Auszahlungen tab to the merged sales/bookings surface, owners currently cannot find their own payout history at all
  - Surface /dashboard/reviews, /dashboard/gallery, /dashboard/loyalty by adding them to the new Mehr sheet, they are fully built and currently unreachable from any nav
  - Cut /dashboard/cases from the owner rail entirely (it is adminOnly, has no reason to occupy owner rail space)
  - Move all 15 ADMIN_NAV pages (approvals, all-salons, all-users, revenue, commission-admin, platform-analytics, badge-manager, content-editor, review-moderation, segments, editor, discovery-admin, homepage-admin, cities-admin, admin-sandbox) off /dashboard/* onto a separate /admin/* route+layout so they stop sharing rail space with owner pages
  - Show only ONE category hub (nail-clients OR barber-clients+barber-ops OR coiffeur-crm OR spa-admin) inside Mehr, gated by the salon's actual category, never all four
  - Delete 3 of the 4 dead nav definitions in code (RAIL_NAV survives as the only source of truth after the merge, OWNER_NAV_GROUPS/ADMIN_NAV-on-rail/STAFF_NAV get resolved into the new 6-destination + admin-split structure)
  - Re-skin nav labels to plain German with no jargon: Warteschlange/Katalog/Verkäufe stay, but kill 'Discovery Posts', 'Barber Ops', 'Coiffeur CRM' wherever the Mehr sheet surfaces them, replace with plain task language (e.g. 'Meine Fotos' not 'Gallery', 'Terminplan' context not 'CRM')

- **Tradeoffs:** Cost 1, extra tap for money detail: refunds and upcharge go from a dedicated rail icon to a filter/drawer inside bookings, a power-user who refunds constantly loses one tap of directness for the sake of a shorter rail; mitigate by making the refund action reachable from a booking row without opening a separate page. Cost 2, staff+clients merge changes a workflow: some owners think of "my team" and "my clients" as genuinely different daily tasks (managing a roster vs. managing a customer base) and a toggle inside one page reads as slower than two dedicated icons, this is a real risk and should get a mockup reaction before building. Cost 3, the Mehr bucket becomes a junk drawer: reviews, gallery, loyalty, reports, marketing, settings, and a category hub all landing in one sheet risks recreating the same discoverability problem one level deeper unless the sheet itself is well organized (grouped, not a flat list); if an owner uses reviews or marketing daily this direction under-serves them by demoting a frequent task to a sub-sheet. Cost 4, admin/owner split is a bigger engineering lift than a nav relabel: moving 15 admin pages to a new /admin/* route tree touches routing, auth checks, and any hardcoded /dashboard/ links across the admin pages themselves, this is not a pure IA change, it is real migration work and should be scoped as its own ticket rather than bundled into "nav cleanup". Cost 5, this direction optimizes discoverability over power-user speed: an owner who already knows the 14-item rail cold loses muscle memory on where things live; the payoff (a NEW owner "instantly gets it") trades against habituated owners needing to relearn 3 to 4 relocated destinations.

- **Mockup targets (post-D1):**
  - Collapsed 6-icon desktop rail (icon-only, hover tooltips) replacing the current 14-item RAIL_NAV in components-legacy/dashboard/DashboardLayout.tsx
  - The merged Verkäufe surface: /dashboard/bookings with a details-drawer or filter chip exposing refund/upcharge actions plus a new Auszahlungen tab pulling in /dashboard/earnings data
  - The Mehr sheet/panel: grouped list showing Berichte, Marketing, Bewertungen, Galerie, Treueprogramm, Einstellungen, and the ONE category hub relevant to a test salon's category

## Consolidate: fewer destinations, same capability, grouped by owner's real job
**Keep every one of the 45 pages' capability alive, but cut the number of places you have to click to reach it by turning today's 14-item flat RAIL_NAV plus 9 orphaned pages into roughly 9 destinations: merge related tools into tabbed hubs (Money, Team, Marketing, Kunden), fold the 4 category twins into the pages they belong to, and split the 13-tab Settings monolith into the 6 groups the mobile index already proves work.**

- **Principle:** One nav item = one JOB the owner has, not one ENGINEERING SURFACE. Today 'Verkäufe', 'Rückerstattungen', 'Mehrbelastung' and the payout page (earnings, unreachable) are four separate destinations for what is one job: 'money in and out of my business'. Group by job, not by which page.tsx file happens to exist.

- **Nav shape:** RAIL_NAV shrinks from 14 flat items to 9, keeping the existing `group` field mechanism (already live, lines 114-131 of DashboardLayout.tsx) but making groups do real consolidation work instead of just a mobile-sidebar label:
1. Übersicht -> /dashboard (unchanged, already the reference-quality page per findings)
2. Kalender -> /dashboard/calendar (unchanged)
3. Warteschlange -> /dashboard/barber-ops (unchanged, barbershop-only)
4. Katalog -> a hub tabbing today's /dashboard/services + /dashboard/bundles + /dashboard/products (services, package deals, retail products are the same 'what I sell' job; products today has zero nav entry per finding #1, bundles and services are two separate rail items for what an owner thinks of as one catalog)
5. Kund:innen -> a hub tabbing /dashboard/clients (CRM, unchanged core) + the category CRM twins folded in as an extra tab ONLY for that salon's category: coiffeur-crm's 4 sub-tabs (formulas/consultations/cycles/metrics) become a 'Haarprofile' tab inside Kund:innen for coiffeur salons, nail-clients/barber-clients become a 'Kundenkarten' tab for their categories. This directly resolves the owner-decision item (5 category pages unreachable) by putting them where an owner would actually look, not a new 'Spezial' group.
6. Team -> a hub tabbing /dashboard/staff (roster, schedule, commission, unchanged) + /dashboard/loyalty folded in as a 'Treue' tab only if it's actually owner-facing (loyalty is currently unreachable per finding #2; if it's a customer-loyalty admin view an owner tunes rarely, Team is wrong, it belongs in Money or a Marketing tab, flagging as an open call, not guessing)
7. Marketing -> extend the EXISTING tabbed hub at /dashboard/marketing (already proven pattern: 3 tabs, EmptyState/ErrorState wired correctly) to also carry /dashboard/gallery ('Portfolio' tab) and /dashboard/discovery-posts ('Beiträge' tab, rename from jargon), both currently unreachable per finding #2. Delete the dead GiftCardManager tab first (already flagged for REMOVED.md per finding, gift cards are killed marketplace-wide).
8. Geld (new, replaces 'Verkäufe' + 'Rückerstattungen' + 'Mehrbelastung' as 3 separate rail items) -> one hub tabbing /dashboard/bookings (renamed conceptually to 'Verkäufe' tab, already what it shows), /dashboard/earnings (the actual owner payout page, currently UNREACHABLE despite being the single highest-stakes page per finding #2, becomes the default tab), /dashboard/refunds, /dashboard/upcharge, and /dashboard/analytics ('Berichte', currently its own rail item) as a 'Berichte' tab since revenue reporting is the same job as money. This is the biggest single win: 4 rail items + 1 unreachable page collapse into 1.
9. Einstellungen -> unchanged rail entry, but the settings PAGE internally regroups its 13 tabs into the 6 buckets the mobile index (groupProfile/groupBookings/groupOffers/groupAbsence/groupFinance/groupCommunication, already coded at settings/page.tsx lines 1319-1363) already uses, applied to desktop too so the ExpandableTabs bar shows 6 groups you expand, not 13 flat labels. Verification also folds in here as a tab inside groupProfile (it is currently its own rail item AND unreachable as a page, a double redundancy) instead of being a 10th standalone destination.

Net effect: 14 rail items + 9 orphaned pages (23 destinations today) -> 9 rail items, zero orphans (every one of the 45 pages' worth of capability is reachable, either as its own item or as a tab inside one). Admin's 15 ADMIN_NAV items and the 20 admin-only pages are a SEPARATE decision (they already share middleware gating oddities per findings) and are out of scope for this owner-facing consolidation, though the same tabbed-hub technique applies there too (e.g. commission-admin + revenue + platform-analytics are one admin 'Plattform-Finanzen' hub) if the owner wants that pass later.

- **Concrete moves:**
  - MERGE /dashboard/bookings + /dashboard/earnings + /dashboard/refunds + /dashboard/upcharge + /dashboard/analytics into one 'Geld' tabbed hub, default tab = earnings (fixes the single highest-severity finding: the owner payout page is unreachable today)
  - MERGE /dashboard/bundles + /dashboard/products into the existing /dashboard/services (Katalog) as tabs, since products currently has zero nav entry (finding #1) and bundles is a second separate rail item for the same 'what I sell' job
  - MERGE the 4 category twins (coiffeur-crm, nail-clients, nail-admin, barber-clients) as category-conditional tabs inside /dashboard/clients (Kund:innen) instead of a dead 'Spezial' nav group, resolving the owner-decision item on 5 unreachable category pages without inventing new UI (category-nav.ts's existing per-category registry already has the right i18n-keyed data to drive which tab shows)
  - EXTEND the already-live /dashboard/marketing tabbed hub (3 tabs today: gift cards, referrals, promos) with 2 more tabs for /dashboard/gallery (Portfolio) and /dashboard/discovery-posts (Beiträge, renamed from jargon), both currently unreachable (finding #2); DELETE the dead GiftCardManager tab in the same pass since gift cards are marketplace-killed per REMOVED.md
  - FOLD /dashboard/verification into Settings as a tab inside the existing groupProfile bucket (it's currently both its own rail item AND an unreachable standalone page, a double redundancy) instead of keeping it as a 10th rail item
  - REGROUP Settings' 13 flat desktop tabs into the SAME 6 buckets (groupProfile/groupBookings/groupOffers/groupAbsence/groupFinance/groupCommunication) the mobile index already uses (settings/page.tsx:1319-1363), so desktop stops being one flat 13-label ExpandableTabs row
  - CUT the two dead nav definitions (OWNER_NAV_GROUPS and the categoryNavGroups useMemo in DashboardLayout.tsx, confirmed never referenced in JSX per finding) once RAIL_NAV is rebuilt as the 9-item version, so there is exactly one nav source of truth again
  - OPEN QUESTION, not a merge: loyalty (/dashboard/loyalty) is unreachable today and its correct home depends on whether it's an owner-tunable program config (belongs in Settings/Money) or a marketing lever (belongs in the Marketing hub); flagging rather than guessing

- **Tradeoffs:** Every hub means one extra tap to reach a specific sub-tool that used to be one tap from the rail (e.g. reaching refunds is now Geld -> Rückerstattungen tab, not a direct rail click); a high-frequency user who refunds constantly may feel that friction, whereas a nav-item-per-tool approach never adds a tap but never shrinks the destination count either. It also puts more weight on tab labeling: a badly-named tab hides a feature exactly as effectively as a badly-named rail item did, so the jargon-label fix (finding: 'Coiffeur CRM', 'Discovery Posts', 'Sandbox') has to happen INSIDE the hubs too, not just at the top level, or this just relocates the instantly-get-it problem one level deeper. Category-conditional tabs (coiffeur/nail/barber logic living inside Kund:innen and Katalog) reintroduce a small amount of the 'if category === X' branching the component registry rules warn against (rule B5), so those tabs need to be data-driven off category-nav.ts's existing registry, not new hardcoded branches, or this direction quietly re-creates the exact duplication problem it's trying to remove. Finally, this is pure IA restructuring: it does nothing to fix the today_revenue KPI bug, the s-coral black-selected-tab bug, or the calendar mobile table, those stay exactly as broken as findings describe until a separate pass touches them.

- **Mockup targets (post-D1):**
  - Kund:innen hub showing the client CRM default tab plus a category-conditional 'Haarprofile' tab for a coiffeur test salon
  - Geld hub showing the earnings/payout default tab with Verkäufe, Rückerstattungen, Mehrbelastung, Berichte as sibling tabs
  - Settings page desktop view regrouped into 6 expandable buckets instead of 13 flat tabs

## Task/Day-First (Direction C)
**Nav stops mirroring database tables (bookings, clients, analytics, settings) and starts mirroring the owner's actual day: run today, get paid, manage my clients, manage my setup.**

- **Principle:** A salon owner opens the dashboard with a question in their head (who's coming in, did I get paid, why can't a client find my hours), not a data-model in their head (I need the analytics entity). Nav items should be answers to the owner's questions, not names of tables.

- **Nav shape:** 4 top-level items, replacing the current 14-item RAIL_NAV: 1) Heute (home, default landing) 2) Bezahlt werden 3) Meine Kunden 4) Mein Betrieb. Category hubs (nail, barber, coiffeur, spa) are not separate nav items, they render as a conditional section inside Heute, shown only for that salon's own category. All 20 admin-only pages move to a separate /admin shell entirely, off the owner-facing /dashboard nav.

- **Concrete moves:**
  - Kill the 3 dead nav definitions in code now; only RAIL_NAV (components-legacy/dashboard/DashboardLayout.tsx:114-131) is live, the other 3 are pure dead code with zero product risk to remove.
  - Promote 'Heute' as the default dashboard landing by merging calendar + bookings + staff (who's working today) onto one surface. This is the daily core the findings already call tight, so make it the front door instead of a generic dashboard home.
  - Fold each category hub (nail-admin + nail-clients, barber-ops + barber-clients, coiffeur-crm, spa-admin) into Heute as a conditional section keyed to the salon's category, not a standalone nav item. Fixes the 0-discovery-path problem for all 5 hubs without adding 5 nav rows, and forces a fix to the nail-admin middleware bug (wrongly gated admin-only) as part of the same move.
  - Create 'Bezahlt werden' as a new top-level item and move earnings (owner payouts, currently fully unreachable) into it as the primary surface, not a footnote. Merge revenue + analytics into one page under this item (they're the same job viewed from two entity names); move refunds here too since 'did a refund hit my payout' is the same job as 'did I get paid'.
  - Create 'Meine Kunden' as a top-level item holding clients (existing), plus reviews and loyalty pulled out of total unreachability, plus messages. Rationale: reviews and loyalty are both about the relationship with a client, not a separate 'engagement' or 'reputation' bucket a non-technical owner wouldn't recognize.
  - Create 'Mein Betrieb' as a top-level item holding services, products, gallery, bundles, and verification (rescued from unreachable, reframed as 'prove my business is real' which is a setup task). Break the existing 13-tab settings monolith into these same 4 top-level buckets by task instead of shipping it as one flat tab bar: payment/commission/VAT/off-peak tabs move under Bezahlt werden, profile/hours/vacation/closures/scheduling/cancellation tabs stay under Mein Betrieb, quick-replies moves under Meine Kunden (it's reply templates to clients).
  - Relocate all 20 admin-only pages (all-salons, all-users, commission-admin, homepage-admin, platform-analytics, review-moderation, cities-admin, discovery-admin, segments, approvals, admin-sandbox, content-editor, editor, badge-manager, discovery-posts, and others sharing the /dashboard URL space) to a separate /admin route and nav shell, so an owner never sees a page meant for the Solen internal team.
  - Rename nav labels to plain German with no English jargon leaking through (kills 'Discovery Posts', 'Barber Ops', 'Coiffeur CRM' as owner-facing strings; those concepts still exist in code, just not in owner vocabulary).

- **Tradeoffs:** Owners lose the muscle memory of "Settings has everything" since the 13-tab monolith splits across 3 of the 4 top-level groups; expect a few "where did VAT go" moments before the new model sticks. Category hubs stop being separately bookmarkable top-level pages (the routes can still exist, but they're no longer distinct nav entries), so a nail-salon owner who previously deep-linked /dashboard/nail-clients loses a dedicated nav row, even though it now surfaces automatically inside Heute. This direction also asks the business to formally split owner-space from admin-space (/admin as its own shell), which is a real engineering lift, not just a relabel, since 20 pages need to move and their access checks re-verified.

- **Mockup targets (post-D1):**
  - Heute (default landing) showing calendar/bookings/staff merged plus a category-hub section for one non-hair category (e.g. nail-admin) to prove the conditional-section idea reads as one coherent surface, not a bolted-on widget
  - Bezahlt werden showing earnings as the primary payout surface with revenue/analytics merged beneath it, proving the unreachable earnings page becomes the obvious answer to 'did I get paid'
  - Mein Betrieb showing the broken-up settings tabs regrouped by task (profile/hours/policies here, payment/VAT tabs pulled out to Bezahlt werden) to prove the 13-tab monolith actually shrinks instead of just moving
