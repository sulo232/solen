## What Solen already does RIGHT (do not "fix" these)

A fresh session's instinct is to improve things. **Several of these took three tries to get right.** Leave them alone.

- **Idempotency keys** on every sampled Stripe charge site, keyed on business-invariant fields computed BEFORE the call, correctly propagated to 5+ money sites. Confirmed independently by three separate audits.
- **`getUser()` discipline is real project-wide**, not aspirational: all 28 `getSession()` hits are verified client-only, and it is hook-enforced.
- **Booking slot-claim**: app-level CAS + a GIST EXCLUDE constraint + a second independent unique partial index. Textbook layered defence.
- **Credit/voucher/promo/member-discount/staff-limit RPCs** all take their lock BEFORE any read (hardened three separate times after real bugs).
- **`processed_webhook_events`** PK-claim + release-on-throw. The only inbound webhook route, and it is correct.
- **Forward-only migrations**: genuinely followed, 0 down migrations across 264 files. PostgREST auto-reload confirmed live.
- **Zero float/money-type currency columns across 148 tables.** The most dangerous data axis, fully clean. Plus 240/240 `timestamptz`, 0 native ENUM, RLS on 148/148.
- **The SSRF guard is better than the research credited**: range-checks the resolved IP, blocks 169.254/16 explicitly plus CGNAT, and both real call sites also pass `redirect:"manual"`.
- **CORS is a real allowlist**, verified live. Mass assignment blocked everywhere sampled.
- **The cache personalization split is correct AND test-verified** (`scripts/ring5b-kill-test.ts` proves it against real invocations). Zero `Vary: Cookie`.
- **`audit_log` is well-designed and load-bearing**: RLS-enabled, indexed, 30+ write sites, and it never blocks the main operation on an audit-write failure.
- **PostHog's consent gate fails closed** and caches only clean reads. Real GDPR/nFADP discipline.
- **Sentry was scaffolded and deliberately REMOVED** for PostHog. "Add error tracking" must not mean "add Sentry."
- **Rate limiting is actively maintained**: 3 prior findings (spoofable XFF keying, SMS victim-bombing, directory-claim brute force) all confirmed FIXED, and coverage went from 1/49 admin routes to 44/51.
- **No premature infra anywhere sampled**: no GraphQL/tRPC, no OpenAPI, no hot standby, no lock service, no broker, no circuit breaker, no SIGTERM handler, no materialized views, no Redis-as-cache, no OTel. All correctly deferred.
