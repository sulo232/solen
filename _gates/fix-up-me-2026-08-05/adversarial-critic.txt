**3. Containment's blind spot**

Containment inspects an artifact: a final reply, a tool payload, a written file. Bias 1, the largest class, has no artifact. The failure is a browser run that never happened, and A2 concedes the gate is "structurally blind to the case where the reference is never mentioned because it was never visited." You cannot contain an absence at the output boundary; you can only contain it at the input boundary by making the tool call a precondition of the task, which is elimination, not containment.

Second, containment is satisfiable by string. `pushback-gate.py` line 79 accepts `might just be agreeing|can't find a reason|push back on me`. Typing rule 3's own escape is the cheapest legal exit, and it fails LAW_SYSTEM §2's own anti-superficial test.

Third and worst: containment has no containment on itself. Nothing verifies that a written gate is a wired gate. That is precisely how 32 dead files accumulated while the docs claimed enforcement.

**4. What is missing**

Neither input covers **dictation noise**. He speaks his messages; ASR mangles product terms, and the assistant resolves a garbled token to the nearest plausible referent instead of asking. That is anchoring on a corrupted anchor, and it is invisible in the corpus because the transcript records the mangled word as though he typed it.

Neither input covers **audience-blind evidence**, and this is the biggest personalisation gap. He cannot read code. Nearly every verification artifact the assistant produces (self-test output, diffs, docstrings, "5/5") is unfalsifiable by the only person who reads it. That is why "self-tested" survived as a completion signal across 32 dead gates: no reviewer existed. Evidence he cannot check is not evidence, it is a costume.

Neither input applies **Liu et al. 2024** ([arXiv:2307.03172](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172)) to *his own messages*. A 400-word dictated message has a middle, and its middle asks get dropped. A2 files that under completeness, which hides the mechanism.

Missing in the other direction: no base rate. 31 occurrences over an unknown number of opportunities is not a rate. And the corpus only contains failures he could *see*, so anything backend-shaped is structurally absent from a dataset presented as an estate-wide census.

**5. The uncomfortable one**

The law corpus is the largest single instance of the bias it documents. 204 hook scripts, a 10KB project CLAUDE.md, dozens of law files, 32 enforcing nothing while the docs say they do. Writing law is the cheapest output that reads as progress, cannot fail visibly, and ends the turn. The owner commissioned it and both parties get a system that feels controlled. The honest recommendation is subtraction: audit every gate against the settings files, wire or delete, cap the total, and make "I wrote a gate" an illegal answer to a repeat complaint.
