Quick read of each:

  • A · CURRENT — image in padded card, rounded corners, text overlay. Feels "contained" — like a slide, not a hero.
  • B · BLEED + TEXT BELOW — image edge-to-edge then text on white. Most legible text. Closest to Revolut's "Your money's safe space" pattern. Two visual moments stacked.
  • C · BLEED + OVERLAY — image edge-to-edge, taller, text overlaid bottom. One unified hero moment. Image is the "stage."
  • D · IMMERSIVE — image fills the viewport, text floats at bottom. Most dramatic, most "cinematic." Risk: pushes everything else WAY down the page.

My pick: B for clarity, C for impact. Tell me which (or a hybrid) and I'll wire it.
