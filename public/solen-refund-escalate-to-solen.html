Escalate to Solen — affordance + states

Item 7 of the refund-appeal inventory. The escalate affordance is the bridge in Lane B: it appears only after the salon rejects a refund request, lets the customer ask Solen to take a final look, then tracks the admin decision. Structure follows the Treatwell / Fresha case-timeline pattern; aesthetic follows the Solen CANON.

case state machine · open → salon_reviewing → salon_rejected → escalated → admin_approved / admin_rejected → refunded
