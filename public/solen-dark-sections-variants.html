What to pick

  • Card style (Layer A): A1 (control), A2 (warm + white cards), A3 (pure black + white cards), or A4 (dark cards inverted). My rec: A2 — warm s-ink stays on brand, white cards keep photo legibility.
  • Page rhythm (Layer B): B1 (no change), B2 (1 dark zone for emphasis), or B3 (Revolut-pattern). My rec: B2 for first try — small commitment, see if dark feels right, expand later.
  • About 80/17/3 rule: B3 would push ink usage past 30% of page area — violates the locked color rule. B2 keeps you ~closer to the rule. If you want B3 anyway, we need to revise the rule explicitly.
  • What happens to brand-green CTAs on dark bg: green stays green (still readable) but loses contrast pop. May need a brighter green token (`#16A34A` → `#22C55E` ≈ Tailwind green-500) for dark sections.
