My narrow shortlist (4 of 12)

1.Solen Sunset #E58840 — the established equity. Familiar, warm, salon-coded. Lowest risk.

2.Hot Pink #E54088 — beauty-coded, modern, Glossier-tier energy. Highest brand differentiation. The salmon-pink heart semantic (#CC4A60) already lives in the system — this would extend that warmth into the primary.

3.Honey Amber #E5B440 — premium golden register. Pairs well with the warm illustrations. Slightly closer to "wellness/spa" register than orange.

4.Mint Teal #40E5B4 — if you want to stay in the green family but escape the deep forest. Bright, modern, wellness-coded. Risk: closer to medical brand than beauty brand.

Don't pick from: Crimson (semantic-error overlap), Spring lime (availability-green overlap), Sky blue (too tech-y for beauty), Cobalt (Linear-coded), Violet (Stripe-coded).
