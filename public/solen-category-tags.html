My lean: B (neutral fill). The B&W pivot's whole point is colour = meaning, and a category label isn't a status, it's just a noun, so it shouldn't spend colour. Blue/green/yellow stay reserved for accent / available / rating. But A is defensible if you want category to be instantly scannable across a long results list. Whichever you pick is a 4-line change in CAT_TAG_COLOURS.
