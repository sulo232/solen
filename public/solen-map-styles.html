Why D: it's Fresha's exact map — same OSM street density, same labels, same structure — but a one-line CSS filter: grayscale(1) contrast(1.06) renders it in Solen's monochrome. High contrast fixes the "washed-out" problem of A (light-v11), and it stays brand-coherent in the B&W app instead of being the one rectangle screaming colour. The honest tension: you asked for an exact Fresha copy, and B is that — full colour. If you want the literal colour map, it's B. If you want Fresha's look fitted to Solen, it's D. That's the call this page is for.
