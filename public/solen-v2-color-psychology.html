Solen — Earthen Wellness palette

Warm earth tones replace the V3 dark-teal + ice-blue. Psychologically, the palette balances warmth (beauty industry alignment) with groundedness (trust required for booking). Color choices below are justified individually, not picked for aesthetics alone.

Moss
#2D3F33
Primary brand. Replaces dark-teal. Forest canopy, not hospital corridor — serious without being cold.
Terracotta
#C97A57
Heartbeat. Warmth + beauty-industry alignment without screaming. Used on CTAs + accent words.
Cream
#F5EBDD
Page bg. Lower eye-strain than pure white during browsing. Open, low-stress.
Sage
#A8B89A
Wellness accent. Whispered "natural / good for you" cue. Aesop / Necessaire territory.
Bone
#E8DDC9
Alternate surface. Differentiates cards on cream without harsh contrast.
Ink (warm)
#2A1F18
Text. Warm charcoal — pure black fights with the warm bg. Same readability, softer feel.

Why this beats V3 dark-teal

• Less category-stereotype: dark-teal-on-pale-blue is what every booking app does (Booksy, Fresha tendencies). Earth + sage + terracotta is distinctive in DACH market.
• Psychologically warmer: terracotta CTA reads as "treat yourself" — V3 dark-teal reads as "checkout button on a banking app".
• Wellness-aligned: sage + cream + moss is the visual language of premium-natural beauty (Aesop, Tata Harper, Necessaire). Solen positions as "the trusted way to find beauty" — palette delivers that emotionally.
• Gender-neutral: warm earth doesn't lean masculine (deep navy) or feminine (millennial pink). Coiffeur, barber, nails, spa — all fit comfortably.
• Photo-friendly: salon photos (which will replace the placeholder letters in production) photograph richer against cream/bone than against pale teal.
• Same brand discipline: 5 tokens + 2 tints. Same "1 saturated accent ≤10%" rule (terracotta is the accent), same dot-cue, same Peace Sans + Open Sauce One typography.
