What I looked up, and what changed

  • Barber chair. A real one swivels a full turn on its base and drops on a foot-pumped hydraulic. It was doing a small nervous sway. Now it swivels properly and the seat sinks on the pedestal, which is the thing a barber chair is actually known for.
  • Nail polish. The cap is threaded, so it unscrews. It was lifting straight up like a pen lid. Now it turns as it comes off and screws back down, and the brush swings round with it.
  • Spa stones. Rocking and settling, not floating apart, because that is what a balanced stack does. This round cleaned two more things off them: the green flecks, which were the leaf's colour bleeding down over the stones and are now gone across all 210 frames, and the brightness, which was 40% lighter than your own spa icon and now matches it.
  • Blow dryer. Untouched, as you asked.
