Why the air keeps looking wrong

The air is put back the way you liked it. Below is the actual reason no amount of tuning has fixed it.

The core cause

Everything in these icons is a lit 3D object except the air, which is a line I paint on top afterwards. A solid form keeps its pixels when you shave one pixel off its whole outline. A line is almost all outline, so it does not.

ElementPixelsSurvives a 1px shave
Airbnb, the house729890.0%
Airbnb, the balloon365992.7%
Airbnb, the bell402592.2%
Our dryer body447091.1%
Our air17646.6%

That single row is the whole problem. It is the only drawing on a page of objects, so it reads as a sticker no matter what colour or shape I give it. It also explains why my last change made it worse for you: I made it longer and more prominent, which just enlarged the one thing that does not belong.

Two ways out

Build the air as real geometry

A tube in the same 3D scene, lit by the same light as the dryer. Then it has an inside, a lit side and a shadow side, like everything else. This is the fix that removes the cause.

Or drop the air

None of Airbnb's three icons adds a motion mark. The house has a tree that sways, the balloon and the bell just move. The dryer would simply sway.
