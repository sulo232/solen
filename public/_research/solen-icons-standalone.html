Why the stones looked cut out

They were one flat tone. The renderer was crushing all the light and shadow into a band too narrow to see, so three lit pebbles came out as three paper shapes. They also sat as perfectly concentric ellipses, which reads machined; each one is nudged off-axis now so the stack looks balanced by hand.
