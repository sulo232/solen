The stones

They lift apart from each other and settle back, each one travelling a little further than the one below, so the stack opens rather than moving as a block. The motion is right. The colour is not: the mesh came back mauve with green speckles bleeding off the leaf, and that is baked into the texture, so it needs a new source rather than a render setting.
