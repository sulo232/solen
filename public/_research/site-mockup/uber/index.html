Every page, in Uber's current look

Measured pixel by pixel from your 11 Uber screenshots, plus Uber's own published design tokens. Same pages, same real data; only the look changes.

What Uber does

  • Mostly flat. Lists, cards, chips and buttons have no shadow. Cards get a thin grey line or nothing.
  • Shadow only on things that float above the page: the main search bar, the bottom bar, toasts, round buttons sitting on a photo, the picked side of a two-way switch, and the bar at the bottom when content scrolls under it.
  • Colour means something. green for proof ("Popular", "Required"), red for promos and missing things, cream and amber for membership (Uber One). Photos carry the rest. Everything else is grey and black.
  • Main button: black, 56px tall, 8px corners (not fully round). Chips, search and round buttons stay fully round.
  • Type: section titles bold (about 21px), rows 16px, second lines dark grey 15px.
