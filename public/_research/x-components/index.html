Your X saves vs Solen

I went through 280 of your saved posts, component by component, and put what they do next to what we do today. Each section ends with our version, built in Solen's colours. Their colours and dark mode are left out.

  1. White page everywhere, dashboard included. Boxes float on one soft shadow, no outline. (11 of 12 posts that show it separate cards with a shadow; 12 of our 13 card styles use an outline instead.)
  2. Three corner sizes. 20 for boxes, photos and sheets. 12 for things inside a box. Fully round for anything you tap. (We use 12, 13, 14, 16, 20, 22, 24 and 40 today.)
  3. Ink means main action or selected. Grey means every other control. Blue is only link text. (Today the selected state has four looks: ink, grey, blue tint, bold text only, and switches and time slots are blue.)
  4. Hairlines only inside a box, between rows. Never around it.
  5. One circle button. Grey circle on plain pages, glass circle on photos. Same for back, menu, share and close.
  6. Colour only when it means something: staff colour in the calendar, green or grey for booking status, green for going up.
