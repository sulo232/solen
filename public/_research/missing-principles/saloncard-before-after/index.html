The imagery floor, before and after

One real screen, the Basel city page, at 390 by 844. Left is what ships today, copied from the live DOM rather than redrawn. Right is the same markup with only the treatment changed, so you can judge whether the principle is right instead of taking a percentage on trust.

Read this before you judge the left column The empty photo slot is not a bug. SalonCard.tsx:152 carries a dated lock: "A3 LOCKED 2026-05-03: photos killed pre-launch, solid category color + Anton salon name". Line 94 still builds the photo list from cover_photo_url and then deliberately does not use it. The card is obeying you exactly. FLOORS LAW 2, dated 2026-07-21, then asks for roughly a third of every browse viewport to be photographic. Both are your decisions, they contradict each other on this screen, and nobody reconciled them. The later one wins by the precedence chain, but that is your call to make out loud, which is what this page is for.

The caveat that changes what you do next. Those photos are the real cover_photo_url values from your live database, self-hosted here so this page works offline. Every one is an Unsplash stock URL, and two different salons currently point at the same photograph. salon_photos has zero rows for 28 salons. So the right-hand column is reachable today with a wiring change and it would still be stock. The imagery floor is not really a design problem, it is a content problem wearing a percentage, and real photography of the actual salons is the only thing that makes it true.
