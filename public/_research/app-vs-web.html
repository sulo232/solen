What the app has and has not got

  • It has five tabs, a home feed, search, map, discover, profile, the salon page, booking, offers, reviews, onboarding and the hair profile. Around 30 real screens.
  • It is missing everything after you book: the confirmation, your appointments, the whole walk-in and queue flow, tipping, vouchers, loyalty, and the category landing pages.
  • It is carrying 35 mockup screens sitting in the app next to the 30 real ones, and 110KB of design notes across 14 files, seven of which are about onboarding alone.
