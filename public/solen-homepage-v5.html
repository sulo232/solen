Palette — derived from Ocean Blue, not Hims

#1D5DC4 · --brand Ocean Blue — banner, "Schöner", logo dot, "Journal" label, final CTA bg
#F4F2ED · --paper — warm-balanced substrate. RGB (244, 242, 237) — B is only 7 less than R (Hims cream is 18 less). Whisper warmth, not yellow.
#EDF2FA · --ice brand-subtle — harmonic tint (it IS the brand desaturated). Used for "Zuletzt angesehen" (your stuff).
#FFFFFF · --surface — listings + cards float pure-white for transactional clarity
#ECE9E1 · --paper-deep — editorial section (slightly deeper paper, breaks rhythm)
#0C254E · --brand-deep navy — trust section. Same hue family as brand (217°), not random dark color.
Why this matches Ocean Blue: the paper has near-equal R/G/B (244/242/237 — 7-point spread) so it reads neutral, not warm. The ice is the brand color desaturated to 92% — it's literally made from the brand. The navy is the brand color darkened to 14% lightness — same hue, different value. Three bg tones, all from the same hue family. Hims used unrelated peach/cream — visually disconnected from a cool brand. This palette is internally consistent.
