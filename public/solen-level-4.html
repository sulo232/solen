Tier 1 (1 hour ship — i18n + 1 CSS file)

  • T1Copy rewrite: all section titles shifted from "what it is" → "how it helps you." Examples in mockup: Last-Minute heute → Heute. Sofort. · Lass dich verwöhnen → Verwöhn dich heute · Coiffeur-Salons → Schnitt. Farbe. Style. · Bewertungen → Echte Geschichten · Solen für dein Geschäft → Dein Studio. Online. In Minuten.
  • T1Section sub in serif italic: single editorial italic line under each h2 explaining "why this section matters" — adds personality without commitment to a full italic-font swap.
  • T1Hover-reveal "Termin buchen →" CTA inside each card photo. Slides up + fades in on hover. Mockup shows it visible on the LEFT card to demo what it looks like; hover the right cards to see the actual interaction.
  • T1Section h2 hover shifts text color from ink to brand green (subtle but communicates "this is also tappable").
  • T1Search row hover nudges the icon 2px right (currently inert).

Tier 2 (1 week — needs content team)

  • T2Bento Stylists section — broke uniform 3-circle grid into 1 BIG featured stylist hero card (with portrait, gradient overlay, "5 Termine heute" signal pill) + 2 smaller side cards stacked. Same content, designed instead of templated.
  • T2Curated close-up photos — swapped Unsplash interior-wides for detail compositions (scissors mid-cut, hands styling, color work). Same Unsplash source but selected for compositional cohesion. Production should commission salon-shot photography with the same brief: "no wide interiors, only detail moments."

Tier 3 (deferred — bigger lift)

  • T3Mega menu on category nav — hover Coiffeur (top of mockup) shows a preview panel with featured stylist photo + city stats + "Alle Coiffeure ansehen →" link. Signals "depth, this is a product worth exploring" per the video.
  • T3Scroll-spy section blur — section h2 enters viewport with a 1-frame blur(8px) → 0 transition. Subtle but reads as "level 4 detail."
  • T3Outdated vs Modern partner-page block (not in this mockup — would live on /fuer-salons): "Telefon klingelt durch" / "Vergessene Termine" / "Keine Übersicht" → "30 Sekunden. Solen."

Video's #1 insight applied

From the transcript at 7:58: "The trick with copy between level 3 and level 4 is to transform from what it does to how it helps." Every section title in this mockup follows that rule. Highest-impact, lowest-cost move in the whole audit.
