Heritage / Aesop lane. Deep walnut brown CTA (#4A352A) feels artisanal + premium, terracotta as accent for warmth. Logo dot in terracotta keeps brand recognition. This is the "luxury beauty heritage" feel — Aesop, Le Labo, Diptyque territory. Risk: brown CTAs may not pop against warm salon photos as decisively as B or A. Honest pushback / pick logic

  • A (blue + butter) — Safest, but you said the V2-D70 blue+yellow doesn't feel right. Refining doesn't change the lane (premium-finance), just polishes it. Pick this if "modern app booking utility" feel is the right lane.
  • B (terracotta-led) — My pick. Single-hue warm identity. Owns the wellness vertical. Terracotta is already YOUR brand color (s-accent in current tokens, logo dot in earlier era) — just promoted from accent to action. Memorable, cohesive, beauty-vertical native.
  • C (walnut) — Distinctive but risky. Brown CTAs against warm salon photos = lower click contrast. Better as ACCENT in a brand than as the primary action color. Pick this if "luxury heritage" is the lane and you're willing to test contrast carefully.
  • What stayed locked across all three: substrate #FAF8F2 (your B pick from substrate-variants), white cards, Bricolage Grotesque + Hanken Grotesk fonts, no atmosphere wash, NO emerald per your direction.
  • Where I'd push back further: the German promo banner copy "Sofort verfügbar — 320 Buchungen heute" is generic across all three. Brand differentiation matters more in COPY than in palette, eventually. Don't let palette do all the lifting.
