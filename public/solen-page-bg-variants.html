My recommendation: A (keep current grey)

Why: the grey bg gives the white search card natural separation without ANY shadow OR border. Adds visual depth + cards float effortlessly. Fresha goes pure white because they use a busy product (lots of imagery), not minimal chrome — they need calm. Solen is already minimal (mostly chrome + text), so the grey is the friend that gives subtle layering.

The council pushed for B (pure white = Fresha-exact) but that was BEFORE we knew you wanted Uber imagery integration (separate research in flight). If Solen gets richer imagery, pure white starts winning because images become the "depth" — bg can be flat.

Pick A if

  • Solen stays minimal-chrome heavy (most surfaces are text + form)
  • You want zero card-border-management work site-wide
  • The "calm grey" backdrop is part of Solen's brand voice

Pick B if

  • You commit to integrating Uber-style imagery (then pure white is the right canvas)
  • You want exact Fresha-match (council Q4 follow-up)
  • You accept the cost: every card surface needs an explicit border/shadow rule globally

Pick C if

  • You want the editorial "magazine cover hero" feel without committing the whole site to pure white
  • You're OK with the hard horizontal line at the white/grey boundary (could be a feature or a bug visually)

Pick A / B / C + I sweep + we look at the Uber imagery research when it lands.
