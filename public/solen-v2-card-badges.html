Pick A or B (or modify)

A = ship as-is, single availability badge top-left, no curation system. Simpler. Fewer dev hours. Less trust signal on cards.
B = full Q10+Q26 spec, curation top-left + availability bottom-left, 4 curation variants. More work (need /api/admin/badges/auto-assign wired + thresholds defined). Significantly stronger trust + discovery signal.
Hybrid possibilities: "B but only Solen Favorit + Top bewertet, drop Beliebt + Neu" — fewer thresholds to define. Or "A for v1, defer curation badges to v1.1."
