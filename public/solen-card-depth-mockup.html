My recommendation

C (text-only pad) keeps the V3 open-photo feel AND fixes the floating-text problem with one small move. Photo still bleeds to its corners; text gets a real surface that doesn't compete with the atmosphere wash.

D (hairline + lift) is the runner-up if you want the lightest touch — pure outline, no fill, atmosphere shows through.

B works but reads more "marketplace listing" (Airbnb / Booking.com) than "salon discovery" — probably too much containment for V3's airy substrate.

E (frost) is the most on-brand vocabulary (matches badges + cookie banner) but reads softer/less crisp than C; could be the right call if you want one consistent glass language across the whole UI.

A (current) loses on contrast — atmosphere is too saturated for naked text.
