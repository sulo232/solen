The X-ray — what real grids actually do

Pulled from live Pinterest iOS screens (Mobbin) + TikTok/Instagram patterns. The thing they share: the image does the work; text is the exception.

PINTEREST

Image-only by default

  • Organic pins: just a rounded image — no title, no handle
  • Save button (♡ / +) overlaid, top or bottom-right
  • ⋯ menu sits at a bottom corner
  • Video pins: duration badge 0:11 + mute
  • Text appears ONLY on rich/product pins: 1 line + price, or a small avatar + @name attribution row
TIKTOK / REELS

Everything on the image

  • Full-bleed thumbnail, no text below
  • View count + play overlaid bottom-left
  • Username sometimes overlaid, small
  • Nothing under the tile → zero cramping
INSTAGRAM EXPLORE

Pure image mosaic

  • Just images + a play / multi-image badge
  • All meta (title, creator) lives on the detail view
  • The grid is a wall of pictures
DRIBBBLE / BEHANCE

When you DO want meta

  • Image, then below: title (1 line)
  • + a single row: avatar + name … ♡ count · 👁 views
  • Meta is ONE horizontal row, never stacked
Diagnosis of the current Solen card: it stacks a long AI haircut title (1–2 lines) and a username as two separate text lines under every tile. None of the references do that — that double-stack is exactly the "cramped" feel. The variants below each remove the stack a different way.
