Head-to-head: where they differ

Dimension B (terracotta) D (deep clay + sage-olive)
Brand / CTA #D87352 — lighter, friendlier, candy-leaning #C15E45 — redder, more saturated, committed
Supporting dark #8B5A3C — mid umber #6B4633 — deeper umber (better for hover + dialog headers)
Spa & Wellness vertical Uses the brand color (no semantic differentiation) #5E6B4E — muted sage-olive accent (quiet wellness whisper)
Vibe Approachable, friendly, beauty-utility Calm, committed, "Swiss salon with excellent software"
Risk Light terracotta can read as "lifestyle-app pastel" — less grown-up Sage-olive accent adds 1 more color to the system to maintain — slight complexity increase
Photo harmony Good — lighter clay blends with warm skin tones (but CTAs may compete with hair-product reds in photos) Better — deeper clay has more contrast against typical salon-photo orange/wood tones
Cohesion Higher (single hue family) Slightly lower (3 hues but tuned to harmonize)
