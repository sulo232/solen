If I had to pick from these 12:

Pine teal #155547 — middle of the bridge row. It's both teal AND forest at once, which means it can pair with cream warmth without feeling cold (like pure teal might) and doesn't pick a fight with the available semantic green like pure forest could. It's the "Aesop apothecary" register that fits Solen's category without being on-the-nose green.

If you want max contrast/punch: Vibrant teal #1B6A73 — most striking against orange (Hollywood complementary).

If you want sophistication/heritage: British racing #1B5E40 — Aston-Martin tier, premium.
