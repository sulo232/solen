Form primitives

The form vocabulary every Solen surface uses. Six primitives × eight states × three sizes, composed against V3 brand-teal, white substrate, ITC Avant Garde Gothic Std body, Cooper BT display. All states are static demos — focus + active states are also live (tab through to feel motion).

Switch §F.1.6 · boolean

Switch (toggle)

For boolean settings only — "turn this feature on/off." Distinct from checkbox ("include in this list"). Track 44×24px, knob 20×20px. On-state uses brand-teal — accepted exception to §1 ≤ 4-instance rule for settings pages with many switches (per V2-D14).

Inline validation §F.1.8 · prevention

Inline validation

The prevention principle: catch errors before submit. 80% of form errors prevent themselves with live validation. On blur for sync rules. Per-keystroke for show-as-you-type validators (strength meter, char counter). Debounced 400ms for async (email available).

Anti-patterns §F.1.10 · banned

Anti-patterns

What Solen forms do not do. Each ban exists because the pattern fights either accessibility, autofill, or the §F.1 prevention principle. New surfaces don't reintroduce these — they compose §F.1 instead.

Banned · floating labels
E-Mail lara@example.ch
Why banned
  • Hurts screen readers + autofill (label-as-placeholder confusion)
  • Reads as decorative animation, not as a form field
  • Breaks in dense layouts when label needs to live above
Banned · pill text inputs
Why banned
  • Pills look tappable — text inputs are typeable
  • §7 anti-pattern: "buttons-waiting-to-be-pressed"
  • Use radius-lg (12px) for fields, radius-pill (99px) for buttons
Banned · required asterisk *
Why banned
  • Asterisk reads as a footnote marker
  • Use trailing red dot · OR mark optional fields with "Optional" tag
  • Required is the default visual; optional is the exception
Banned · clear field on error
User typed it. Preserve it. Let them edit.
Why banned
  • Steals user's work + forces re-typing
  • If a typo, they want to fix it — not retype the whole address
  • Always preserve the input, even on hard errors
