V2-D69 proposal — warm minimal lane

Base: #F9F8F6 warm pearl bg / #FFFFFF cards. Soft contrast lets cards "float" with shadow.

Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans single family at extreme weight contrast — 800 h1 (clamp 40-60px, -0.035em tracking) vs 500 body vs 400 microcopy.

Hero gradient: radial peach #FFF0E6 fading to bg, ellipse at 60%/30% — Fresha "glowing top wash" feel, fully contained to hero.

Search card: single white floating card, 24px radius, 3-layer soft drop shadow. Inputs share the card — divider lines, no individual outlines.

CTA: brand green #3B7A57 (matches your logo green). 16px radius, weight 700.

Cards: 22px radius (up from 18px), 3-layer soft wide drop shadow. White surface lifts off pearl bg.

Badges: "Heute frei" = pale mint #E5F2EA bg + brand green text. "-20%" = terracotta #D87352 bg + white text. "15 Min" = glassmorphic (white 55% + blur 12px) + brand-green dot. Heart = glassmorphic, no solid white circle.

Stylists ("Lass dich verwöhnen"): letter placeholders (E, M, S) replaced with real photo avatars. Each circle has soft drop shadow + floating white rating-pill with star.

Inspiration cards: 9:16 aspect, full image + bottom dark gradient overlay, white text pops cleanly. Replaces "muddy brown/grey gradient" approach.
