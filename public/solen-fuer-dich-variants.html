"Fuer dich" — section variants

Two things made the row feel unbalanced: (1) ragged label widths ("Treueprogramm" 13 chars next to "Gruppe" 6, and "Haar-Verlauf" wrapped to 2 lines), and (2) 3-4 tiles left-aligned in a scroll row leave a gap on the right -> left-weighted. Short labels fix (1); the layouts below fix (2). All use the real icons + real tokens.

My pick: Centered cluster + keep the "Fuer dich" heading + short labels (variant 3). Centering looks intentional whether a category shows 3 tiles (Spa) or 4 (Coiffeur) - it never left-weights and never over-spreads. Even-spread (variant 2) is the close runner-up if you want the row to span edge-to-edge, but it spreads too wide on 3-tile categories. For the Entdecken label I lean "Reels" or "Inspo" (it's the salon-video feed) - options at the bottom.
