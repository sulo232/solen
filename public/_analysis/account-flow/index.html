2Findings, ranked

2 real bugs, 4 information-architecture problems, then the missing-screen gaps in section 3.

BUGBare spinner on the Termine list/profile/bookings shows a centered spinner while loading, not a Skeleton. The design contract says loading = Skeleton matching the final layout.
BUGThe same word points at two different thingsThe hub notification row opens the inbox (/notifications); the settings row opens preferences (/settings/notifications). One label, two destinations, a coin-flip for the user.
IAContent and management are tangled in one hubThe hub mixes things you browse (appointments, favourites, looks) with things you configure (hair profile, forms, settings, help). Every benchmark keeps these apart. This is exactly what the Pinterest split we mocked fixes.Fresha, Uber and Airbnb all separate a hub-of-destinations from a settings sub-page.
IAIdentity split across two edit screensPhoto, name and bio in /profile/edit; email and phone in /settings/personal. Fresha bundles all identity fields (name, mobile, email, DOB, gender) into one edit-profile sheet.
IABeauty profile and hair profile overlapTwo "about your hair and beauty" surfaces (settings/beauty and the hair-profile hub row). Hair type is already editable in both places, one should be canonical.
IADestructive and session actions are splitSign-out sits on the hub; delete-account sits in settings. Fresha co-locates both at the bottom of settings, delete visually red and separated. Our B2 settings mockup already groups them together.

4Recommended order

The analysis validates the Pinterest split and then points at the money screens.

1Ship the split, profile = content tabs (saved, appointments, third), everything managerial behind the gear into the B2 settings list.Already mocked and approved-pending; all three benchmarks confirm the separation.
2Merge identity into one edit screen, photo + name + bio + email + phone on one page (Fresha's edit-profile pattern), killing the two-screen split.Removes the "two homes" problem in section 1.
3Fix the two bugs, Skeleton on the appointments list, and disambiguate the duplicate notification label (inbox vs preferences). Make one of beauty/hair profile canonical.Cheap, high-trust, no new backend.
4Add Payment methods, the single biggest gap; an account-level saved-cards screen (Airbnb's masked-list plus "Add" pattern is the cleanest to copy), reusing the Stripe setup we already have.Then Security (pw-last-changed, 2FA, manage Apple login) and verified/unverified chips.

Sources this turn: Playwright walk of 19 live account routes (all 200) plus a Mobbin benchmark of Fresha, Uber Eats and Airbnb (Treatwell not on Mobbin).
