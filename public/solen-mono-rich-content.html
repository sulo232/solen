Why B works for Solen specifically

Chrome stays clean — banner ink, header white, hero white, type ink, CTAs orange. That's the "Uber discipline" you wanted.

Content gets to be alive — Amplemarket-style decorative category tiles (pink/brown/orange-red) make the Für dich section feel rich without painting the chrome. Salon cards earn real signal colors (green available, red urgent, blue verified) instead of vague grey badges. BentoBusiness dashboard uses real up/down/info chips like a real product (green up, red down, blue info) instead of decorative chrome.

What this solves:
  • The "too monochrome / too tech-startup" risk from pure mono — content does the warmth
  • The "too yellow / too warm chrome" complaint — chrome is neutral
  • The "where does teal live" question — gone, status colors don't need brand-defined accents
  • The 60-30-10 discipline — chrome IS 60-30-10, content colors are signal/decorative (outside the rule)

Ship cost: larger than previous swaps. Need to: (1) add real status colors to salon cards (~6 components), (2) re-render Für dich tiles with colored bgs (1 component, easy), (3) build the dashboard data chips into BentoBusiness (1 component). ~2 hours of surgical edits across the homepage.
