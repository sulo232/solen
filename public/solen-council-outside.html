Vibe: Swiss watchmaking, alpine sunlight, IWC / Rolex heritage. Calm-premium not promotional.

PRO: Swiss DNA on the nose (watch + alpine); pairs warmly with orange (both warm family but different lightness/sat); premium without being neon

CON: yellow + orange share warm hue family → button doesn't "pop" against orange banner. Risk that the banner + button blur into a single warm zone instead of branded+action contrast.

Quick honest read of the 3:

Chartreuse is brave but reads sport/club. If Solen's audience is broad Swiss-DE (25-55 mass-market salon bookers), this is risky. If Solen wants Gen-Z + Berlin-ish, it works.

Violet is the most defensible of the three semantically (no collision with any locked color). But the design-taste-frontend skill explicitly bans "AI Purple/Blue" — Claude's #6B2FE3 is bold enough to escape that trap, but borderline. Premium-violet works at premium prices; for booking salons, may feel mismatched.

Gold has the strongest concept (Swiss heritage) but the weakest contrast — yellow + orange = warm zone, button doesn't pop. Unless you go MUCH darker on the gold (deep ochre, mustard-bronze), it'll fight the banner.

Honest pushback: none of these 3 feels obviously right to me. Violet is technically most correct, but breaks the "Swiss beauty/wellness" register. Chartreuse is too edgy. Gold doesn't contrast. My instinct after seeing all rounds is the safest path is back to warm ink #1F1612 — even though it's not "exciting," it's the only color that lets the orange banner do its job without competing.
