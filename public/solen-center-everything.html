The honest tradeoffs

  • Wins: the "Fuer dich" row is no longer the lone centered thing - everything is symmetric, so it stops clashing. Calm, premium, distinct.
  • The seam (red box): the salon grid + its "4 Salons / sort" row are a left/right structure - they don't center. So centered chrome sits on top of a left-aligned grid. The "weird" feeling can just move down here.
  • Header nav: category tabs in the header get ~248px of a 390px screen (~2 visible) and lose prominence vs a full row below. Bell has to move into the menu.
  • Scroll origin: centered filter/category scroll rows hide where the list "starts" - users expect scrollers to begin at the left edge.
  • App consistency: homepage + salon pages are left-aligned; this page would speak a different alignment language.

My lean: centering everything trades one small misalignment for a bigger structural seam at the grid. If "Fuer dich" still feels weird left-aligned, I'd bet the issue is the section itself (what it is / where it sits), not the alignment - worth exploring before reflowing the whole page. But this is your call - the compare is right here.
