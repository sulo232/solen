My pick

My read: D-Teal (orange + deep teal). The orange/teal pairing is iconic for beauty (vintage Schwarzkopf, modern Charlotte Tilbury accents, retro Pan Am posters), it's distinctive without being kitsch, and the sea-glass section tint gives the warm illustrations a calm cool stage to land against. D-Navy is the runner-up — more conservative editorial (Hermès / Diptyque vibe). Pick D-Forest if you want to preserve the most existing brand equity. Skip D-Sage (too granola) and D-Charcoal (too clinical for beauty).
