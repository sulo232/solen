Read this with the frames

  • A → B → C is your full idea as one flow: browse curated rails, tap the search bar to open the Airbnb where/what/when panel, submit into a map view.
  • The cost of A-B-C: three result states to build + maintain (rails, panel, map), the map is a big unbuilt dependency, "Empfohlen/Favoriten/Bald frei" each need DB/auth/slot data, and the category page stops being a clean SEO results list.
  • D (recommended): keeps the category page a fast filterable grid (Fresha-faithful, SEO-safe), adds one "Beliebt in Coiffeur" rail for discovery flavor, filters act on the grid (no empty-rail confusion). The full rails experience moves to the homepage; the Airbnb panel + map become a later, separate phase once booking + map infra are solid.
