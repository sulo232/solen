My recommendation

V2 · Light body (Hanken 300) — start here

Hanken 300 gives the Uber-modern contrast you described (display 900 ↔ body 300 = 3× weight ratio) while staying readable for German compound words on small viewports. V3 (Hanken 200) goes further but risks letter strokes thinning out on mobile retina; we can step up to it later if V2 feels too safe.

V4 (single family Inter Tight everywhere) is the closest match to Uber Move's actual brand. Trade-off: loses Hanken's slightly warmer body character. Considered if you want "Tap2/Linear maxxed."

V5 (heavy data emphasis) is a smaller tweak — addresses "where is the eye supposed to go" by making the booking time visually carry the most weight on each card. Could be additive on top of V2 once weight is right.

Implementation cost of V2: 5 lines changed (globals.css font-weight loader + Hero sub + SectionHeader + SalonCard + FeaturedStylists). ~3 minutes.
