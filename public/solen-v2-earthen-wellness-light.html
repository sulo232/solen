What changed vs the original Earthen Wellness mockup

1. Primary brand: moss-deep #2D3F33 → moss-soft #5C7765. Same hue family, ~25% lighter value. Search submit + B2B border use the lighter version. Old moss demoted to accent (Spa cat letter, hover state).

2. B2B panel anatomy: was dark-moss bg + cream text + white floating stat. Now cream bg + thin moss-soft border + ink text + butter-yellow stat-card. Dropping the dark panel removes ~25% of darkness from the page.

3. Atmosphere wash alphas: 0.45-0.55 → 0.18-0.32. Color hint preserved, but cream bg now dominates ~85% of viewport (was ~65%).

4. Butter #F2D77B introduced: bright accent in stat-card + a soft blob (alpha 0.18). Adds visual energy without breaking earth-tone family. Optional — could remove if too playful.

5. Spa cat colorway: bg moss #2D3F33 + letter cream → bg moss-pale #C8D4C9 + letter moss-deep #2D3F33. Now consistent lightness with the other 3 cats.

What didn't change: hue family (still earth-tones), structure (same anatomy), typography (Peace Sans + Open Sauce One), heart icon, sage accent role, bone alt-surface role.
