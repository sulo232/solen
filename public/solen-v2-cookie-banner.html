Cookie consent

Legal-baseline GDPR / Swiss DSG (Datenschutzgesetz) consent banner. Non-negotiable for DACH market launch. Necessary cookies always on (auth session, language). Analytics + marketing require explicit opt-in. Persistence via localStorage (cookies-for-cookie-consent = chicken-and-egg). 12-month expiry — banner re-shows after.

Datenschutz

Cookie-Einstellungen

Wir nutzen Cookies und ähnliche Technologien, um Solen zu betreiben und zu verbessern. Notwendige Cookies sind immer aktiv. Du entscheidest, ob wir auch Analyse + Marketing-Cookies setzen dürfen.

Notwendig
Auth-Session, Sprachpräferenz, dieser Cookie-Banner selbst. Immer aktiv (legitime Interessen).
Analyse
Anonyme Nutzungsstatistiken via PostHog — hilft uns zu verstehen, welche Salons gefunden werden und wo Buchungen abbrechen.
Marketing
Konversions-Tracking + Retargeting (Meta, Google) — damit wir relevante Anzeigen ausspielen und neue Kund:innen erreichen.

Du kannst deine Einstellungen jederzeit über den Footer-Link "Cookie-Einstellungen" ändern. Mehr in unserer Datenschutzerklärung.

Anti-patterns §F.8.8 · banned

Anti-patterns

What Solen cookie consent does not do.

Banned · GDPR
Pre-checked analytics / marketing toggles
Default state is OFF. User opts in. Per GDPR Art. 7 + Swiss DSG, consent must be active.
Banned · dark pattern
"Reject all" hidden / harder to find than "Accept all"
Both buttons must be EQUALLY prominent. Per GDPR / EDPB clarification — buried "reject" violates active-consent principle.
Banned
Auto-accept on continued browsing
Active consent only. Scrolling, clicking elsewhere, etc. does NOT count as accepting.
Banned
Cookie banner blocks page interaction
Page must be fully usable while banner is visible. Cookies are a layer above content, not a modal.
Banned
Persisting consent in cookies
Chicken-and-egg. Use localStorage. Cookie banner CANNOT use cookies to persist its own consent decision.
Banned · DSG
Loading analytics before consent given
PostHog / Meta / Google scripts must NOT mount until consent.analytics === true. Even "anonymous" tracking needs consent in DACH.
