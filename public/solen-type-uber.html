My recommendation: B (Geist Sans)

Closest free analog to Uber Move + already on the Vercel platform → no font-loading drama. Single family for the whole site (drops the 2-stack overhead — Inter Tight + Hanken Grotesk → just Geist). Headings sit at 700 instead of 800 = solves "too heavy" without dropping below readable. Body sits at 400 instead of 300 = restores natural-feeling reading weight (the 300 was a Hanken-specific compensation that read thin on lots of surfaces).

What changes in the codebase if you pick B:

  • 1 file: `tailwind.config.js` — swap font families: display + heading + body all → `['Geist', ...]`
  • 1 file: `app/[locale]/layout.tsx` (or root) — add Geist font import via `next/font/google`
  • ~30 files: every `font-extrabold` (800) → `font-bold` (700); every `font-light` (300) → `font-normal` (400). Mechanical sed sweep.
  • 1 file: `_design-system/LOCKFILE.md` §2 — update the type table.
  • Risk: low. Geist Sans renders cleanly on all Latin scripts (German umlauts, French accents, Italian).

If you pick C (Manrope) instead:

Same scope of changes. Manrope is softer, more humanist — closer to current Hanken in attitude. Good for "warm + welcoming" brand voice. Geist is sharper + more product-tech.

If you pick A (keep current):

Just lighten weights across the board: 800 → 600, 700 → 500. ~30-file mechanical sed sweep, no font changes. Cheapest. Solves "too heavy" without identity shift.

Look at the 3 panels side-by-side, pick the one that feels closest to your Uber reference, then I execute.
