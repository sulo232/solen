What the best apps do — the rules

  • One hero, not five. Each app picks a single thing you glance at. Ride apps → the ETA ("14 minutes left"). Queue apps → the position ("#1"). Pickup → the code ("WGN551").
  • Details go in a compact grid, never a vertical list. Boarding passes pack Gate / Seat / Status into tiny side-by-side cells. American · Qantas · Emirates — this is the fix for "feels tacked on."
  • Cancel is demoted. A quiet text link or row, never a loud filled button. "Leave the queue" Waymo · "Cancel request" Freenow · "Cancel ride" Bolt.
  • Progress is often drawn. A stepper or position bar makes "the line is moving" tangible. Wonder · 7-Eleven · Honest Greens · Greg's slider.
  • No map for us. Ride/food apps show a map because the thing is moving — your barber isn't. Skip it.
