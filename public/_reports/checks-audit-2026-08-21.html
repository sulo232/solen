The checks, audited

You said the checks are not firing. They are. The problem turned out to be the opposite one, and it cost you a real word on a real screen today.

What was actually wrong

One defect, repeated across the whole set.

They could not tell a change from a creation

A check looks at what an edit puts IN and refuses if it sees something banned. Almost none of them looked at what the edit takes OUT. So changing one word inside a line that already had the banned thing looked exactly like typing it fresh, and the check said no.

That is how it surfaced. The German sweep had to change one word inside a heading whose styling was already banned. The check refused, the only way past was to switch it off, and the agent correctly refused to do that. So the word never changed, and that one screen kept saying Stores while the rest of your product was fixed.

Why ten of them are right to be blind

“It was already like that” is not always a defence

It is a fine answer about a corner radius. It is no answer at all about money moving out of your Stripe account, a password sitting in a web address, an unsafe database query, a legally required price, or fabricated data in production. For those, the harm is the code existing at all, not who typed it.

So those ten stay exactly as they are, and the shared piece names them so nobody wires the exemption into them later.

My own fix had a hole, twice

The decoy

Ten checks got the fix, and someone who did not write it broke all ten within the hour. Some rules police a pairing, a wrong radius next to a card shadow. Mine asked whether each of the two things existed anywhere, separately. So planting two harmless decoy lines bought a genuinely new violation for free. Plain readable code, no cleverness. Closed.

The tool that lied

One round reported 38 checks as never firing, including three I had personally made fire an hour earlier. The tool was reading those files the wrong way and finding nothing in any of them. Thrown out and redone.

A check I accused wrongly

One of the last two turned out to be fine. It already reads the file properly and its own notes record it learning that lesson three days ago. My test made it look guilty because the thing I added was a genuine violation, not a reword.

Something else this turned up

Three checks existed in two copies, three weeks apart

Which copy runs depends on which folder a session starts in. The copies on this branch were the old ones. Had the branch merged as it stood, they would have overwritten today's fixes and quietly undone them, and nothing would have said a word. Synced.
