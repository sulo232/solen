What is protecting you

You said you do not know what these rules are. Here is every one of them, in one sentence each, saying what mistake it stops and what you or a customer would see if it shipped.

No file names, because you cannot act on one. Read the money and customer-data sections if you only read two. The rest are mostly aimed at me.

Money

Three rules stand between a mistake and your Stripe account. 3 in total.

Stops a database change that touches money, an appointment slot, or a booking's status from skipping the check that it actually took effect, the exact gap that once let a card get charged without re-confirming the booking was still valid.

Stops a brand-new payment, voucher, or gift-card endpoint from being built with no login check at all, the exact hole that once let anyone anonymously mint free voucher codes or reset a voucher's balance.

Keeps the most expensive AI model reserved for genuine judgment calls instead of routine build work, so money isn't spent on the costliest option for a job a cheaper one could do.

Customer data and security

These are the ones that matter most. Each stops a specific way a customer's private information could leak or be changed by the wrong person. 14 in total.

Stops a customer's own typed words from being fed straight into an AI request with nothing wrapping them, which is how a customer could sneak hidden instructions into what the AI writes back.

Stops a new file upload or delete route with elevated storage access from skipping an ownership check, the exact gap that could let one customer or salon delete or read another's photos.

Stops a database change from inventing a real fact about a real salon, like claiming it's wheelchair-accessible or LGBTQ+ friendly, from a random-number trick instead of an actual answer someone gave.

Same protection as the sibling rule: stops any future database change from assigning a real fact about a real business using randomness instead of a genuine source.
