Six decisions

Each one written out properly: what it is in your product, why it is stuck, and what happens either way. You can answer any of them with a letter.

1

The discount box on the payment screen

12 days

What it is. When someone pays, there is one box to type a code into. Today that box is for GIFT VOUCHERS only. It shows for every salon, including salons that have never issued a voucher, so most people who type a code into it get an error.

Why it is stuck. Two different features share one box, and nobody decided which one it is.

AHide the box unless that salon actually has a voucher with money on it. Smallest change, and the box stops lying.
BBuild a real promo-code box, so a salon can run its own discounts. Bigger, and it is a feature you have not asked for yet.

Every week this sits, customers keep hitting a code box that cannot work for them.

2

Rating a salon you never booked

12 days

What it is. Right now one person can leave one rating per salon without ever having an appointment there. I built it that way and you never asked for it.

Why it is stuck. I made a product decision that was not mine to make, and flagged it instead of guessing again.

AKeep it. More ratings, faster. Anyone can rate anyone, including competitors.
BOnly people with a real completed booking can rate. Slower, and every star is earned.

Whatever is live now is what launches if nobody decides.

3

What I do when I run out of room mid-job

12 days

What it is. On a long job my memory of the conversation fills up. I can either compress it myself and carry on alone, or stop and hand back to you.

Why it is stuck. Carrying on alone risks me forgetting something you said. Stopping costs you a turn every time.

AKeep going on my own. Fewer interruptions, small risk I drop something.
BStop and check in. Nothing gets dropped, you get pulled in more often.

Today I stop, which is why long jobs keep bouncing back to you.

4

Two button sizes on the salon page

7 days

What it is. The Buchen button on a salon page is 13px on a phone. Your own floor says a button is never below 13, so it is sitting exactly on the line. 14 is the normal size everywhere else.

Why it is stuck. It may be deliberate for density on that row. A mockup was built and you never picked.

ABump both to 14, so every button in the product matches.
BLeave 13 on that row, because it is a dense list and 14 crowds it.

Tiny either way. It is on this list because it is the last thing blocking that punchlist from closing.

5

The German word for “Salon”

21 days

What it is. Everywhere the product says “Salon” in German. It is a normal German word, so it is not a translation problem, it is a naming choice. Store, Studio, Betrieb and Salon all read differently to a Swiss customer.

Why it is stuck. This is brand voice, not a rule I can derive. Picking it myself would put my taste in your product name.

AKeep Salon.
BGive me the word you want and I sweep it everywhere in one pass.

It appears on nearly every screen, so the longer it waits the bigger the eventual sweep.

6

How I write to you

2 days

What it is. Two councils read all 22 of our exchanges and found why my messages do not land: they always open with something from my workshop instead of the thing you asked about. New rules are written and not switched on.

Why it is stuck. Changing how I talk to you is not a change I should make without you seeing it.

ASwitch them on.
BRead the page first, then decide.

Every message until then opens the way you have been objecting to.

Three more, not your call

These are stuck on something other than a decision. Listed so you know they are not waiting on you.

A second city in the data

The Cities row on the homepage shows exactly one tile, because all 20 test salons are in Basel. That row cannot be designed until a second city exists.

Needs seed data, not a decision.

Buying a product end to end

Nobody can complete a real product purchase in test mode, because no salon is set up to take online payment yet.

Needs a Stripe connection, not a decision.

Distance and “near you”

Distances and the nearby row cannot be real until location is on and real salons are seeded.

Needs both were deferred by you earlier.

Age is how long since the question was written down. Answering one with just its number and a letter is enough.
