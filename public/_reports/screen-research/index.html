The panes above show 9.0px rather than 10.0px because this page's subtitle line height is 16px against the component's rendered 17px. The defect is the same, the table carries the real number.

Why it happens. The row centres everything vertically. On a one line row that looks perfect and always will. Add a second line and the block gets taller, its middle drops by half a line, and the icon and arrow follow it down while the title stays put. Ten pixels is that half line.

Why I got it wrong. I checked the settings screen, which is built entirely from one line rows. The bug is invisible there by construction. I measured the case that passes and told you the case you were looking at was fine.

The ten things to change

Ranked by how fast you would notice them opening the product yourself.

  1. List rows: get the icon, text and arrow onto one lineThe thing above. Fix it once in a shared row component instead of in five places.
  2. One salon has to look like one salonThe same salon renders as four different objects depending on the screen. Home gives a photo card. Saved gives a three photo collage at a different shape, a different name size, and no price. Search gives a third thing. Twelve more files draw their own.
  3. There is no Button component, and it showsEvery button is drawn by hand where it sits. The black button is written 443 times and its corner rounding is spelled twenty different ways.
  4. Search results carry no bookable time, and we already have the dataOur card states a time as a sentence. Fresha gives three tappable time chips. We fetch the slots and throw them away.
  5. The salon page book bar is an empty buttonOne black pill and nothing else. Every comparable app puts a fact next to the button.
  6. Empty states read as unfinishedNo services or no reviews prints one grey sentence. Our own rule says that slot takes a headline, a subline, a button and an icon. Right now most seed salons hit this.
  7. Desktop home opens with no salons on screenOn a laptop the first screen is a headline and a search bar. No salon, no photo, no category, until you scroll.
  8. The heading font is on things that are not headingsOur display font and its letter tightening apply to every heading tag at any size, including 12 and 14 pixel labels where two tightening operations stack and the effect is invisible.
  9. The search heading and the results do not share a left edgeThe results count line starts 16px from the edge, the grid below starts 12px. Four pixels off on the busiest page we have.
  10. Motion: remove one thing, add nothing yetThe desktop sidebar collapses as you scroll and leaves a naked button. Nothing in 781 screens does this.

Your five questions, answered

Font

Keep Inter. Keep Inter Tight, but only at 24px and up. I measured both faces: identical x-height, cap height, ascent and descent. Inter Tight is not a display font, it is the same font 8.7 percent narrower. That saving is worth under 2 pixels on a card title and 26 pixels on a 28px headline, which is exactly where a long German salon name overflows. Do not change typeface.

Components

Build three things that do not exist, then stop drawing screens by hand. A Button, a settings Row, and a page container. Our worst files are worst in one specific way: the search page is 2412 lines and uses 3 of our components. The profile page is 463 lines and uses 2. The home page composes 11 and owns one div of its own. That is the model.

Grid

We ship 62 different container widths against a contract that locks 2. That is the whole finding.

Alignment

Answered at the top, with numbers.

Motion

Nothing gets added yet. One thing gets removed. Adding motion to screens that are structurally inconsistent just makes the inconsistency move.

What I could not get

Mobbin has zero screens for SimplyBook.me, Booksy, Treatwell, Vagaro, StyleSeat, Squire and Mindbody. I searched each by name across every agent. The only beauty booking products actually in the corpus are Fresha and Square Go.

So this research is strong on booking and marketplace mechanics generally, and thin on our exact trade. Every beauty specific conclusion says so in its own file. If SimplyBook matters to you it needs a live capture rather than a corpus search, and I need one line from you confirming that is the product you meant.

Nothing has been changed. This is research and measurement only. The alignment bug is written down, not fixed, because the first thing off this list gets a mockup before I touch a real file. Tell me which one to start with, or I start at number 1 since it is the one you already caught.
