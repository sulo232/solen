The one that was doing nothing

And it is a rule about a complaint you made four times in a row.

What it is meant to stop. You say "overhaul this screen, I want it nothing like it is now", and you get back the same rows, in the same order, with new paint on them. That happened to you four rounds running on the profile page on 3 August.

Why it stopped working. It compares a proposed design against your screen as it actually ships. When your product was translated into four languages, the screen stopped containing words: its labels became internal codes. A real design never contains those codes, so the comparison always came out at zero and the rule could never object to anything, on any input, ever.

The part that should worry you more. It was reporting itself as fully working the whole time, because its own test hands it a list of real words instead of making it look at the real screen. A rule that reports green while being blind is worse than no rule, because it makes everything look guarded.

Fixed. It now looks each code up in your language files. Tested against your own 3 August case: the repaint is refused, the same repaint written in German is refused, a genuine redesign passes, and the same rows pass when you only asked for a small tweak.
