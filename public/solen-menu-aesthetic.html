The hamburger-menu aesthetic, applied

The menu's signature: soft surface tiles with a faint resting shadow that lifts on hover, ink-circle icons, bold title / grey subtitle. Shown here on the header chrome + shortcut tiles + a content row. The HOVER-tagged ones are forced into their lifted state so you can see it.

The lift only appears on hover (desktop) or press (mobile); at rest it's the faint shadow. This is the menu's exact recipe (rounded-16 surface, 0 1px 3px → 0 6px 18px on lift).
