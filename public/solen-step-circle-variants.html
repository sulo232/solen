My recommendation: A (ink)

The step circles are the highest-emphasis visual anchor in the section — they're what your eye lands on. Ink-fill says "these are the steps, take them seriously." Accent-pale lightens the visual weight but says "these are friendly little markers, the copy is what matters."

For a B2B sales page, ink emphasis converts better — it reads as confident + commercial. The accent-pale read is softer + more SaaS-trial-y. Solen is a marketplace selling to salon owners; you want the "we're serious about your business" read.

Also: the rest of the page already uses ink for primary buttons (LOCKFILE §0.2). Ink circles maintain visual consistency. Accent-pale would be the only royal-blue surface on the whole page = floating accent moment with no echo.

Pick B only if you want the section to feel lighter overall (e.g. if testing on small business owners shows ink reads as "intimidating corporate" vs "premium").
