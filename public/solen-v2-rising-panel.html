What changed (just one thing):

A "feed-zone" panel wraps everything BELOW the hero. The panel:
• Rounded TOP corners only — `28px` mobile / `40px` desktop
• Subtle white-ish glass tint (white/85 + blur 8) so wash still shows through softly
• Soft upward shadow `0 -12px 32px rgba(4, 51, 56, 0.06)` — emphasizes the "rising" effect
• `margin-top: -24px / -32px` slightly overlaps the hero's bottom — makes the panel feel like it's coming up from below

Hero zone above: heavy atmosphere wash visible.
Feed zone below: same wash but muted by the panel's white tint, so feed sections sit on a calmer surface.

Section frames inside the feed zone got a SLIGHTLY different glass alpha (white/30 instead of white/40) so they read as sections-on-panel, not floating-on-wash.

Everything else V3 stays untouched.
