My recommendation top of section 3

Combo 2 · Inter Tight 900 + Scale B (-10%)

Inter Tight at weight 900 is closest to what Tap2 ships — modern, confident, no personality the user has to negotiate with. Scale B (-10%) gives breathing room on small viewports without losing the "premium" feel of large hero copy. Tight tracking (-0.035em) is the modern code: it makes heavy weights feel intentional instead of just chunky.

Trade-off vs current Bricolage: lose some warmth/character. Bricolage has variable optical sizing + slight humanist curves that read "friendly." If Solen wants more "Tap2-modern" than "Airbnb-warm", swap. If we want warmth back, Combo 3 (Neue Montreal 700) is the compromise.
