What changed and why

  • Hero typeface: chunky display Peace Sans → editorial Instrument Serif italic accent. Skill rejects "category-reflex" big-display, prefers personality through serif italic.
  • Surfaces: heavy-glass FeedZone + frosted card pills → flat surfaces with hairline borders. Skill bans glassmorphism as default ("rare and purposeful, or nothing").
  • Salon listings: nested-card grid (photo block + frosted text pill) → flowing editorial list (small square photo + name + meta line + availability). Skill bans nested cards categorically.
  • Search: opaque white card with rounded edges + saturated CTA pill → inline form between hairline rules + bordered text-button CTA. Skill says "don't wrap everything in a container" and the CTA shouldn't be the loudest object on the page.
  • Color space: hex tokens (#043338, #FFF1DD) → OKLCH with chroma-tinted neutrals (oklch(0.985 0.005 95)). One accent (teal, <10% surface area).
  • Copy: em dashes used freely → em dashes removed. Lede sentence added. "Schweiz · Salons, Spas, Studios" replaces "Beauty in der Schweiz" (more concrete).
  • Atmosphere wash: 4-color radial gradient ambient → flat tinted-neutral background. Skill prefers commitment to one color strategy (Restrained for product register).
  • Card category colorways: Combo Z / G / A / I named-pair palette → muted OKLCH-shifted neutrals per category. Less brand identity, more editorial restraint.
