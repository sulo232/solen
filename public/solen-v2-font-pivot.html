Watch in the specimen: Anton has only one weight (400). Bold/medium/light don't exist. Things to look at:
  • The salon card name at 14-16px — Anton was made for billboards, see how it reads on a 200px-wide card
  • The italic accent in "Guten Morgen, Sulo" — v2 has Instrument Serif (real italic moment), V1 falls back to Figtree italic (a body-font italic, less distinctive)
  • The numerics "ab CHF 85" — v2 has JetBrains Mono (distinct mono character), V1 uses Figtree tabular nums (same body family)
  • The section header "Coiffeur in Basel" — Anton at 28px on cream substrate is the loudest comparison point. Decide which energy you want.
