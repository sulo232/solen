How others handle the background

Same banner, same logo, same hero — only the substrate changes. Four patterns, with the brand inspiration + tradeoff for each.

A · Current

Pure white everywhere

Inspired by: Linear, Vercel, Stripe Docs. The "design system everywhere" school — the canvas is infinite and disappears.

Recommendation

B (Hims-style warm cream) if Solen leans wellness. C (cool blue tint) if Solen leans tech utility.

The reason this isn't an obvious A or D pick: Solen is in the wellness/beauty marketplace category. Pure white (A) is what tech utilities use — it's hostile to "I'm spending money on self-care." Card-on-grey (D) is what Booking.com uses — works for hotels, feels clinical for facials. The interesting choice is between warm (B) and cool-tinted (C) tonal alternation.

  • Warm (B) wins if you want Solen to feel personal, like Glossier-meets-Hims — the booking flow as a self-care ritual. Risk: warm+cobalt is a tension pairing; works in moderation but can clash if you push the cream too saturated.
  • Cool tint (C) wins if you want Solen to feel modern + Swiss + utility-first — Booking-style ease but with brand cohesion. Risk: feels like every other 2025 SaaS landing page.
  • Pure white (A) only works if you're committed to the "Linear of beauty booking" identity — minimal, fast, no decoration. Cards must do all the heavy lifting.

First-principles tradeoff: tonal alternation buys you rhythm + emotional warmth, but costs you the brand-cobalt's "loud accent" energy (cobalt against pure white is its sharpest). It's a choice between BRAND PUNCH (A) and PAGE COMFORT (B/C).
