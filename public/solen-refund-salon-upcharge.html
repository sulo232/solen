Flow notes. Salon-initiated upcharge on a completed booking (Lane A, REFUND_APPEAL_PLAN.md §11). The customer must explicitly approve before the difference is charged to the saved card — no silent auto-approve (decisions D8 / D13). On decline or no response, no charge and the booking stays at its original total. Upcharge is capped at +50% of the original (§5). Charge runs off the SP-G2 saved card via Stripe. Notifications aren’t live yet (owner’s later piece) — the salon-side warn strip flags that the customer must be told to open the link. Tokens, type, radii and the ink-CTA / functional-accent rules follow CANON.md + LOCKFILE §1-§6. English copy = internal mockup convention.
