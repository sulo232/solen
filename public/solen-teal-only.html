Orange is GONE from the brand · teal #1A6B73 is now the only CTA color · 2 anchor options to compare

The trade

What you gain: a single coherent cool brand (less competing temperatures), teal becomes unmistakably "the Solen color," brand reads as wellness/spa/clinical-trust, easier to be consistent across the app.

What you lose: the warm energy orange brought, the visual harmony with the warm fakejolart illustrations (the brick walls + golden tungsten in CategoryPromos will now sit AGAINST a fully cool brand frame), and the punch — teal CTAs at 10% are less attention-grabbing than orange CTAs at 10% because teal is closer in lightness to the surrounding white substrate.

Honest pick: A (deeper-teal anchor) if you want full-teal commitment. B (ink anchor) if you want one cool primary + the BentoBusiness section visibly different from everything else.

One thing to think about

The fakejolart CategoryPromos illustrations were generated WARM — exposed brick walls, golden tungsten light, peach skin tones. With orange gone from the brand, those illustrations no longer "echo" anything in the system. They'll read as a contained warm island inside an otherwise cool brand.

Two ways to handle that: (1) live with the contained warmth — the illustrations stay as "imagery zone" while the rest of the chrome is cool, OR (2) regenerate the illustrations cooler (teal-leaning lighting, mint walls, cooler skin tones) so the whole system reads coherent. (1) is free, (2) is another ~3 hours of prompt iteration.
