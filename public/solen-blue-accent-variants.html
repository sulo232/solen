My recommendation

V2 · Eyebrow-only blue

Smallest blue footprint that still gives the brand a non-neutral chrome signature. Eyebrows are the lowest-stakes location: small font, tied to identity, not to interaction. If V2 feels too restrained after a week of living with it, you can step up to V3 in a single PR. If V2 reads "off" you can revert in a single PR.

Why not V3 right now: it flips the secondary CTA (outlined "Wie es funktioniert") which we treat as part of the chrome system. Three CTA-style changes in one week starts to look like uncommitted design direction.

Why not V4 (Tap2-exact) right now: V3-D146 locked "CTAs use s-ink" just yesterday. Flipping it 24h later is the kind of churn that erodes design system trust. If we like blue on CTAs after a month, ship it then with a deliberate "we tried both" framing.

Implementation cost of V2: 3 lines changed (one CSS rule for eyebrow color; nothing else). Reversible in seconds.
