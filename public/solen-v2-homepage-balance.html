Homepage — Whole-page balance audit

No restructure. No new sections. Just sizing tuned so each element's weight is proportional to where it sits — same way a face's eyes/mouth scale with head size, not absolute pixels. Five focused changes across the 10-section homepage. Most fix desktop where overlays/cards stayed mobile-sized while their containers grew.

BEFORE = current shipped state · AFTER = proposed · green = changed · total: ~12 className edits across 4 files

1. SalonCard — width on desktop (affects RecentlyViewed + LastMinute + Nearby + Coiffeur)

Cards on desktop sit at 180px — only 30px bigger than mobile. In a 1280px container with ~5 cards visible, the row reads as "scaled-up phone view," not desktop. Bumping to 200px + slight gap bump gives presence without restructure.

2. FeaturedStylists — avatar size on desktop

Stylist row sits between salon-card rows on the homepage. Salon cards on desktop become 200px (group 1); stylist avatars at 130px feel orphaned next to them — the row reads as "less important content" purely because of size, not by design intent. Bumping avatar to 150px keeps the row visually proportional.

4. Section vertical rhythm

<Section> currently sets mb-1 md:mb-2 — that's only 4–8px between sections. Combined with SectionFrame's pb-4, the total separation is ~20–24px which reads cramped against the colorful cards. Bumping to mb-2 md:mb-4 = 8–16px adds breathing without restructure.
