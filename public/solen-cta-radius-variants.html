CTA radius — which one?

The mockups used 13px rounded-rect. The shipped app (LOCKFILE btn) uses a full 99px pill on every other CTA. Same buttons at each radius:

My pick: Rounded 14px — but see the trade-off note
Pill99pxApp standard (LOCKFILE)
Soft20px
Rounded14pxMockup / modern (Stripe·Revolut·Apple)

The trade-off: for a tall full-width primary button, rounded-rect (~14px) is the current modern standard and is what the mockups use; the 99px pill reads more "bubbly" at this width but is what every other CTA in the app uses. So it's really: keep the app's pill for consistency, or switch the full-width primary CTAs to 14px for a more modern look (which would ideally be a system-wide change, not just these screens). Pick one.
