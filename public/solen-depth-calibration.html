Depth calibration — how far to pull back

Same salon cards, three depth levels. The video's core point that hits us: "visual overworking" (over-decorating) is the mid-level mistake; senior work is restraint. The heavy float I shipped is exactly that over-decoration. Pick the level and I roll it through the design system (one token cascades everywhere).

Now · heavy float (overworked)
What I shipped: a wide, spread shadow. Reads as “effect” rather than quiet lift — this is the over-engineering you flagged.
Subtle · restrained lift my pick
A light, tight shadow. The card still lifts so it’s not the “2018 flat” you disliked before, but the effect is quiet. This is the senior-restraint middle.
Hairline · flat (most minimal)
No shadow, a 1px outline. Cleanest and closest to how Fresha/Uber actually separate cards (hairlines + whitespace). Risk: can edge back toward “flat”.
If you pick Subtle or Hairline, the fix is one token change that cascades to every card I floated (homepage tiles, salon section cards, service rows). The rule I’d write into the system: depth is subtle + earned — resting cards get a quiet lift (or a hairline); only true overlays (the gallery, lightbox, sheets, dropdowns) keep a stronger shadow, because they sit on top of everything. No wide floats on resting content.
