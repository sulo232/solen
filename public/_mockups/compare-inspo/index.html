Every fix for Inspo, one after another

Today, nothing on Inspo's phone screen is big. Scroll through it now and the biggest word you will find is the little gray "Styles suchen..." placeholder text inside the search bar, at 14px. Everything else on the screen sits at roughly that same small size, so nothing tells your eye where to look first.

Below are five different ways to fix that, plus the page exactly as it is today for comparison. Every box is your real, live Inspo page, not a redraw. Scroll inside each box to see the real photo feed underneath. The only thing that changes between boxes is the one treatment named in its caption.

THE FIVE OPTIONS, IN ORDER BELOW

  1. A, smallest change. Add the title back. Nothing else moves.
  2. B, hierarchy first. Add the title, and also shrink the small filter tags so the screen reads in clear layers, not one flat block.
  3. C, real content higher. No title added. Tightens the button row above the photos so the real photo grid starts a little higher and sooner.
  4. D, less bold, the Airbnb way. No title added. The two bold words on the screen become not-bold. Shown for completeness; measured to not actually fix the missing-title problem, explained below.
  5. E, biggest change. Add both a title and a subtitle, in the same style your home page already uses for its own heading, so this page's heading matches everywhere else.
