Now

The heading tops out at 25px, and the four category buttons are all bold, even the three you are not standing on.

Fixed

The heading grows to 28px. Only the button you are actually on keeps its weight; the other three go back to plain text.

29% bold
What actually changes. Two things. The heading grows from 25px to 28px, which is your own floor for the one piece of text on a screen that is allowed to be the biggest thing on it. And the three category buttons you have not tapped stop being bold, matching the same rule this system already uses everywhere else: the one you are on stays bold, the rest stay plain. Before this, every button was bold regardless, which is most of why 44% of the text on this screen carried weight against a 30% ceiling.
About the photos, since I was told they were the real problem here. They are not, not on the page as it actually renders. I measured it fresh with your own check script against the live page: 43.18% of the first screen is real salon photography, comfortably over your 33% floor. The 0% figure I was handed looks like a screenshot caught during the half-second gray-loading flash before your salon list arrives, not the page a customer actually stands on. I am not adding a photo here because there is nothing here that needs one.
What this does not touch. The layout, the salon cards, the photos, the search bar, the category icons, the colors, the copy. Nothing here was invented: both fixes reuse sizes and weights this design system already locks elsewhere on the same screen.
