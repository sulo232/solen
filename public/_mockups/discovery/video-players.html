Discovery · Video player · 2 options

Same look, two ways to play it. A is the only legal option for the TikTok content we have today , TikTok's own embed (their player, their branding, a cookie-consent step). B is the native, Apple-style player with swipe-to-scrub , only possible for video we own (salon / user uploads), because we have the actual file.

A · TikTok content

TikTok official embed

Plays inline and you can scrub on their bar , but it's their UI and wordmark, with an engagement rail, "Watch on TikTok", and a cookie-consent step (their iframe). We can't restyle it. This is what's allowed for scraped TikTok looks.

B · Our own video

Native player (swipe to scrub)

Clean Solen chrome: play/pause, a real timeline you drag (or swipe the video left/right), elapsed / remaining time, fullscreen. Exactly the Apple feel. Requires the raw file, so it's for salon / user uploads, not TikTok.
