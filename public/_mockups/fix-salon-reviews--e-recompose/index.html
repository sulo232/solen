1. The reviews sit in a card now, matching every other section on the salon page. Right now this page is bare text on a white background with nothing grouping it. Your own salon page already wraps the exact same review content in a soft-edged white card with a thin border and a light shadow. This applies that same card here.

2. A real photo of the salon appears at the top. Read live from your own salon page's own photo. Not a placeholder, not invented, and not a downloaded reference photo.

3. The circular initials next to each name turn from blue to grey. The blue version is a leftover from before your avatar rule was written: your locked design system says avatars are always black-and-white, never colour-coded, and the reviewer photos on your salon page already follow that. This page's own reviewer initials never got the memo.

This page had it and the salon page did not: one more small mismatch between two screens showing the same thing.

5. Text stops being bold almost everywhere. Names, the filter chips, the "write a review" button outline, all currently bold. Only the "Bewertungen" heading at the top stays bold, since that is the one thing on the screen that should draw the eye first.

Why this arm is different from the other three you are also seeing today

The smallest version of this fix (already on disk, a separate file) nudges five text sizes by a few pixels and adds a photo. It works, but it treats the symptom: the page LOOKS inconsistent because it reads different sizes.

This version answers a different question: WHY does it look different. Your salon page and this reviews page show the exact same review data, but they are built from two separate files: your salon page uses the version your design team registered and approved, this page uses an older, hand-built copy that was never updated to match. That is the actual mechanism behind "some places use a background for gray, some places are weird, hierarchy is not correct, typography too": there are two authors for what should be one screen. This file fixes the SAME symptom the small version fixes, by closing the actual gap between the two files instead of resizing the old one.
