The 12 survivors of 217: before / after for your yes or no

Both IG files were evaluated principle by principle against your law and the real code: 143 already exist here, 35 collide with your locks, 27 are wrong for this product. These 12 are genuine gaps, ranked by value. Seven are visual (pairs below), five are behavior or process (honest text cards, nothing to draw). Full reasoning per principle: the verdict record in the design-system research folder.

ig1

Password rule is backwards: length beats symbols

Today the signup and reset forms demand 8 chars plus a capital letter plus a digit. That rejects long safe passphrases and happily accepts weak ones that tick the boxes; the server only enforces the 8-char floor anyway, so the checklist is pure friction on a conversion-critical screen. After: one entropy-scored bar plus one exact-cause line, floor unchanged.

ig8

White text on photos reads heavier than it is: compensate one weight down

Bright shapes on dark ground look thicker (the irradiation illusion). Category-card titles are bold white on photos today and read heavier than the same size elsewhere. After drops one weight step for optical balance, size unchanged.

ig9

Icon strokes matched to the text weight next to them

Lucide icons default to a 2px stroke everywhere. Next to a 12px label that reads chunky; next to a 24px header glyph it reads thin. After matches the stroke to the neighboring text stem so icon and label feel like one voice.

ig2 + ig3 + ig10 + ig11 + ig12

The five with nothing to draw (behavior and process fixes)

These are real gaps but a static picture cannot show them; here is exactly what changes.

ig2, phone field caret. Before: fixing a mistyped digit mid-number throws the cursor to the end on every keystroke (both checkout phone fields). After: the caret stays where you were editing. Pure behavior fix on the money path.
ig3, feed pagination. Before: while you scroll the Inspo feed, a newly published item shifts the pages and the next load repeats a card you already saw. After: cursor-based paging, no repeats. Backend change, no visual change.
ig10, dashboard summary grid. Real gap, but it belongs to the parked operator-home redesign decision (the traced.html candidate you have not reviewed yet); mocking it separately would fork that decision. Recommendation: decide it there, not here.
ig11, balance collapse-test. Adds one measured step to the taste-diagnosis procedure (collapse the layout to gray blocks, check weight distribution). Process addition, no UI.
ig12, progress-bar motion as status. The weakest of the twelve (the evaluation produced no concrete surface brief). Recommendation: skip now, revisit inside the motion round of the Taste Lab.
