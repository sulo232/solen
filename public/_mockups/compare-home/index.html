What's your call, and what's just catching this page up to one it already passes

  • Your call: whether the home page gets a headline at all. It was turned off for phones on August 1st on purpose ("why is homepage still that bro"). Options B and E turn some version of it back on. Options A, C, and D leave it off.
  • Your call: whether cards get wider. The current card width was a decision you approved in July on purpose, over a bigger option. Option C reopens that call; the other four leave it alone.
  • Your call: headline versus photo. On a fixed-height phone screen, a real headline and a bigger photo compete for the same room. Option E shows this plainly rather than pretending both fit for free.
  • Not your call, this one just matches the screen you already like: bringing bold text down from 37% to under 30%, and giving the page one real display anchor instead of ten identical section titles, are both just this screen catching up to the same ladder your own Cuts & Culture salon page already clears. Nothing new is being decided there, it's the same rule applied here that already applies everywhere else.
