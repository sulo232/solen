Motion - sent

When a message sends: a green ring pulses out, the disc spring-pops in, and the white checkmark draws its stroke. Deep solid green with a white tick, not a pale wash. Looping here so you feel the timing.

What changed

Split the single unbreakpointed two-pane into a mobile branch (lg:hidden, list OR thread with a blue ChevronLeft "Messages" back button) and the existing desktop two-pane (hidden lg:flex), and stopped auto-selecting the first conversation so mobile users land on the inbox list first. This is a direct port of the proven salon-side twin at dashboard/messages/page.tsx, reusing the same row, thread-header, and ChatWindow markup. Restyled to v2: white surfaces (cream dropped), blue is the interactivity signal (tappable rows, unread pill, active tab, composer icons, back link, send), sent bubbles stay ink as the single strong anchor, and on-load motion stages the list rows and bubbles in with a stagger.
