Dashboard selected state , three treatments, pick one

On the operator dashboard, "selected" is drawn three ways: gray-sunken (bundles, calendar), blue-tint (services, staff filters), and ink-fill (clients tabs, coiffeur CRM). The dashboard is the vibrant skin where blue is the primary language, so this is a real choice, not just "match the customer gray." Which one, everywhere?

B , blue tint fits the vibrant skin

Soft 10% blue fill + blue text. Reads as "active" in the dashboard's own primary colour (§12: active nav = accent blue). Most on-brand for the vibrant operator skin.
