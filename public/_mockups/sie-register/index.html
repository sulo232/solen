Formal is not uniformly longer, which is the surprise. Across 26 real strings the mean is +4.0% and the median +0.7%. Thirteen grew and thirteen shrank, because the possessive gets shorter in the formal register while the verb gets longer.

The cost is not spread out, it sits in one grammatical place: imperatives, where the formal register forces the pronoun back into the sentence. That is the worst case in the table at +39.3%, and it lands on buttons and step copy, which is exactly the fixed-height control the contract already warns about. So the risk is not the labels, it is the verbs.
