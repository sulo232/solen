The results page, with the phone screen spent on salons instead of on bar and label

Both copies below are the real Basel hair-salon results page, loaded live, scrollable like the real thing. Nothing is redrawn from scratch.

Today, after the search bar, you scroll past a filter pill, empty space, and a small label before you see a single salon photo. That is a real measured 212 pixels, about a quarter of a phone screen, spent on nothing you can book.

The fix does two things to the exact same real cards: it trims that empty space, and it makes each card meaningfully bigger. The size change is what actually moves the number. Trimming the space above only bought about 12 pixels. Making the card itself bigger is what turns this screen from mostly chrome into mostly salons.

Cost, not hidden: a bigger card means fewer price and distance labels fit on screen at once, so the share of bold text on the page goes up too (33.7% to 37.5%), moving further past the 30% ceiling this product holds itself to. That is a real trade this mockup makes, not one it fixes.
One correction before you read the numbers above: the brief for this task said this screen has 0% photo coverage today. Checked twice on the live page: it is 41.0%, and it already clears this product's own 33% minimum before any change here. The heading size (18px, this task does not touch it) is a real problem on this screen, it is just not this one.
