Four directions to pick between

These are four directions, not four things to build. Each is a static picture of the same product at 390 wide, rebuilt from a corpus of 84 screens across 44 apps rather than from what we ship today. Look at them, pick one, and only that one gets built.

The dashed line on each phone is the fold at 844px: above it is what a person sees before scrolling. The three numbers over each phone were measured here, one method for all four.

The defect all four exist to fix. Our home opens with a headline and a search bar filling the screen, and no salon appears until you scroll: 0 salons and 0 photographic pixels in the first screen, against a floor of four content units and roughly a third of it photographic. The current home was an input to these four, never a constraint, so bands are reordered, merged and dropped freely.

What all four share

Not one of these is a style preference. Each is a place where the corpus was one sided enough that arguing with it would need a reason we do not have.

0 of 34
home screens opened with a decorative hero photograph
Zero. Not a weak majority, an empty column. Across 34 home screens from the apps we would want to be compared to, none of them spends its first screen on a mood photograph or a headline over a picture. They open with a way in (a search field, a category row) or with the real inventory itself. Our home is currently the only screen in this comparison built on the opposite assumption, which is why every direction here starts with either a search field or a real salon, and why none of the four proposes a hero.

put search in the top zone
All four keep a search entry at the very top, differing only in how much room it is allowed to take.
16 of 19
put the photo above the text on a provider card
Every card in every direction is a photo with the words underneath it, never text beside a thumbnail.

One badge is the ceiling, so a card here shows a discount or nothing, never a stack of labels over the picture.

Three things to know before you judge these

  • The language split is not a design difference. A, C and D are written in English because of the dated rule that mockups are always in English; B is in German under that same gate's locale exemption, because its author judged that the copy itself was under review. Whichever direction wins, the built version is in all four locales. If you want the four to match before you decide, that is a copy swap and nothing else.
  • No number on these screens is fabricated, and none of them is live either. The photography is the real seeded salon imagery. The salon names, prices and ratings are illustrative stand-ins for seeded values, because a static picture is not wired to a query. Every from-price names the service it buys, which is a legal requirement here, not a style choice.
  • Motion is a separate deliverable and is deliberately absent. Nothing on this page animates, transitions or responds to a tap. These are pictures. How the chosen direction moves is researched and decided after you pick one.
