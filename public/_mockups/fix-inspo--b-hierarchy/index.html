One anchor, four clear steps

Right now Inspo has no headline at all on your phone. The biggest text on the whole screen is the 14px search placeholder. Below, one title comes back at the top, and the small style tags underneath the filter row (Defined Brows, Glossy Finish, and so on) step back a little so the screen reads in clear layers instead of one flat block of small text.

One number I was handed was wrong, so I checked it myself and did not build on it. I was told Inspo's photo feed measures 0.71% photographic on the first screen. I measured the live page myself, twice, on two different browser tabs: it actually measures 65.43%, well past the 33% floor. That matches what a sibling mockup found independently with a different tool. Inspo's photos are not the problem. Its missing headline is.

Now

No text on this screen reaches 28px. Everything sits at 12px or 14px, flat.

biggest text: 14px

With a real anchor and four steps

One title, one clear second tier, and the style tags step back to make room for it.

biggest text: 30px
30pxAnchor, new. The page's own title, weight 500 per the ladder's rule that emphasis is carried by size, not by piling on weight 600.
14pxSecond, unchanged. The search placeholder and the global category nav row at the top of every route.
12pxBody, unchanged. The five category tile labels and the salon name / price under each look in the feed.
11pxRecedes, changed. The nine quick style tags (Defined Brows, Glossy Finish...). They used to sit at the exact same size as the category labels above them with nothing telling them apart. Now they read as optional, not as a section.
What this fixes. Our own new 30px anchor over our own 14px body is 2.14x, past the 1.8x floor and the exact ratio the salon page you already like clears today. The screen now carries four distinct sizes (30 / 14 / 12 / 11); the densest 8px window still holds only three (11, 12, 14), inside the four-per-window ceiling.
What this does not touch. Photo area was already passing (65.43%, corrected number above) and this mechanism does not add or move a single photo. Weight share was already well under the 30% ceiling before this treatment (2 of 31 visible text elements at weight 600 or higher) and stays there after: the new anchor is weight 500, and the nine demoted chips move from 500 to 400, neither direction touches the bold count.
Where the title text comes from. It is the same title string the desktop header already shows today, pulled from the product's own translation file, identical across all four languages. Nothing invented.
A separate, smaller defect, not pictured here. The category tile row falls back to a bare tinted box with no icon and no initial when a category has zero cover photos, breaking the locked missing-photo fallback. Every tile that renders on /de/inspo today already has a cover photo, so there is nothing honest to show side by side for it without inventing an empty category, which the no-fabrication rule forbids. Confirmed in source, not fixed here.
