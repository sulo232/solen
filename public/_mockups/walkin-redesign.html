Walk-in band, redesign

Measured on the live homepage, then redesigned. The data is already real (GET /api/walkin/nearby), so this is purely a design problem, not a fabrication one.

What is actually wrong today (measured, not eyeballed):
1. It leads with the worst option. Cuts & Culture (70 to 98 min) sits first; Blade & Stone (35 to 49 min) is buried second. The band is not sorted by wait.
2. The hero number is bad news and vague. A 28 minute spread, set 20px/800 in green, is the loudest thing on the card.
3. Cards truncate. flex-basis 42% gives a 174px card, so "Elsaesserstrasse 10, Basel" cuts off.
4. A decorative "Live" pip. A coloured status dot, which the taste rules ban.
