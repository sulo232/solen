Correction on the numbers I was handed

The brief for this fix said the page measures at 0.71% photo coverage, a 14px headline, and a 1.02x size ratio, effectively no hierarchy at all. I checked this against the live page with the project's own measuring script and could not reproduce those numbers as the settled state. Every stable reading, run repeatedly, is 41.75% / 18px / 1.5x, as in the table above.

The 0.71% / 14px numbers are real, but they are a loading-state snapshot, not the settled page: I could reproduce them on demand by cutting the page's settle time short, and they disappeared every time I gave the page enough time to finish loading its salon data. The dev server was under heavy load from other work happening in this same project folder while I was measuring, which is the likely reason a loading-state reading got captured as the page's resting state. The real defect is smaller than reported: two checks fail, not three, and by a smaller margin than described. The fix above still clears both.
