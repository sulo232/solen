What actually changed

Two CSS rules, nothing else. Your name: same 28px, just medium weight instead of semibold. The "Edit profile" link: 14px down to 13px, the same size every row's small grey subtext already uses. That single move also fixes a second problem: five different text sizes were bunched inside an 8px window, which reads as noise rather than hierarchy. Moving the link's size takes that down to four.

What I was told that did not hold up

I was told this screen and the settings screen measured identically and share one template. I checked, signed in as a real seeded account, before touching anything. They do not. Your profile screen already passes its size-anchor and ratio floors right now (28px, 2.15x). Settings fails both (24px against a floor of 28, because with no photo on that account the fallback initial letter is the largest text on the screen; 1.6x against a floor of 1.8). They are two different components in the code, not one, so this fix only touches the one named in the title. Settings needs its own pass.

The photo question, your call either way

This screen has no photo, and the rule that wants roughly a third of the screen to be photographic is failing here, at 0%. I did not add one. Five weeks ago you removed the one photo this screen had, a small favorites photo strip, saying "konto hub better" about the plain row list that replaced it. Putting a photo strip back to satisfy a percentage would undo that. Two honest paths, both yours: leave this screen text-only and mark it exempt from the photo rule the way forms and checkout already are, or bring a small strip of the salons you favorited back, using the same salon card the rest of the app already uses, never a one-off picture.
