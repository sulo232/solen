How we mix it , the integration question

You liked the Uber-minimalist but not everywhere. Good instinct: I read all four of your other screens in code, and the app is already about 70% there , the salon info sections and the notification switches are ALREADY borderless. What sticks out are the leftover card boxes: the settings sections, the bookings grid, the service tiers, the team/reviews panels. So the real question is not "which screens" but "what is the rule". Here are three ways to draw it.

My recommendation: Model B , "chrome off, density stays". One rule for the whole app: remove the decorative box (card border + shadow) everywhere, so content sits on white grouped by hairlines, whitespace and bold labels. KEEP a dense control only when it carries a real decision , filter pills, prices, taxonomy pickers, photos, status. It gives you ONE visual language everywhere (no "two apps" feeling, which was the risk with a screen-by-screen split), it is decidable element by element, and the code shows you are already most of the way there.
