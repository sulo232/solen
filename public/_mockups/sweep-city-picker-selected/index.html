City picker , selected option

In the city dropdown, the current city is only bolder than the others, no fill, no check. The locked list-option treatment is a gray sunken fill plus a trailing check, which every other menu in the app uses. Since three separate pickers (desktop selector, mobile menu, header chip) all under-signal it the same way, this is one fix across all three.

Direction A , sunken fill + check the locked list-option

The active city gets the gray sunken fill, semibold, and a trailing check, exactly the locked menu-option pattern. Selection is unmistakable at a glance instead of relying on a one-step weight difference.
