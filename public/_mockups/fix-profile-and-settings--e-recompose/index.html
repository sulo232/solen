Clearing every floor on your account pages at once

Four boxes below, two per screen: your account page and your settings page, each shown as it is now and then with a real change applied live. Nothing here is a new drawing, every panel loads from your dev server. I also found that the numbers I was handed for this run were already out of date for one of the two screens, that correction comes before the panels, not after.

What I found before touching anything

Your account page had already been fixed for three of the six floors. I was briefed with an 18px anchor, a 40% weight share and a 1.5x ratio. Measuring the live page just now, it is 28px, 22.3% and 2.0x, all already clearing their floors. Reading the component's own code explains why: it carries a comment from an earlier session documenting exactly this fix, moving the name from 24px to 28px and dropping row labels from bold to medium. I am not claiming credit for that or re-doing it, I am telling you the number you were given for this run does not match what is live, so you are not surprised later when someone re-measures and gets a different answer than this file.

Your settings page was not part of that fix and still needs it. It has its own separate name element, 20px and bold, not the 28px/600 one the account page uses. Same person, same fact, two different sizes, that is the exact thing FLOORS LAW 8 exists to catch.

Both pages are genuinely thin on real content. 1.09% and 1.24% photographic on the first screen, against a 33% floor. That part of the brief was right.

Where this mechanism cannot move the number, and why

The size-spread floor still fails once you count the WHOLE rendered account screen, chrome included: the shared top header (an 18px bold page title, a 10px notification-count badge) and the shared bottom tab bar (12px labels) add three sizes that AccountHub's own content does not carry. Both are named off-limits in this task, so this mechanism cannot touch them, and neither can any of the sibling variants being built alongside this one. If that count matters more than the content-only count, it needs a separate, product-wide pass on the header and bottom-nav components themselves, not a fix scoped to this one screen.

Settings keeps 8 distinct content-plus-chrome sizes after this change, one swapped (20 became 28) rather than removed. Consolidating settings' own five nearby content sizes (12, 13, 14, 15, 16) into fewer values is a real, separate cleanup this mockup did not attempt, it touches every row on the page rather than one element.

The favorites section and the settings-name resize are both DOM patches applied live in your browser to the real running pages, nothing has been written to any component file. The imagery badge above the account-page after panel is computed by the script below the moment the nested favorites iframe finishes loading, from the real photo pixels on screen right now in your seed data, not a number I typed in.
