What changed in v2

Dropped the warm-glass chrome and warm-cream surfaces. The card is now a flat white panel on a cool #FAFAFA background with a neutral sunken Lock tile. Blue now signals interactivity — the show-password toggle, the input focus ring, and the Back to sign in link all read blue, while body labels stay ink. The Change password button is the single ink commit anchor. The success state is the headline change: a deep dark-green #15803D disc with a white checkmark that animates in sequence — ring pulse, disc spring-pop, check stroke-draw, then the confirmation text rises in — so the result feels confident and deep instead of a pale wash. All states open with an entrance rise + stagger, the card lifts on hover, and the button presses on tap.
