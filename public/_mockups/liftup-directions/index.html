Profile , the direction question

The same real profile page (Appointments, Favorites, Loyalty, Stamps, Hair profile, Looks, Forms, Refer, Notifications, Settings, Help), treated two ways. Left: today, grouped cards with shadow and hairlines. Right: the Uber direction you like , borderless, full-width rows on white, only whitespace and bold section titles as grouping.

My recommendation: the minimalist direction (right) for all personal / account / list surfaces (Profile, Settings, Appointments, Forms) , there, calm and readability beat density. The pills / Fresha-dense patterns stay on Browse / Search / Inspo, where dense scanning of salons and photos is the point. So not one style everywhere, but: minimalist for account surfaces, density for marketplace surfaces. Tell me if that split is right, or if you want the minimalist direction everywhere.
