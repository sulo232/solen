What this mechanism was asked to fix, and what it actually fixed

This mockup's one job was the weight fix: take every piece of bold (weight 600) text on the category page and turn it to a plain medium weight (500), the way Airbnb does, so size and colour carry attention instead of boldness. I built exactly that, as one injected style rule, nothing else touched. It works: every bold word on the screen, the "Top Coiffeur" heading, the four salon names, the price chip, all move from weight 600 to weight 500, and the bold share of the screen drops from 28% to 0%.

What it does not do is fix anything, because there was nothing broken on this specific check. Ahead of this fix, 28% of the screen's text was bold, already under our own 30% ceiling. Once applied, it is 0%. Both numbers pass. The screen's two real failures, an 18px biggest-text element where the rule wants 28px, and a 1.5x size step where the rule wants 1.8x, are both about SIZE, and a font-weight rule cannot change a font-size number. I checked: the "Top Coiffeur" heading measures 18px with the fix off and 18px with the fix on, on every run.

One thing to flag rather than hide: taking weight away from the rail heading, without adding size, makes it read as slightly LESS of a heading, not more, since weight was one of the two things (with its 18px size) telling your eye it was a heading at all. Look at the two panels above; "Top Coiffeur" is a little harder to pick out in the second one. If this fix ships alone, the page gets flatter, not clearer. It needs to ship together with a real size fix, not instead of one.

Correction on the numbers I was handed for this screen

The brief said this screen measures at 0.71% photo coverage, a 14px heading, a 1.02x size ratio and effectively no hierarchy. I could reproduce those exact numbers, but only as a LOADING-state reading: the heading text at that moment reads "Coiffeur Schweizweit", a skeleton label, not the real "Top Coiffeur" heading the page settles on. I ran the project's own measuring script three times against this worktree's dev server (localhost:3457) and, separately, wrote a byte-for-byte copy of that script's own weight and size logic and ran it directly in the browser once the page finished loading. Both methods agree on one stable resting state: 41.75% photo coverage, an 18px heading, a 1.5x ratio, and 28.13% bold text. Two checks fail on the settled page, not three, and the weight check (this mockup's job) already passes, both in the loading snapshot (10%) and the settled page (28.13%).

This is the same correction the sibling mockup for this screen (floor-fix-category-pages) already reports, reached independently. I am naming it again here because my own assigned floor, F7a weight share, is the one the brief's stale numbers got most wrong: the brief's "measured today" block already said weight share passes at 10%, and my own measurement agrees it passes, just at a higher, settled number (28.13%). At no point, loading or settled, does this screen actually fail the weight-share floor.
