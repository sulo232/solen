What's his call, and what's just matching the salon page he already likes

  • Whether the page gets a title at all. It has never had one; every option except 4 adds real, first-time visible content, not a style tweak.
  • If a title is added, whether it's bold (options 1 and 2) or medium weight (option 5). Bold matches the salon page's own title exactly; medium keeps the page's total bold-text share lower.
  • Whether to touch every bold word on the page (options 4 and 5) or leave the cards' bold names and prices as the only bold text (options 1, 2, 3).
  • The title's size (30px) and font. Both are copied directly from the salon page's own locked title, not invented.
  • The 30/14 title-to-body size ratio and the under-30%-bold ceiling. Both are the exact numbers the salon page already clears, applied here, not new rules.
