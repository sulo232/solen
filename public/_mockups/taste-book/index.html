1. Boxes and grouping

1a. Separate things get separate boxes

If each item is its own thing you can tap, it gets its own card. One big box makes the eye stop and treat it all as one blob.

1b. No box inside a box
Wrong
Cancellation policy
Free cancellation up to 24h before your appointment.
Right
Cancellation policy
Free cancellation up to 24h before your appointment.

Never put a card inside a card. Inside a card, use a thin line or just space.

1c. If it already looks different, no line needed
Wrong
Atelier Marie
Marktgasse 12
Right
Atelier Marie Marktgasse 12

Bold name next to grey text already reads as two things. A line between them is noise.

Facts about one booking belong in one box. Splitting them makes four things out of one.

2. Text sizes

2a. Three sizes max

A screen needs big, medium, small. More sizes means the eye cannot rank anything.

2b. Make the important thing clearly bigger
Wrong
Booking confirmed
Your appointment is set for today at 14:30.
Right
Booking confirmed
Your appointment is set for today at 14:30.

If something should lead, make it 30 to 50 percent bigger. A 1px difference just looks like a mistake.

2c. Headings stick to what they introduce
Wrong
Salon details
Payment
Visa, Mastercard, TWINT accepted.
16px above, 16px below
Right
Salon details
Payment
Visa, Mastercard, TWINT accepted.
24px above, 8px below

More space above a heading, less below. Then it visibly owns the text under it.

2d. One kind of emphasis at a time
Wrong Book now
Right Book now

Pick one way to emphasize. Stacking bold plus color plus size plus underline reads as shouting.
