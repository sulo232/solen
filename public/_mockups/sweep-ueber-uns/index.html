About page , structure

The About page is a bare stub, thinner than the site's own marketing pages. Its real content is short (a few paragraphs), so the question is a LIGHT typographic polish, not a hero-heavy rebuild that would need invented imagery.

Direction A , light polish recommended

Same content, better hierarchy: Page-title H1, one lead sentence, quiet grey section labels instead of heavy underlined H2s, roomier line-height. Reads intentional without inventing content or imagery.
