One heading becomes the anchor

Right now the Basel page has one heading, "Salons in Basel", but it tops out at 25px, and every salon name below it is smaller than the subtitle sentence next to it. There is a peak and then a flat floor, nothing in between. Below, the heading grows to your own house size, and every salon name gets one clear step of its own. Nothing moves. Nothing new is added.

With one clear heading and a real second step

The heading is now the standout. Every salon name reads as the next thing to look at. Everything else was already fine where it sits.

biggest text: 30px
What actually changed. Two things, both just size and weight, nothing else. The page heading, "Salons in Basel", grows from 25px to 30px and drops from bold to medium weight, the same recipe this system already locked for its display anchor. Every salon name on the page, all of them, the same one component, grows from 15px to 16px and from regular to medium weight, so it reads as a real second rank instead of sitting smaller than the paragraph above it. Nothing shrinks, nothing moves, nothing is deleted or added.
What this fixes. The heading was 25px against your 28px floor, one hair under it, and only 1.79x the 14px body text against a 1.8x floor, also one hair under. Both come from the same cause: the heading's own size formula bottoms out at 25px on a phone screen. Raising that floor to 30px clears both numbers at once: 30px against the 28px floor, and 2.14x against the 1.8x floor, the exact ratio the one screen you already like, the Cuts & Culture salon's own page, runs at today.
What this does not fix, and why I did not touch it. Just under half of the visible text on this screen is bold today (I measured 47.06%, live, this turn, not the number I was handed, see the full note in the file's header), against a 30% ceiling. That is a real problem and it is not a small one, but it is a different bug with a different fix: the category buttons under the heading (Coiffeur, Barbershop, Nails) are bold unconditionally in the component's own code, whether or not you are standing on them. Making an anchor bigger does not fix a button that is bold for no reason. A sibling variant of this same screen targets exactly that. This panel's own two changes happen to nudge the number in the right direction anyway, from 47.06% to 41.18%, because the heading itself went from bold to medium, but that was not the job here and the number is still failing after this panel's fix.
What this does not touch. The photos, the salon cards' layout, the category buttons, the search bar, the colors, the copy. The photo share of this screen already clears its own floor today (43.18% against a 33% minimum, measured live), so nothing needed adding there either.
