Four ways to fix your profile and settings pages, one under the other

Right now these two pages have real problems and they don't share a fix. Your name renders at a good size on your profile page (28px) but shrinks to 20px, one tap later, on the settings page, the same name, two sizes. Both pages are almost entirely icons and text, under 1.3% of the screen is a real photo, against the roughly one-third that a page like your salon page for Cuts & Culture carries. And the small text on both screens is cut into too many almost-identical sizes to read as clean groups.

Below are four different fixes for that, each applied live to your real, running pages, real data, nothing invented. A fifth option was supposed to be here too (the smallest possible CSS change) but its file was never actually written, that's called out below rather than skipped quietly. Scroll down to compare each option against "Now" at the top of each screen's section.

Option B

Fold the small text together

Your name stays exactly as it is. The badge, the subtext under each row, and the small grey title at the very top all step into one matching secondary size instead of splitting across three sizes that were 1-2px apart.
moves: how many sizes are on screen, 7 down to 4 queued
Does not touch: the photo (still ~1.1%) or how much text is bold. This option only cleans up sizes, it does not add content.
Option C

Put your real favorites where you can see them

Nothing is invented. This pulls the real salon cards from your own favorites page (the one behind the heart icon) and shows them directly under your name, so the first thing you see is a real photo instead of a list of icons.
moves: real photo on screen, from ~1% toward 33%+ queued
Does not touch: the seven-sizes problem or how much text is bold. Needs your yes: a salon-photo section on this exact screen was removed twice before, on 2026-07-21 and 2026-08-02 ("konto hub better"), so this is shown for you to decide, not applied. It also only works because this test account happens to have real favorites saved, a brand-new customer with none would need a different fallback.
Option D

Let bold text calm down

About a third of the text on this screen is bold today: the top bar title, your name, every section label. This makes all of that one step lighter, medium instead of semibold or bold. No sizes, spacing, or colors change.
moves: how much text is bold, down to 0% queued
Does not touch: the photo, the sizes problem, or your name (already big enough at 28px, this option leaves it alone).
Option E

The real favorites section, cropped and measured

The same idea as Option C, your real favorite salons shown under your name, but here it is cropped to a fixed height as it loads.
moves: real photo on screen, live queued
Does not touch: the seven-sizes problem or bold text. Needs your yes, the same graveyard collision as Option C: this exact kind of section was removed twice before. This builder argues the earlier removal was about a search bar and a bespoke grid, not photos themselves, but says plainly that is his own read, not a settled fact.

Option E

Just the name, matched, nothing else

The smallest of the five moves on this whole page: one CSS rule, your name grows from 20px/700 to 28px/600, matching your profile page's own recipe exactly. Nothing else on the screen moves.

Does not touch: the photo (stays ~1.24%) or the seven-sizes problem. This builder recommends leaving the photo off settings on purpose (an admin screen's job isn't browsing), and says so as a recommendation, not a decision made quietly.

What's your call, and what's just catching these pages up to one you already like

  • Your call: whether a real salon photo goes on either page at all. This exact kind of section (a photo grid on the account hub) was removed twice before, 2026-07-21 and 2026-08-02 ("konto hub better"). Options C and E both reopen that decision, using your real favorites through the real card component, no search bar, no bespoke grid. Two of this page's four builders think that is a different thing from what was removed, but say plainly that is their own read, not your settled call.
  • Your call: whether settings gets a photo section too, or just the name fix. Option C puts the same favorites splice on settings; Option E's own builder recommends against it there, arguing an admin screen (password, notifications, payment) isn't the job a browsing card serves, and that the profile page one tap away already carries it. Both are shown so you can pick either way.
  • Not your call, these just match the page you already like: growing your name on settings from 20px to 28px, and folding seven crowded text sizes down to four or five, are both this screen catching up to the same ladder your own Cuts & Culture salon page already clears. Nothing new is being decided there, it's the same rule applied here that already applies everywhere else. Every option that touches bold text (D, and part of B) is the same kind of catch-up: it moves weight share toward the ceiling this system already enforces on every other screen.
