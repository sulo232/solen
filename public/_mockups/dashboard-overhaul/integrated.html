Solen Operator Dashboard

Integrated: sidebar + chair cards + action dock

The three fresh directions merged into one home, per the owner's request to combine what they liked from each. The integration logic that keeps two bars from feeling redundant: the sidebar is PLACES, the only section navigation (Overview, Calendar, Clients, and so on, all nouns), shown open and labeled. The bottom dock is ACTIONS, five verbs that do something right now (New sale, New appointment, Walk-in, Payments, Reports) and never a section link. The chair cards in the center are the live day, who is in the chair and who is next. The right rail is what needs you: to-dos and a live activity stream.
