Solen Operator Dashboard

Design overhaul: three directions

All three show the same real Overview (/de/dashboard), reskinned: icon rail, setup, KPI strip, a Today timeline, revenue and appointments charts, activity, top services, top team. Only the look changes, the structure stays. Scope is the operator dashboard only (LOCKFILE §12); the locked customer B&W system (§1-§11) is untouched. Recommendation: A (Refined Light) as the ship base, B (Command) as an optional dark mode.

A. Refined Light, the recommended ship base
Today's skin, done right: calm white, a real 4-up KPI strip (missing today), soft depth instead of flat boxes, blue disciplined to links and the active state only, one ink CTA. Friendly for non-technical salon owners, the smallest jump from the locked §12.
Low risk

B. Command, the statement
A dark cockpit (Revolut charcoal, like the iOS dark mode). Less eye strain across the day, live data and numbers glow, blue becomes the accent glow. The boldest, most differentiated option. Caveat: dark can feel cold to some owners and weakens photo-heavy sub-pages, so ship it as a toggle, not the only skin.
