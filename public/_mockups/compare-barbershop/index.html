Six versions of the barbershop page, one under the other

Right now this is the worst-scoring page in the product. The walk-in queue block near the bottom shows real barbers with no photo at all, and it repeats each barber's name in bold a second time, right under the same name already shown once, in a rail higher up, with a photo. The result is less than a quarter of the screen is photography, the single biggest piece of text is a small repeated status word instead of a real heading, and nearly half of all the text on the screen is bold. Below are five different fixes, each applied live to your real, running barbershop page, real salons and real photos, nothing invented. Panels load one at a time, in order, so the browser is never asked to run all six at once. Scroll to compare them one at a time against "Now" at the top.

These numbers came off your own salon page for Cuts & Culture, the one screen that already passes every check and the one you said you like. That page is the ruler every fix below is measured against.

What's your call, and what's just catching this page up to one it already passes

  • Your call: whether card width reopens. Options C and E both widen the real barbershop cards past the 1.5-cards-per-screen value approved in July on purpose. Fewer cards are visible per swipe once they're this wide. Options A, B, and D leave card width alone.
  • Your call: whether the bold-weight rule reopens sitewide. Option D's full mechanism, applied everywhere the same way, would also demote the salon card's locked name+price pair (LOCKFILE V3-D442). This page's iframe copy never touches that lock, but shipping the pattern for real needs that value reopened by name first.
  • Your call: whether the walk-in row keeps its second bold name. Options B and E both shrink or reweight the walk-in row's repeated copy of a name and rating the rail above already shows once. That repeat is real information (queue status), just currently rendered louder than the same name's first, photographed appearance.
  • Not your call, these just match the screen you already like: pulling bold text down toward 30%, giving the page one real display anchor instead of a status word that happens to be biggest, and closing in on a 1.8x-or-better headline ratio are all just this screen catching up to the same ladder your own Cuts & Culture salon page already clears. Nothing new is being decided there, it's the same rule applied here that already applies everywhere else.
  • Not settled by any of these five: the 33% imagery floor. Every option that touches photo share (A, C, E) moves the number in the right direction and still lands short. Clearing it for real needs more real barber content surfaced higher on the page, which is a content decision, not a treatment this page can show as a CSS-only mockup.
