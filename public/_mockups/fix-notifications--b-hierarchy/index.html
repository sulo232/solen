With a real anchor

The title grows to your house value. The row headline steps up one notch to stay clearly second. The small blue "mark all read" link steps down into the same tier as the row preview text, since it is the least important line on the screen.

Why this screen needed it. I measured the real page myself, signed in, at your phone width. The title is 20px next to a 14px body size, a 1.43x step. Your own house rule, taken from the one screen you like, wants that step at 1.8x or more. At 30px the title clears that at 2.14x, the same ratio that reference screen already runs.
Why the link moves down, not just the title up. Before this change, five different text sizes were visible on the screen at once (12, 13, 14, 15, 20), and all five sat inside one narrow 8-pixel band. Nothing had room to be clearly bigger or clearly smaller than anything else. Moving "mark all read" down into the same size as the row preview text removes one of those five sizes entirely, so the remaining set spreads out instead of bunching up.
What this does not fix. Two separate things are still wrong with this screen and this change does not touch either: there is no photo anywhere in the inbox, and a second mockup already built for this same screen found the shadow/depth question needs its own separate look. Both need a different kind of fix, not a resize.
One correction. The task brief I was handed for this screen said the title was already 28px and already passing. I checked the real page myself before building anything and that is not what it renders: it is 20px, and it was genuinely failing the ratio floor. I built against what I actually measured, not the number I was given.
