What you would actually see change. The big headline at the top gets a little bigger and a little lighter, the same weight your salon page's headline already uses. The line under it, which used to be its own size, drops to match the size every section title on this page already uses further down, so it reads as a supporting line, not a second competing headline. The three checklist lines under "Chatten Sie live" get very slightly smaller, matching your normal body text everywhere else. Nothing moves, nothing is added, nothing is removed.

Why the ratio number in the brief needed a correction. I was handed a target ratio measured against whatever the second-biggest text on screen already was. That text was the 20px subtitle, and the subtitle was never a locked size on this product's own scale, it was just sitting there. Once it folds into the section-title size it already matches, the ratio that actually matters is the headline over your real body text, 30 over 14, which is 2.14 times bigger, the exact ratio your Cuts & Culture salon page already uses and that you like.
What this does not fix, on purpose. Two things named in the brief are not touched here because a text-size change cannot fix them. First, there is still no photo anywhere in this view, zero percent, against a one-third target; fixing that means pulling real salon cards onto this page, which is a content decision with two honest options (explained side by side in the sibling file in this same folder), not a styling one. Second, this page was measured as the grayest screen in the product, about three quarters of it is the sunken gray background across two stacked full-width sections. That is a bigger call than a type fix and it is yours to make, not something this panel changes.
