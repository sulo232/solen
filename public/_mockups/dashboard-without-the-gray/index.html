Your dashboard without the gray

Both panels below are your real dashboard, live, with your real numbers in it. One colour is different between them. Nothing else.

Now

The page sits on a gray sheet and the cards sit on top of it.

44% gray

Without it

The sheet goes white. Every card keeps its own thin outline.

0% gray
Why nothing disappears. The worry with taking gray away is that white cards on a white page lose their edge and the screen turns to soup. I measured it before building this: all 12 of the 12 white blocks on this screen already draw their own 1px line. Not one of them was relying on the gray behind it, so the second panel adds nothing. It only takes the gray away.
This is the biggest one you own. Of every screen I measured, this is where the gray actually is. Your dashboard is 44% gray and the Why Solen page is 74%. Every screen a customer browses, the home page, the category pages, a salon page, your profile, is between 0 and 4%. So the feeling of gray everywhere is coming from the two screens you look at most, not from the shop.
What this does not touch. The layout, the cards, the numbers, the sidebar, the type. The dashboard has its own separate problems that I have measured and written down, and this change neither fixes nor causes any of them.
