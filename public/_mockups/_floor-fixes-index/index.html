I took the pass and fail counts off this page on purpose. Every measurement today was taken while forty other jobs drove the same test machine, so screens got photographed before they finished drawing, and four counts moved when they were checked again. What each screen is missing has not changed and is described above in plain words. The exact counts are being re-taken on a quiet machine and go back on when they hold still.
Two faults in my measuring tool came out of this. It could not log in, so when it measured your notifications and profile screens it was really measuring the sign-in screen. And it waited a fixed four seconds before looking, which is not enough on a busy machine. Both are being fixed, and the same tool runs your nightly check, so this was never only about today.
