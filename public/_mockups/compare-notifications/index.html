Your notifications screen today, and five ways to fix it

Right now the title on this screen is small, most of the text is bold, five different text sizes are all crammed close together, and there is not one photo anywhere on it. That is why it reads flat and busy at the same time.

Below are five different repair jobs, all built on your real, live notifications inbox, nothing redrawn. Panel 1 is the smallest possible touch: it only swaps two icons for photos. Panel 2 only fixes the title and text sizes. Panel 3 only fixes the photo problem, by taking the one notification that already mentions a real salon and growing it into a photo card. Panel 4 only fixes the boldness. Panel 5 does everything at once: bigger calmer title, less bold, one gray tray behind the list, and a photo on the one row that is actually tied to a real booking.

Scroll down, compare, and pick. Each caption names the one number it moves.
