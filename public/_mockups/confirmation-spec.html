1. What this screen is

The post-payment booking receipt a customer sees after booking (route /{locale}/confirmation, component components-legacy/booking/BookingConfirmation.tsx), plus the reschedule sheet ("Move your appointment") it opens from the date row. Reference mockup: public/_mockups/confirmation-v2.html.

2. Backend truths (all verified live, 2026-07-15)

  • Money split comes from the bookings row: paid_amount (Rappen, already paid online) and the remainder due at the salon. Show BOTH: paid recedes (small, grey), owed is the money hero (biggest number on screen). Never fabricate amounts.
  • Reschedule: POST /api/bookings/[id]/reschedule , exists, zod-validated, authorized (resolveBookingActor), slot-claiming, rate-limited, 24h minimum lead. Body: new_starts_at + new_ends_at (ISO); derive new_ends_at from the booking's real duration.
  • Availability for the picker: GET /api/availability/unavailable-dates + GET /api/availability/time-slots (same endpoints the booking flow uses; both read the availability_slots table).
  • Cancel exists and is wired (/api/bookings/[id]/cancel via CancelBookingSheet). There is NO generic edit endpoint (change service / stylist after the fact) , so those rows must NOT pretend to be editable.
  • Guest access link: 256-bit token, only its SHA-256 stored, 30-day TTL, timing-safe compare. Safe by design; never put personal data in URLs beyond it.

Geometry + elevation (locked rows)

  • Radius: card/block 16, button/chip pill, input 16, sheet 28, image flush(0) (photos full-bleed, square corners, edge-to-edge).
  • Shadow: cards carry elevation-2 at rest = 0 2px 8px rgba(50,47,44,.09) (flat cards read cheap , owner taste log 2026-06-07); controls over a photo = frosted glass (rgba(255,255,255,.80) + blur 4 + 1px rgba(255,255,255,.6) + soft shadow); calm controls on white = flat.
  • Type: Inter Tight headings / Inter body. Receipt headline 24/700; row title 14.5/600; meta 12-13 ink-2; CTA 15/600 (never 13 or below); prices tabular.
  • Spacing: 4-pt scale only (12/16/24/32). Page gutter 16. Touch targets 44px minimum; icon-button 44 (38px circled X allowed for sheet close).

Behavioral laws

  • Dead-click contract: an arrow/chevron exists ONLY if tapping does something real today. No affordance without a backed destination.
  • Copy economy: delete every word the context already says. One meaning appears ONCE on screen (no date repeated in headline + row; no duplicate edit affordances).
  • No fabricated data: every number wired to a live source, or omitted.
  • Semantic color is not decoration: confirmed = green word or green disc + white check; never monochromed to ink, never a pale wash on big surfaces.
  • Icons: Lucide only. No em-dashes in copy. One primary ink CTA per screen; secondary = neutral outline. No decorative separator dots; contrast or spacing separates adjacent metadata.
  • Footer: none on focused/terminal flows (confirmation is terminal). Help = circled ? icon top-right over the photo (frost).

5. The reschedule sheet ("Move your appointment")

  • Bottom sheet, radius 28 top, grab handle, title 19/700 + 38px circled-X close (flat hairline circle , it sits on white, not on a photo).
  • Lead note as plain text (12.5 ink-2, info icon): "Free to move up to 24h before. Your CHF 25.50 stays on the booking." , both facts from the backend (24h rule, paid amount).
  • Date strip = the real DateTimePicker primitive cell-for-cell (app/[locale]/_components/primitives/DateTimePicker.tsx): 72px-wide cells, radius 16, weekday 12/500 + day numeral 22/700 tabular + month 12; disabled day keeps its border, only text drops to 30% ink; no scrollbar. NEVER a bespoke date UI (V3-D445).
  • Slots: the primitive's bordered panel (radius 12, padding 16) per period group (Morning/Afternoon/Evening label 12.5/600 ink-3), pills rounded-full, 14/600 tabular, 4 columns gap 6; unavailable = 40% opacity, no strikethrough (invented treatment, removed).
  • Selected day + slot fill = accent blue , the locked exception (booking date/slot stays blue; everything else selected in the app is gray sunken).
  • Commit: one ink pill naming the choice ("Move to Thu 16 July, 10:30"), disabled at 50% until a slot is picked. Sends new_starts_at/new_ends_at to the reschedule endpoint; bookings under 24h away never show an enabled commit.
Open contradiction to resolve (found 2026-07-15): the live booking step (DateTimeStep.tsx:201) currently passes selectedTone="ink", but the owner's round-3 lock (TASTE_LOG 2026-06-07, audit B1) says selected date/slot = blue everywhere, ink reserved for the commit button. The spec above follows the lock (blue). The live step should be flipped back to accent, or the lock formally re-decided by the owner.
