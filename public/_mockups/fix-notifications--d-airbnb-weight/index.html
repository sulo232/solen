Making the notifications page a little less bold

Same inbox, twice. In the second one, every bold or semi-bold word on the screen (the title, the unread count line, the mark-all-read link, the section label, the unread row titles) gets a touch lighter. Nothing moves, nothing resizes, nothing changes colour. Scroll inside each box to compare.

What changed: right now, a bit over a third of the words on this screen are bold. That includes the page title, the unread count line, the mark-all-read link, the section label, and the title of every unread row. When that much is bold, nothing stands out, it just reads heavy. This test makes all of that a touch lighter (medium instead of semibold or bold) everywhere on the screen. The sizes stay exactly the same, the title is still the biggest word on the page and the row titles are still bigger than their timestamps, so you can still tell what matters, that job just shifts from weight to size, which is already how the page is laid out.
What this does not fix: three other things are still off on this screen and this change cannot touch them, because they are about size and photos, not weight: the biggest word on the page is only 20px (we want at least 28px, a real headline moment), the gap between the biggest and smallest text is too small, and the screen still has zero photos in the first view. Those need their own fix, and the imagery one needs your call first (row thumbnails only get to about 4% coverage, nowhere near the 33% target, without a bigger redesign of the row format).
One correction: the brief for this test said this screen already passed on size, ratio, and spread, and only had a weight problem to look at for completeness. Measuring the real, logged-in page directly found the opposite: only imagery was correctly flagged, everything else including the weight number itself was wrong. The real weight number is 36.36%, which is why this fix has real work to do here, not none.
Both panels are the same live route, loaded through the dev login helper so the real inbox shows instead of the login redirect. The lightened weight is applied here as a test overlay on Tailwind's shared "semibold"/"bold" utilities, which are used across the whole product (including the bell icon's unread badge in the header, which is shared chrome), so shipping this for real would scope the rule to just this page's own elements instead of a global override.
