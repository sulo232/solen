Bookings , loading state

The bookings list currently loads with a bare centered spinner, then hard-swaps to content. The design contract locks loading to a Skeleton shaped like the final layout, so the page settles instead of popping. Which loads better?

Direction A , layout-matched skeleton recommended

Two BookingCard-shaped skeletons (date block + two text lines + status pill) fade to real content. The page never jumps , the skeleton is the same shape as what arrives. This is the locked rule.

Direction B , spinner (current)

Keep the centered spinner. Simplest, but it shows nothing about the layout and then hard-swaps to a full list, so the content "pops" in , the exact jump the Skeleton rule was written to remove.
