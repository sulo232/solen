Reviews page: content higher and bigger

Today this page opens on chrome, a heading, a filter row, a sort button, and no photo at all. The fix below puts a real photo of the salon at the top and tightens the space around it, so the page opens on the actual place instead of only its controls.

Before
The live reviews page, untouched. No photo anywhere on the screen. The first review's full text and reply are visible, and the second review's avatar is just starting to peek in.
Measured: 0% of the first screen is a photo (needs 33%+).
live, unedited
After
Same live page. A real photo of Cuts & Culture, the actual salon this page belongs to, now sits at the top. The heading, rating line, filter row and sort button are pulled tighter together underneath it, so the first review still starts on this screen.
Measured: 34.36% of the first screen is now a real photo. Trade-off, said plainly: the first review's name and date now clear the screen, but its comment text starts just below it, where before the full comment and the salon's reply were both visible.
live, fix applied
Both boxes above are the real page loading right now, not a drawing. Before is exactly what ships today. After has four DOM changes made by a script once the real page loads: one new photo element, and three spacing values tightened on existing elements. Nothing is redrawn, invented, or hardcoded.
