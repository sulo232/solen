Partner FAQ , layout

The partner page's FAQ is a bespoke horizontal swipe-card widget; the app has a shared vertical accordion (FAQItem) used elsewhere. An FAQ's whole job is scannability , which layout serves 9 questions better?

Direction A , shared accordion recommended

Reuse the FAQItem vertical accordion: every question visible as a stack, tap to expand, keyboard + ARIA correct for free. Scannable at a glance, no gesture needed, and it drops a bespoke widget.
