Dashboard status pills , the primitive vs hand-rolled

There's a DashStatusPill component for exactly this, but the earnings, settings and content pages hand-roll their own pills, and some reference tokens that don't exist, so those classes emit no CSS: the "recorded" pill ends up with no background and no colour, and "pending" text falls back to ink. Route them all through the primitive?

Direction A , DashStatusPill the primitive

Pale tint + saturated semantic text + a small dot: paid = green, pending = amber, recorded = neutral. Every status pill on the console reads the same and none can silently lose its colour to a dead token.
