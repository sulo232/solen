How to show "selected" (no black box)

Your photo chips can't fill with a colour (the photo IS the chip), so the indicator goes ON the chip. Here is what real apps do, applied to your Shag / Textured / Layered chips. The 2nd chip is "selected" in each row.

A · Checkmark badge Recommended
Instagram & Pinterest multi-select. A small solid check in the corner. Reads instantly, sits on the photo, no border, no recolour.
Shag Textured Layered Fringe
B · Darken + center check
The photo-chip version of a solid fill (Fresha / Uber fill their text chips dark). Darkens the selected photo and centers a check. Clear, but heavier.
Shag Textured Layered Fringe
C · Dim the rest
Selected stays full, the others fade back (Netflix / gallery pattern). Elegant, but ambiguous when nothing is selected yet, and weaker on small chips.
Shag Textured Layered Fringe
D · Bottom bar (tab style)
A short ink bar under the selected chip (Uber Eats tabs). Clean, but reads more like a "tab" than a "filter", and adds height.
Shag Textured Layered Fringe
E · Dim only the selected Your idea
Darkens just the selected chip, nothing else. Minimal and clean. One worry: a darker chip can read as "off / disabled" rather than "chosen", because we expect the selected thing to stand OUT, not recede. Look at "Textured" below and see if it reads as picked to you.
Shag Textured Layered Fringe
For text chips + the filter (Textur, radios): these CAN fill, and that is exactly what Fresha, Uber and Pinterest do for theirs. Two clean choices:
Textur solid ink fill (Fresha / Uber) Damen soft blue tint (lighter, on-brand)
My pick: A (checkmark badge) for the photo chips. it needs no border or recolour (so no "black box" and no blue ring), works on any photo, and is the literal Instagram/Pinterest pattern for "this one's chosen." For the few text things that aren't photos (the Textur control, the filter radios), keep the solid ink fill like Fresha/Uber. one system: photos get a check, text gets a fill.
