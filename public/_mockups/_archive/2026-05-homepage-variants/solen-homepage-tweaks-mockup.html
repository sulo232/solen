The 6 tweaks

  • 1
    Hero density. Headline ~46→30px, kill the ~120px dead gap → the search card + CTA sit above the fold. Conversion-critical · one component.
  • 2
    Kill the twin carousels. "Top auf Solen" becomes editorial (bigger cards, Solen-Favorit badges); "In der Nähe" becomes map-led + distance-first. No more two identical salon rows with the same salons. Biggest "page feels tighter" win.
  • 3
    City → one pill. The redundant "Du siehst gerade Basel" bar + duplicate pill + mystery arrow collapse into a single Basel ▾ pill in the header. Reclaims the top strip.
  • 4
    Card meta diet. From "address · city / time · CHF" (4 grey signals) down to ★ rating + ab CHF — the two that drive a tap.
  • 5
    Format variety. A full-bleed dark feature band (here: Walk-in) breaks the six-identical-carousels rhythm and gives the eye a peak.
  • 6
    Trust beat. ★ 4.9 · 12’000+ Termine · 800+ Salons right under the search, instead of burying proof at the very bottom.

Not touched: the design system (tokens/type/color are all on-spec), the "Profis in deiner Nähe" stylist list (it's the strongest section), and the Inspiration row (just cleaned the burned-in captions).
