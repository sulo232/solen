What the curation logic does (3 changes):
  • ① Reorder the "Für dich" tiles — picked categories (Nails, Spa) jump to the front + get an ink-ring "selected" marker. Logged-out / no-picks users see today's default order. Low risk — one component.
  • ② + ③ Inject category rows — "Weil du Nails magst" and your 2nd pick, each a row of real salon cards queried for that category. This is the real payoff (the promise: "wir bauen deine Startseite darum"). Adds sections to the shared feed → I scout the other agent's files first.
  • Interests ("Deals") surface as the −20% price badge / ordering hint; "Top-Salons" biases the rows toward higher ratings.

How it stays fast: the homepage keeps its static cache; personalization runs client-side (reads your profile, then reorders + fills the rows) so we don't slow down the busiest page.
