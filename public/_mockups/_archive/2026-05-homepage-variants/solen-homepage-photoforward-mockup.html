What makes it feel like the references

  • Image hero. A real salon photo with the search floating over it — instead of opening on an empty white screen. This is the single biggest "premium marketplace" signal (Fresha/Airbnb/Uber all do it).
  • Photo category chips, no 3D emoji. The glossy emoji icons were the #1 "beta" tell. Real photo chips (Coiffeur/Barber/Nails…) read like Fresha/Uber.
  • Bigger photo-led cards. ~78% width, 200px photo — the image leads, text supports. Your card meta is unchanged (name+★ / street / time·price).
  • Pinterest masonry inspiration. A 2-column staggered image wall — dense, scrollable, visual. The most "Pinterest" moment on the page.
  • Kept: B&W chrome, Inter Tight / Hanken, the Walk-in band, the city pill. Nothing colorful added — the photos carry it.

Note: photos here are Unsplash placeholders; real salon/treatment imagery would be even stronger. This is a direction to react to — tell me what lands and what doesn't before I build any of it.
