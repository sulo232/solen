Making bold text do less work on Your profile and settings

Two real screens, each shown twice. In the second copy of each, every heavy-looking text on the screen (section labels, the page title, your name) gets a touch lighter. Nothing moves, nothing resizes, nothing changes colour. Scroll inside each box to compare.

One thing in the brief turned out to be wrong, so this reports the real number instead. I was told both screens measure an 18px title and a 40% weight share. Measuring the live page myself, the profile hub's title is actually your own name at 28px, already big enough, and the weight share on both screens is closer to 32%, not 40%. The settings page really is missing a big title (20px), that part of the brief was right for settings, just not for the hub. Numbers below are what I measured just now, not what I was handed.

Right now, about a third of the text on your profile page is bold: the top bar title, your name, and every section label (bookings, wallet, personal details, and the rest). This test makes that text a touch lighter, medium instead of semibold or bold, everywhere on the screen. The sizes stay exactly the same, so you can still tell your name from a section label, that job just moves from boldness to size, which is already how the page is laid out. Your name already renders big enough (28px) to be the one clear headline moment on this screen, this fix does not touch that.

Same change on the settings page: the section labels and row titles go from bold to medium. It closes the same gap, from just over 31.8% bold text down to zero.
What this does not fix, on settings specifically: this page's own title is only 20px, smaller than we want for a screen's one big moment (28px), and nothing else on the page is much bigger than the body text (1.33x, we want at least 1.8x). Lightening the bold text cannot create a big headline that isn't there, that needs an actual size change to the title, which is a different fix than this one. This screen also has close to no photo on it (about 1%), same as the profile hub, this fix does not touch that either.
All four panels are the same two live routes, not a redraw. The lightened weight is applied here as a test overlay on Tailwind's shared "semibold" and "bold" utilities, used across the whole product, so shipping it for real would need it scoped to just these two screens.
