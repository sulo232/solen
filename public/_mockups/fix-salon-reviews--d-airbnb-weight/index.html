The Airbnb weight fix

Right now, almost half the text on this page is bold. Airbnb barely uses bold at all: about 3 words in every 100. It leans on bigger text and colour instead.

Below: the same page, with every bold element turned down to medium weight. Nothing moves, nothing resizes, nothing is added. Just less bold.
