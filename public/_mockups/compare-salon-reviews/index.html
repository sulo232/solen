What is his call, and what is just matching the salon page he already likes

  • His call: which of these five to ship, or whether to combine pieces of more than one (for example, D's weight recipe plus C's photo placement).
  • His call: which photo to show. All three photo options here read the salon's own cover photo live, the only real photo this route can honestly reach today, since no individual review has a photo of its own yet. Adding real photos to individual reviews later is a separate, bigger decision about shared data, not shown here.
  • His call: variant E also shrinks the avatar circle from 56px to 44px, as part of matching the registered size exactly rather than keeping the old size with just a new colour.
  • His call: variant D turns the filter chips' own bold weight down to medium too, which touches a separately locked rule (selected filter pills are meant to stay semibold/600). Named here as a real collision between two decisions, not resolved silently.
  • Not his call, already decided: the target numbers themselves (photo at least a third of the screen, bold text under 30%, at most 4 sizes crowded together, the 30px heading). Those came from the salon page he already approved and apply the same way to every option above.
  • Not his call, already decided: variant E's card, grey-avatar and no-label recipe are not new designs, they are copies of what the salon page already ships for this same review content today.
