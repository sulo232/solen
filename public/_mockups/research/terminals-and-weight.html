Icon terminals and weight ceiling

See the two findings, not just read them

Both findings were measured live this session against airbnb.com and this tunnel's own /de/profile, and re-confirmed a second time this turn with a direct Playwright check. Every icon below is real Lucide path data (node_modules/lucide-react, v0.577.0); panel 3 is a live capture of the real route, not a redraw.

1. Icon terminals: round versus butt/miter

Airbnb: every stroked icon measured returns stroke-linecap butt and stroke-linejoin miter, 18+ instances across 3 live pages, 0 exceptions. Ours: round/round on every stroked icon measured, 117+ instances on /de alone and 12 of 12 re-checked again this turn on /de/profile, because that is Lucide's shipped default and nothing in the codebase overrides it.

2. Weight ceiling: 700 versus 600

Airbnb never exceeds weight 600 anywhere measured, live on its homepage and Help Center, and in the cited account-screen stills. We ship weight 700 (font-bold) on the profile display name.

Ours measured five distinct values across four routes, including two same-role card titles on the home feed tracked differently for no visible reason (-0.14px on one, normal on the other, same size and weight).
