4The Inspo finding

The menu has no visible way to open on this one screen

Found while measuring live, not a fix, not chased further this pass.

WHAT RENDERS THERE

The save-heart icon, same slot Home and Category use for the hamburger.

WHAT'S MEASURED THERE

The hamburger element itself: 0 x 0px, in the page's own markup.

Result: unlike Home and Category, Inspo has no visible way to open the sitewide menu from its own top row.

5Still open, no call made yet

1. Plain white rows, or a grey block behind the group?Adding a block undoes what removing the card just did; staying plain can read unfinished on a small screen.
2. Match Airbnb's divider grey, or keep Solen's own?Their measured #EBEBEB vs our locked #E4E4E7. Switching breaks the one-token-every-divider rule; keeping it means this line is never a pixel match.
3. Soften body ink to Airbnb's tone, or keep Solen's own?Their #222222 vs our #0A0A0A. Softening is a sitewide call, that ink is nearly every word plus the one commit button; keeping it, no accessibility rule pushes either way.
4. Side margin: 20, 23, or 26px?Three numbers exist on paper and the shipped page currently matches none of them; picking any one is low-risk, but nobody has picked.
5. Standardize letter-spacing to normal, or leave it?Standardizing is minor, undecided work with no broken rule behind it; leaving it, the inconsistency stays invisible to every written check.

Airbnb's account screen spends almost no colour or weight: one ink for text, one grey line reserved for group boundaries, their brand colour only on the active tab. Three things stay unmatched on purpose: the bottom tab bar (Solen has none, already turned down on a prior date), their own Cereal typeface (Inter stays), and rows with nothing extra written on them (Solen's rows carry real numbers, like the next appointment date or how many stamps remain, and deleting those to look barer would be hiding real information to chase a look).
