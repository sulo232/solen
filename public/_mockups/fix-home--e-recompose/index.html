The one honest problem: the headline and the photo are fighting for the same space

Your phone screen is a fixed 844px tall. Every pixel a headline uses is a pixel a photo cannot use, and I could not find a way around that on this screen without touching something that was already deliberately tuned.

What the headline costs: even trimmed as tight as I could get it (top gap, the H1's two lines, a small bottom gap, no subline), it is roughly 100px of screen height that did not exist there before.

What the pill-row squeeze buys back: 16px. That is the only spacing on this screen I could tighten without reopening a call your own comments say was already measured and settled this month: the search bar's own height is copied 1:1 from the category-page search (owner-approved August 2nd), and the gap between the pill row and the first heading was already tuned against a competitor's own number on August 10th ("everything is unbalanced").

Net effect, estimated, not re-measured live (my browser session hit its tab limit while building this, shared with the three other mockups running alongside it, the same limitation the floor-fix-home mockup already ran into): about 100px minus 16px equals roughly 84px pushed down, against a target that only needed about 37px of extra photo to begin with. So this combination is likely to leave imagery further from 33% than it started, not closer. I am not claiming a precise after-number here because I do not have one I trust.

Two ways to actually close this, both his call, neither applied here:

  1. Skip the headline, keep the photo. The imagery floor itself says a 28px headline is not required "unless the photograph is the focal point." Imagery only needs the small pill-row squeeze to get close to 33%, per public/_mockups/floor-fix-home. If he is fine with the photo carrying the screen instead of a headline, the headline requirement stops being a problem to solve at all, and this whole headline change goes away.
  2. Keep the headline, reopen one more locked number. The next 84px of room only exists inside the search pill's own height or the section rhythm below it, both dated, named, owner-approved changes from this past two weeks. Reopening either is his call, not mine to guess at.
