What's your call, and what's just matching a page you already like

  • Your call: whether this screen gets a headline at all. It's never had one; a photo-only screen with no title is allowed by your own rule when the photos are doing the job, and this screen's photos already clear that bar today.
  • Your call: which of the five mechanisms you want, since they trade off differently. Options 1 and 5 add a new title. Option 2 grows what's already there. Option 3 changes spacing and card size only. Option 4 changes weight only.
  • Your call: option 3's card-width change goes one notch past a size you already picked by name earlier ("V2, not V3"). It's a step further in the same direction, not a reversal, but it is past an already-decided number.
  • Not your call, already decided: the exact size, spacing and weight used in options 2 and 5 (30px, medium weight, the salon-page title recipe). Those numbers come straight off the one screen you already approved (the Cuts & Culture salon page), so they're matching, not proposing something new.
  • Not your call, a correction: the "how broken is this today" numbers you may have seen earlier (near-zero photos, a 14px heading) don't hold up on the live page. The real numbers are in the box above box 0: two things are actually broken, not three, and the photo count already passes.
