hA , "Stacked, Airbnb proper"

Airbnb does not sandwich its search between the logo and the menu, it stacks: a logo row, then a search row of its own beneath it. This is that, at 390.

1. The navigation answer

Two rows of chrome, and the hamburger is the only navigation. Row one (y 0 to 64) carries the wordmark left and the 44x44 hamburger right, which he said he likes. Row two (y 64 to 120) carries nothing but search, running the whole 358px between the 16px gutters. Nothing is docked to the bottom edge of this screen. Every destination that is not a salon lives behind the hamburger; every destination that is a salon is a card. There is no third kind of place to go, which is why one menu button is enough.

The search control is the real one re-geometried, not redrawn: the same three CollapsedRow segments, the real lucide search / map-pin / calendar glyphs, the real labels Service / Stadt / Zeit, the same white + elevation-2 + no-border container (edge-visibility route c), and the same ink commit control shrunk to a 44px round button. 250px of stacked card became one 56px row.

2. The measured number that drove it

His complaint is arithmetic and it is correct. On /de the logo ends at x=71 and the hamburger starts at x=330, so a sandwiched pill gets 243px. Stacked on its own row it measures 358px. That is +47.3%, bought for one 56px row.

The second number set the whole vertical budget. Today the first salon card sits at y=842, one pixel inside an 844 fold, and the phone first viewport measures 0.00% photographic. Deleting the h1 and its subline and collapsing the 250px search stack to 56 buys 708px back. Measured on this file at 390 wide:

  • first salon card top = y=134 (was 842)
  • card rows at 134 / 356 / 578 / 800
  • 6 full cards visible, plus 44px of row 4 cropped above the fold: the scroll promise
  • photographic area in the first 844px = 47.27% (floor is ~33%)
  • 3 distinct font sizes (24 / 14 / 12), ceiling is 4
  • 25% of first-viewport text at weight >= 600, ceiling is ~30%
  • smallest touch target 44px; 8 photo slots, 8 distinct scenes, 0 broken images

Two columns is not a style preference, it is the only shape that clears both floors at once. The locked 1.25 photo ratio makes a one-column card 358x286, so one column puts 1.5 cards in view and fails the >=4-unit density floor. A photo-left list row hits 4 units but drops photographic area to roughly 14% and fails the imagery floor. At 171 wide the photo is 137 tall and both floors clear.

3. What this direction gives up

  • 56px of every screen, forever. The row is the cost of the row, and nothing else on the page gets that space back.
  • The middle of the logo row is empty, a measured 269px of it. Sandwiching at least put something there. A resolved-city chip would fill it, but Stadt is already a search segment and stating the city twice is the duplication taste rule 2 exists to stop.
  • No display anchor >= 28px. With the promise headline deleted the largest text is the 24px wordmark, 1.71x body, under the 1.8x ratio floor. Legal only because FLOORS LAW 6 exempts a screen whose photograph is the focal, and at 47.27% it plainly is. Naming it rather than growing the wordmark to game a number.
  • The category taxonomy is now below the first viewport. A first-time visitor learns what Solen covers from the salons, not from a grid. Same trade Airbnb makes, opposite of the one Fresha iOS makes.
  • A magnifier renders twice in the search row, once as the Service field glyph and once in the ink button. Airbnb web and the real SearchBar both do this, but it is a redundancy and whether it reads as one is his call.
  • Smaller photos than the real rail card, 171 wide against 231. Density was bought with photo size.

4. Two things the measurement caught, and one supply limit

Both defects passed every number and were only visible in the render. First, a UA body margin put the header at y=8 and shifted every row down with it. Second, and worse, the reset .hA-root button outranked .hA-go on specificity, so the one ink commit control computed rgba(0,0,0,0) and was invisible while every measurement still reported it 44x44 in the right place. Both fixed and re-verified.

The supply limit, which is not a design choice. The 12 files in _assets/salon-photos are not 12 photographs. p04 and p05 are byte-identical, and p01/p10, p02/p08 and p09/p11 are the same scene at different crops. There are 8 distinct scenes in the folder, which is exactly the number of cards here, so this mockup reaches zero visual reuse only by consuming the entire pool. Two of the eight (p06, p07) are product flat-lays that show no salon at all, so they sit in the cropped fourth row rather than the first viewport. A ninth card could not be added without repeating a scene, which is the same defect the live home page already has at nineteen slots to eleven photographs.

One rule collision, surfaced not settled. The resurrection gate forbids a review count on this card (C11, owner-approved 2026-07-13); PSYCHOLOGY law 6 requires one. SalonCard.tsx:492 ships the bare star today, so this mockup matches the live component, but one of those two gates is enforcing a superseded rule and it needs an owner sentence to settle.
