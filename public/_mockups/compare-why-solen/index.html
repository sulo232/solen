JUST MATCHING THE SALON PAGE (CUTS & CULTURE) YOU ALREADY APPROVED
  • The headline size and weight in B and E, 30px at weight 500, is the exact number already live on the salon page you like. Not a new opinion, just applying a number you've already signed off on.
  • Trimming the crowded text sizes in A, B, and E is folding sizes that were never real decisions (an unset 16px default, an unlocked 20px subtitle) into sizes your product already locks elsewhere on this same screen.

YOURS TO DECIDE
  • Whether this page shows real salons at all. Options C and E both need real content pulled onto a page whose whole job today is explaining the product, not browsing it. That's a decision about what the page is FOR, not a style tweak.
  • Which salons show, if you say yes to C or E. Neither option has a real answer yet, both borrow from the nearest populated route (Coiffeur Basel, or the homepage's own rail) rather than a real "what should show on Why Solen" query.
  • The gray background. This page is the grayest screen measured anywhere in the product, about three quarters of it is sunken gray across two stacked full-width sections. None of the five options above touch it. It's a bigger call than anything shown here and it's on the record, not decided.
  • Whether the photo target should even apply here. Every other option except C and E leaves the photo floor open on purpose because a type fix cannot add a photo. If this page's job stays "explain the product," missing the 33% imagery floor may simply be the right call for this specific screen.
