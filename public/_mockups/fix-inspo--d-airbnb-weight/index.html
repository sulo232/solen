Inspo, less bold, the Airbnb way

Two words on this screen are bold today. In the second panel they are not. Nothing else changes.

This is the honest result, not a hopeful one.

The word count Airbnb bolds is small enough to matter as a source, so I checked whether Inspo has the problem it fixes. It does not. Inspo already bolds less text than the 30 percent limit allows, well under it. This change makes an already-fine number a little finer. It does not touch the two things actually wrong with this screen: no text on it reaches the required size, and the biggest word is barely bigger than the smallest.

What this does not fix

The biggest word on this screen is the 14px search placeholder. Airbnb's rule is about how MUCH text is bold, not how big anything is, so lightening two words cannot make a third word bigger. Reaching the required 28px anchor needs a different kind of change, sizing something up, not weighing something down.

A number I was handed for this screen's photo coverage was wrong. I measured it myself, twice, a few seconds apart: right when the page finished loading it read 0.71 percent, matching what I was told, but the grid photos load lazily and are still mid-decode at that instant. A few seconds later, same screen, same scroll position, it read 63.62 percent, well past the 33 percent minimum. The photos are there. My first check simply ran while they were still arriving.
