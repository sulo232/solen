Why this is not a new design. On 10 August you looked at two different back arrows on one site and froze the answer, in your words: a circle, with a shadow, and "not, like, an arrow. Like, I want, like, a good triangle." A triangle is the chevron on the right. It was written down and then it was never built.

Where it stands now, counted by reading every one of them. Your site has 20 places where a customer goes back a screen. A week ago they came in 17 different looks, every one drew the straight arrow, and 11 of the 20 were too small to press comfortably, the smallest at 28 across where the floor is 44. Today all 20 draw the triangle, all 20 are 44 across, and 17 of the 20 are the same one thing. The three above are what is left.

The one on the screen you like. It was 40 across while the share, save and report buttons beside it on the same row were all 44. It is 44 now, so that row is even.

The one word I need from you, and it settles all four. Your lock says white. But on the salon page it is see-through today so the photo shows through, and the same is true of the board. Say "over a photo it stays see-through" and I move all four to that rule: white circle on a page, see-through circle on a photo, nothing else anywhere. Say "white everywhere" and all four go white. Either way it is one change and the last three stop looking different.
