Why this is not a new design. On 10 August you looked at two different back arrows on one site and froze the answer, in your words: a circle, with a shadow, and "not, like, an arrow. Like, I want, like, a good triangle." A triangle is the chevron on the right. It was written down and then it was never built.

How far it drifted, counted by reading every one of them. Your site has 20 places where a customer goes back a screen. They come in 17 different looks. All 20 draw the straight arrow, none draws the triangle you asked for, and 11 of the 20 are too small to press comfortably, the smallest at 28 across where the floor is 44.

The one on the screen you like. Measured today: it is 40 across, while the share, save and report buttons beside it on the same row are all 44. One control in that row of four is smaller than the other three, and it is the one people press most.

The one thing I am not deciding for you. On the salon page it is see-through so the photo shows through. Your lock says white. I kept it see-through on both sides here, because the salon page is the one you told me looks right and a solid white circle sits heavy on a photo. If you want it white there too, say so and it is a one line change.
