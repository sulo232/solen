One heading becomes the anchor

Right now every section title on the home page, "Top auf Solen", "In der Nähe", "Top Coiffeur", all of them, prints at the exact same size and weight. Nothing tells your eye what to look at first. Below, the very first one gets bigger and everything else steps back a little. Nothing moves. Nothing new is added.

Now

Ten section titles, all the same size, all the same weight. No anchor.

biggest text: 18px

With one clear heading

The first title you land on is now the standout. The other nine step back.

biggest text: 30px
What actually changed. One thing: the size and weight of the section-title headings. The very first one you scroll to is now 30px and a little lighter (weight 500 instead of 600), the same recipe the hero headline already uses higher up on desktop, just not shown on phone today. The other nine section titles drop from 18px/600 to 16px/500, so they read as clearly secondary instead of repeating the same size ten times down the page.
What this fixes. The phone screen had no text above 18px, so it had no display anchor and its biggest heading was only 1.5x the body text, below the 1.8x floor. A 30px heading over 14px body is 2.14x, the same ratio the one screen you already like, the Cuts & Culture salon page, clears today.
What this does not fix. Two other numbers were measured against this screen and are not this panel's job: the photo share of the first screen (28.56% against a 33% floor) and how much of the visible text is bold (37.14% against a 30% ceiling). Those need more real salon content higher up and a pass over every other bold element on the page, not a heading resize. Two of the other variants built alongside this one target exactly those.
Where this idea already shipped once. The profile page lost its own big page title in July for being redundant ("people already know that theyre in the profile"), and when it did, the anchor requirement did not go away with it, it moved to that screen's first section heading instead. This panel does the same move here, on a screen that still has its heading, just never let it stand out.
