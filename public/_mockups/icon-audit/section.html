25 line icons and 10 picture icons, taken from the running site across 8 pages. Nothing here is drawn by me: each shape is the site's own output, copied as it is.

What a swap has to fix, measured

Six different line thicknesses in one set. The icons are drawn at 1.8, 2, 2.2, 2.25, 2.4 and 2.5. That is why the set reads slightly uneven even though every shape comes from the same family. A new set only fixes this if we pick one thickness and hold it.

The same icon is drawn at different sizes on one screen. On the home page the star appears at 12px 49 times and at 11px 17 times. The right arrow runs 11px and 18px. The pin runs 17px and 13px. The magnifier runs 12px and 24px.

Two generations of category pictures are live at once. Four categories have a newer set, and the older flat one is still being served beside it. Walk-in and map never got a new version, so they cannot match whatever the others do.

Nothing is hand-drawn. Every line icon on these pages is real library artwork, so a swap is a straight replacement with no odd one-off shapes to redraw.
