What a swap has to fix, measured

Six different line thicknesses in one set. 1.8, 2, 2.2, 2.25, 2.4 and 2.5. That is why the set reads uneven even though every shape is from the same family.

The same icon is drawn at different sizes on one screen. On home the star is 12px forty-nine times and 11px seventeen times. The right arrow runs 11 and 18. The pin runs 17 and 13. The magnifier runs 12 and 24.

22 of the 28 carry motion. A new set has to keep it, or the site gets quieter without anyone deciding that. Two timing curves are in use, and each icon says which one it has.

Nothing is hand-drawn. Every one is real library artwork, so a swap is a straight replacement with no one-off shapes to redraw.
