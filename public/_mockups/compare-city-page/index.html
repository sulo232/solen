His call versus just matching the salon page

  • Just matching a number he already approved, not a new decision: the title growing to 28 to 30px. That's his own rule (one big title per screen, at least 28px) already used on the salon page he likes. Panels A, B and E all do this.
  • Just matching a number he already approved, not a new decision: the bold buttons going back to plain text (panels A and D). His own rule for buttons and pills already says only the one you're on should be bold; these four were simply never built to it.
  • His call, this is a real look change: Panel B making every salon name bigger. That's not fixing a broken rule, it's a taste choice about how loud the salon names should be.
  • His call, this is a real look change: Panel C's tighter spacing. Nothing is broken here by his own rules; this is trading white space for showing salons sooner, purely a preference.
  • His call, this is a real look change: Panel E's button rebuild. The gray fill itself is not new (he already banned black-fill buttons elsewhere), but swapping this page's actual buttons for it is still a visible change he should see before it ships.
