What actually changes. Three spacing rules on the top two blocks of the page. The space above and below the "Salons in Basel" heading shrinks, the small gap between the location line and the heading shrinks, and the empty strip under the category buttons shrinks. 70px of that, all air, comes back, and the real salon photos move up into it. Nothing is deleted, resized, or reworded.
About the 0% figure I was handed for this screen. I could not reproduce it as the steady state. I measured this three separate ways, the project's own check script cold, the same script run again a moment later, and my own read of the live page in a browser, and every read after the very first one landed between 43% and 47% photographic, already past the 33% floor. The salon list on this page loads a moment after the page appears, and while it is loading you see grey placeholder cards with no photos in them at all, that is where a 0% reading comes from. My best explanation, not confirmed, is that this only shows up on the very first hit to this route on a dev server that has not compiled it yet. Because of that, this fix does not carry the imagery floor from failing to passing, there was nothing failing once the page actually settles. What it does do, measured directly: it makes that settled photo share noticeably bigger, from 47% to 55% in the same frame, which is what "push it up and make it bigger" should produce even when the floor was not the real problem.
What this does not touch. The salon cards, the photos, the search, the category icons, the colors, all the copy, and the two other broken numbers on this screen, the heading size and the bold category buttons. Those need a different fix than spacing; this file only proves the one mechanism it was asked to prove.
