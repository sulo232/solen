Account rows

Why these two changes tap to read

Icon size, 19px to 22px. Not a guess. The reference account list measures 17.3 to 22.7pt per glyph (gear 22.3, shield 22.7, bell 22.7) in _design-system/references/airbnb--profile-list.md. 22 is the top of that band, and it is already the size of the box this component draws the glyph inside.

Which explanations go. The test is whether the second line tells you something the label does not. Five carry real data and stay:

  • Bookings, your next appointment with the date
  • Wallet, whether a card is saved
  • Vouchers, how many are active
  • Saved, how many stores
  • Stamps, how many left until the reward

Two are just the label said twice, and those go:

  • Hair profile, a list of what a hair profile contains
  • Settings, a list of what settings contains

The cost, so you can weigh it. Those two rows get shorter than the other five, so the list stops being a perfect grid. I think that is the right trade, because an even grid built out of filler is worse than an uneven one built out of facts. If you would rather keep every row the same height, say so and I will leave both lines in and only resize the glyphs.
