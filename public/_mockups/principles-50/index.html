Shape & geometry

1Concentric corners. Outer radius = inner radius + padding, never equal.
2The 4pt grid. Every gap and size is a multiple of 4.
3Touch floor 44. Everything tappable is 44px+, pad invisible hit areas.
4Optical beats mathematical. Align to what the eye sees, not the bounding box.
5One radius family per container. Card 16, input 12, sheet 28, pill round.
6One icon stroke table. Calibrated by size, never blind-scaled.
7Border or shadow. Never both on one box.
8Every edge visible. A container must read against its actual background.

Type

9One anchor per screen. Exactly one biggest thing , two biggests = none.
10Jumps, not nudges. Levels differ 30-50%, never 1-2px.
11The 4-size budget. Four body sizes + one display anchor, counted before shipping.
12Two weights. 400 and 600 , bold is a role, not a mood.
13Copy by word width. Glyph height lies (underlines, tails); word width doesn't.
14Emphasis binds whole units. Bold the whole price, never half a phrase.
15A display anchor of 28+ on every screen unless the photo is the hero.
16Lines cap at ~68 characters. German compounds need room.

Color & surface

1780/17. Neutral surfaces carry the app, ink speaks, accents are crumbs.
18Photography carries the color. The chrome stays quiet.
19Blue is tiny. Small clickable bits only , never structure, never big CTAs.
20Semantic colors are sovereign. Star gold, success green, error red never go monochrome.
21Selected has three dialects. Gray fill for options, ink underline for content tabs, blue only for booking slots.
22The gray tray. Photo-less groups sit on sunken gray; alternate gray/white down the page.
23The palette is closed. Not a token = doesn't exist.
24Warmth is mandatory. Zero photos and zero semantic color in a viewport = dead, not minimal.

Layout & grouping

25Chunking. Break content into small meaningful groups , grouped checkout, formatted numbers, cuisine rows.NEW
26Proximity is truth. A caption sits closer to its own image than to anything else.
27Contrast is the separator. Never add a dot between things that already differ.
28Name the grid first. Reading page, repeated peers, equal tiles, or one hero , wrong grid, wrong page.
29Design the full state. The populated screen is the target; loading/empty/error derive from it.
30Density floors. Four-plus items per browse viewport, the next one peeking, full card info stacks.
31Whitespace is designed. Trapped dead space is a bug, not air.
32The composed page is the unit. Design with the app shell and real data , isolated mockups lie.NEW

Content & copy

33Recognition over recall. Show recents, suggestions and saved things instead of demanding memory.NEW
34Promise, then gesture. Empty states tease the future, teach the filling action, carry one real CTA.
35Delete-test every word. If meaning survives, the word was padding.
36Real data or nothing. Never render an unwired number , omit and flag.
37No times on cards. Dates only; times live in the booking picker.
38One primary verb per screen. Exactly one ink commit button.
39Icons carry labels unless in the closed 7-icon universal set.
40Stars never travel alone. Rating = star + value, nothing bare.

Speed & feedback

41The 400ms rule. Respond instantly or show honest progress , skeletons matching the final layout, never spinners.NEW
42Every tap answers. Press states, outcome toasts, inline errors that keep your data.
43Waits feel active. Optimistic where safe , the heart fills instantly, reconcile after.NEW
44Motion is physics. One vocabulary; nothing bounces hello.

Psychology

45Goal gradient. Show progress toward the next step , started beats empty.
46Peak-end. The confirmation moment is the memory of the whole flow , invest there.
47Defaults are recommendations. Preselect the sensible option; never start at zero.
48Honest persuasion only. Bounded loss framing, no fake urgency, no invented social proof.

Process
