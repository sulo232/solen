Theme 5: where your refs collide with your own locked rules

These I will not build silently. Each names the locked rule and the refs pulling against it. Default answer is "keep": the lock stays law until you reopen it by name.

C1Color per category vs your neutral-tags lock

Refs 04, 22 and 23 color-code by category or time of day (pastel badges, colored calendar events). Your locked rule says category tags are neutral, no per-category color. Liking these refs might mean you want per-category color back, or it might just be what those designers do. keep or reopen?

C2Colored primary buttons vs your ink-commit lock

Ref 14 commits with a blue pill, ref 23 with a green circle. Your locked rule: the one primary commit button stays ink, blue is only for small clickable bits. keep or reopen?

C3Selected states vs your gray-fill lock

Ref 08 marks selection with a white pill and a dark ring, ref 13 with a dark fill, ref 09 with a grey capsule. Your locked selected state is the calm gray sunken fill with semibold ink text, and it explicitly rules out dark fills. Three refs pulling three different ways suggests this axis keeps attracting you; if the gray fill ever feels wrong, say so and we probe it properly instead of drifting. keep or probe it?

C4Full-bleed color heroes vs your 80/17 restraint

Ref 05's second iteration fills the top of the screen with saturated blue and renders the score directly on it. That is the opposite of your 80/17 neutral-surfaces budget. If you like it, the honest scope would be a one-off celebratory surface (a confirmation, an onboarding end), never daily screens. keep restraint or scope one exception?

C5The big question: staging taste or product taste?

Seven of your refs (01, 03, 05, 07, 21, 27, 30) share one trait that has nothing to do with their UI ideas: colorful ambient backdrops behind white UI (wallpapers, gradients, blurred photos). Two readings: staging = you like how designers PRESENT work, and we should adopt it for our own mockup and case-study pages; product = you want more color in Solen itself, which would be a big, deliberate break from the black-and-white pivot. Which is it? Answer "C5 staging", "C5 product" or "C5 both".
