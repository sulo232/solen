The results page, with the bold turned down

This is the real Basel results page, loaded live, twice. Nothing is redrawn or faked. The only difference between the two copies below is one rule: text that was already bold now sits at a lighter weight.

Right now, 31% of the text you see on this screen is bold: both rail titles, every salon name, every price. That is over this app's own 30% ceiling, and it is a big reason the page reads loud instead of readable. Airbnb, which you named as the source of truth, runs its own results-style screens at 3.1% bold. It gets its hierarchy from size, not from making almost everything heavy.

What this alone will not fix. The rail titles are still small (18px, the contract wants 28px) and there is still no image large enough to anchor the page. Turning down the bold does not make either of those bigger. Those are separate fixes; this is the weight fix only, on purpose.

With the fix

Same names, same prices, same photos, same layout. Only the weight changed. The rail titles are still the biggest text near them, so you can still tell what is a heading, it just is not shouting to do it.

1 of 29 text lines bold
The one line that changed. Every card name and both rail titles were already using the app's semibold weight. This turns that one weight, everywhere it appears inside the results area, from 600 down to 500. Same font, same sizes, same colours, same spacing.
Measured, not guessed. Before: 9 of 29 visible text lines were bold, 31.03%. After the same one rule: 1 of 29, 3.45%, almost exactly Airbnb's own 3.1%. The one line still bold is the small blue saved-count badge in the top bar, which belongs to a different, shared part of the app and was left alone on purpose.
Numbers I was handed that turned out wrong, checked live before building this.
  • Bold share was given as 0% (passing). It actually measures 31.03% (failing). That is the number this fix is aimed at.
  • Photo coverage was given as 0%. It actually measures 41% of the screen (already passing the photo floor).
  • The biggest heading was given as 14px. It actually measures 18px (still short of the 28px target, just not by the margin given).
