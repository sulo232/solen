What the lines are doing

Section dividers — full-bleed 1px hairline at rgba(26,26,26,0.10). Sits between every paper-bg section.
Section numbers — "01 · Für dich" etc. The number + label + auto-extending hairline is a Swiss editorial signature.
Listing inset dividers — salons in "Heute noch buchbar" are separated by inset 1px hairlines, not card backgrounds. Reads like a printed directory.
Trust grid dividers — 4 stats separated by vertical + horizontal hairlines on the navy bg (white at 14% opacity). Cross structure feels like a data table.
Tag + Heart borders — 1px hairlines define interactive shapes. No fills, no shadows. Pure outline language.
Why this works for Solen: Swiss-editorial design (NZZ, Brand New, A24's site) uses hairlines as the structural language. Pairs cleanly with the Bricolage display + Hanken body type system. Removes 80% of the bg-color decision-making — page rhythm comes from the LINES and the TYPOGRAPHY hierarchy, not from coordinating warm/cool sections. The brand color is the only saturated thing on the page, which makes it actually feel like "the brand" instead of just "a color."
