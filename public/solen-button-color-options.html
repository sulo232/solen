My pick: 2 · Warm ink #1F1612

A CTA button's job is "click me." For that, max contrast neutral beats any chromatic color. Warm ink (#1F1612 — black with the faintest brown tint) anchors orange without competing with it. Page stays at exactly 2 chromatic anchors: orange (brand) + navy (B2B trust). Black does the rest. This is what Fresha + every premium booking site converged on after trying everything else.

Order of personality if you reject ink: Deep teal (most distinctive, but adds 3rd hue) → Forest (autumn editorial, but greens compete) → Chocolate (cohesive but weak CTA affordance).
