Walk-in — how to structure it

The question: integrate walk-in into the barbershop page, make it a separate flow, or both? Below: the 3 options compared, then the recommended flow with every screen linked so you can click through and verify each UI.

The 3 options

Option A

Integrated

Walk-in lives on the barbershop page only.
Home→Salon page→Join here→Pay→Ticket (on salon page)
+ Join is right where you choose the shop.
− After joining, your ticket is stranded on the shop page — bad "track my spot" loop.
− Weak "any walk-in near me now" discovery.
Good for the entry, wrong home for the live ticket.
Option B

Separate

A standalone walk-in hub + flow.
Home→/walk-in hub→Pick shop→Pay→Ticket (in hub)
+ Clear home; easy to track your ticket.
+ Strong "walk-in now" discovery.
− The shop's own page never shows its live queue — missed discovery at the moment of choice.
Great home for tracking, but disconnected from where people pick a shop.
Recommended
Option C

Both, split by job

Salon page = entry. Standalone = track.
Home band/Salon page→Join→Pay→Ticket = home widget + ticket view
+ Join where it's contextual (the shop).
+ Track where it's persistent (home widget + ticket).
+ Discovery on both the shop page and the home band/search.
− Two surfaces to keep in sync — needs the clear role split (below).
Pick. Each surface does what it's best at: shop page joins, standalone tracks.
