What changed

  • Play button 36 → 48 · icon 16 → 20 — proportional to bigger card, reads as a confident "watch" affordance

Why this looks more "human-balanced"

In figure drawing, the head is the unit and everything else (limbs, torso, features) scales to it. Cards work the same: card width is the unit, every overlay should scale with it. Below, the same single card at three sizes — 200 (current desktop), 240 (proposed desktop), 165 (mobile-after). Notice how the proposed pair feels visually consistent because the play button + pills scale-with-card; the current desktop looks like a shrunken phone.
