Pushed harder. Closer to the poster reference. Texture clearly dominates. Risk: at this opacity, small text (subtitle, card meta, sbar-row placeholders) starts to feel slightly grimy on phone. If E2 feels "almost right but not quite enough texture" → pick this. If E2 feels right or strong enough → skip.

Settings (CSS recipe per variant)

Variant Grain opacity Blend mode Gradient blob opacity Perceived intensity
E1 — Subtle 0.65 soft-light 0.45 / 0.45 / 0.20 / 0.35 ~ 18% (hint of texture)
E2 — Flower-match 0.90 soft-light 0.65 / 0.55 / 0.20 / 0.45 ~ 25% (matches the flower)
E3 — Heavy 1.20 soft-light 0.65 / 0.55 / 0.20 / 0.45 ~ 32% (poster territory)

Why soft-light, not multiply: at 25%+ grain opacity, multiply darkens the substrate noticeably (cream → khaki). Soft-light blends grain WITHOUT shifting overall brightness — substrate stays warm cream, grain just adds the texture layer. This is the difference between "film grain on warm paper" (good) and "dirt on a window" (bad).
