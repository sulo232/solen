Why mixed dark + bright?

Republik does both. Some articles bright (hot pink, red, sunny). Some dark (forest green, navy teal). The MIX is the system. Solen's mapping:

  • Coiffeur · bright rose (`#B5588A`) · dark ink text
  • Barber · bright sunny (`#E8A957`) · dark ink text
  • Nails · bright clay (`#C77A5C`) · dark ink text
  • Spa · DARK sage-deep (`#3F6F55`) · cream text
  • Makeup · saturated coral (`#D66547`) · white text
  • Wellness · DARK camel-deep (`#5C3D22`) · cream text

If you want ALL bright (no dark) or ALL dark (no bright) — say so and I render that variant.
