What powers the pills (already built):
• Categories — fixed taxonomy (Coiffeur / Barber / Nails / Spa / Massage), same as the homepage rail.
• For-you / quick searches — /api/recommendations + /api/discovery/chip-terms + your recent searches & viewed salons. Falls back to /api/discovery/trending ("Trending in Basel") for logged-out / new users so the row is never empty.
• Behavior — category pill fills the Service field (keep composing); for-you chip runs the search (it's a full intent). My pick: B — least clutter, most useful, and it's the one that actually uses the algorithm you're asking about.
