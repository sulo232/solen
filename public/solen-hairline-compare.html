Hairlines: do we need to differentiate?

Short answer: yes, but the line stays one weight. The system differentiates with background, space and elevation, not by darkening the border. Here's the proof, on white.

1 · The border scale (all on white)

My sweep replaced border-s-ink/{opacity} with the single token border-s-border. The token sits at ~ink-10%, so faint hairlines got the SAME or SLIGHTLY MORE visible; only deliberately heavy borders got lighter.

Verdict. A hairline's job is to whisper "these are different rows," not to draw a box. At ink-6% the old lines were nearly invisible (≈ #F0F0F0); border-s-border (#E7E5E4) reads as an actual line. So the sweep mostly improved separation while making every edge identical. Where you genuinely need a surface to stand apart, the answer is background or elevation (section 2), not a darker line.
Honest flag. Two borders got lighter from the sweep (they were ink/25-30, heavier on purpose): the ServiceDetailSheet option-ring and a dev/new-primitives button. If you want a selected/active control ring to stay heavier than a hairline, that's a real second token (e.g. a 1.5px border-s-ink/20 "control border") — say the word and I'll add it instead of flattening those.
