My honest read (not just agreeing)

  • Works well: the black panel groups nav + category + search into one clear "control zone," and the rounded bottom + white body below reads premium and modern (Revolut / banking-app vibe). The seam problem from "center everything" is gone - the white body is a normal left-aligned page.
  • Solves Fuer-dich: with the black panel above, the icon row sits clearly in the white zone, so dropping its "Fuer dich" heading no longer fuses it into the filters - the black/white split is the separator.
  • Aesthetic shift: a big black slab is a strong departure from the current near-all-white Uber-clean look. It's a real brand decision, not just a layout tweak - it'll change how the whole app feels. Worth being deliberate.
  • Category in header: the bar gets full width here (better than the cramped between-logo idea), but it's now under the logo, so the logo row + category + search make the black panel ~150px tall - eats vertical space above the results.
  • No Fuer-dich label: first-time users may not know what the 4 icons are without a word. A tiny label or keeping "Fuer dich" is safer - flag if you want it back.
