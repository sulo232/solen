Card Aspect Ratio

This is the #1 visual difference between Solen and Airbnb/Fresha. Cards currently use 1:1 square. Which should we switch to?

1:1 Square (current)

Current state. Works on mobile. Shows less of the photo. Feels more like a product grid.

2:3 Portrait (Airbnb)

Premium editorial feel. Shows the full salon ambiance. Cards per row reduces from 4 to 3 on desktop.

4:5 Tall Square (Fresha / Instagram)

Middle ground — taller than square but not full portrait. Text info overlaid on bottom of photo. Feels bold and modern.

Note: The FeaturedSalonCarousel (hero row) already uses 4:5 with full overlay text. This question is about the category grid cards below it (SalonCard component).
