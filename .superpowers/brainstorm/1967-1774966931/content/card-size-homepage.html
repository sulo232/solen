Homepage Card Size

The category rows (Coiffeur, Nails, Barbershop…) use a horizontal scroll carousel. How big should each card be?

~240px wide cards

Current (240px)

Full overlay text on photo. Big and cinematic. Shows ~2.5 cards per screen on mobile.

~180px wide, text below photo

Compact (180px, Airbnb-style)

Square photo + name/location/price below. Shows ~3 full cards + peek. Clean, browsable, familiar.

~150px wide, shows 4+ at once

Tight Grid (150px)

More cards visible at once. Shows 4+ on mobile. Quick scanning, less immersive. Good if there are many salons.

Context: The hero Entdecken carousel (discovery content) at the top stays as-is with its cinematic tall cards. This question is only about the category rows — Coiffeur, Nails, Barbershop etc.
